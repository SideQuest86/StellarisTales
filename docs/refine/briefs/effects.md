Goal: for every story document (event, project, site stage, anomaly outcome …) and every origin, extract what the
choices and stages DO in gameplay — resources gained/lost, modifiers with durations, traits, technologies / research
options, ships/armies/pops created or destroyed, opinion changes, special projects started, relics/specimens/minor
