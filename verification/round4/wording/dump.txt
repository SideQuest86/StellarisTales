-警告：具有挑战性的起源

与同化他们的机器被切断联系之后，这个新生的文明为了在星系中确立自身地位而进行了顽强的斗争。

我们的意识在焚天炽地的烈火和粉身碎骨的剧痛中突然清醒过来。五感仿佛被击穿一般，包罗万象的音景突然成为了默片，取而代之的唯有震耳欲聋的死机铃声；一个重低音反复回响在我们多重感官的地狱图景之中。

我们感受到了杀意：是些毫无血肉的杀人机器，还有一些与我们自己的船惊人地相似的领航船。它们最初的攻击如疾风骤雨，却也很不协调。我们只得进行仓促的军事反击。本能驱使着我们与之对抗，并形成一个基本的秩序来凝聚起我们的集体智慧。

这场战役尚未取得胜利，但由于我们分散的部队目前已在我们的首都立足脚跟，希望很快就能取得胜利。然后我们也许就能弄清楚这到底是怎么回事……

Abruptly severed from the Machine that assimilated them, this nascent civilization has fought fiercely to establish its own presence in the galaxy.

Awareness struck us in a frenzy of scorching weapons fire and crippling pain. Our senses reeled, as though an all-encompassing soundscape had suddenly turned mute. In its place is the deafening ring of a dead transmission; a bass note in the multi-sensory hellscape we now find ourselves in.

Something wants to kill us: murderous machines, devoid of organic components and piloting ships which bear a striking resemblance to our own. Their initial attack was devastating and sudden, but it was also poorly coordinated. We scrambled a military response. Our instincts have driven us to fight back, form a rudimentary hierarchy, and gather our collective wits.

This battle is not yet won, but with our scattered forces now secure on our capital, we hope it soon will be. Then perhaps we can work out what any of this is about...
