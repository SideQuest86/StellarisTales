具现身份局势：由 2010.b、3100（90 天后）或十年检查（已有派系时）开启。初始进度 50；每 1800–1920 天按所在阶段走一步：去义体化（3105 → 3106 → 3107）、维持现状（3115 → 3116 → 3117）、义体化（3110 → 3111 → 3112）；先走完的一条路线以 3090 结束局势。去义体化步骤之后可能出现随机后果 5001–5004（各一次）。 The Embodied Identity situation: started by 2010.b, by 3100 (+90 days) or by the decade check once factions exist. It starts at 50; every 1800–1920 days it takes a step of the track matching its stage — decyberize (3105 → 3106 → 3107), status quo (3115 → 3116 → 3117), cyberize (3110 → 3111 → 3112); the first track completed ends it with 3090. Decyberization steps may bring one of the random follow-ups 5001–5004 (each once).

2445 中：选项 b（需合成进化，或局势结束后主要物种仍保有最高级的正面义体特质）与 c 都通向 2446 创造者的恳求 → 2450“机械万岁”，继承机械王座；选项 d 启动装置 → 2455“重生”，机械世界改造为盖亚星球。两者都结束事件链。 At 2445, option b (Synthetic Evolution, or the main species still has the top positive cybernetic trait after the situation) and option c lead to 2446, the Creators' plea → 2450 Long Live the Machine; option d uses the device → 2455 Rebirth, the machine world becomes a Gaia world. Both end the chain.
身份局势结局 3090：三条路线中最先完成的一条。 Identity situation ending 3090: whichever track completes first.
