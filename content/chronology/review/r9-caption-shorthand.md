# r9: continue-card captions in readable words (2026-10-26)

Scope: 179 reader-facing captions in 85 fragments that held script shorthand (ids such as `enclave_stage_2`,
`completed_inetian_traders_precursor`; event ids and numbers such as "federations.10400", "hidden 20"; flags; "fires",
"pulse", "on_action"): 126 `bridges[].label` (the caption under a continue card), 48 parallel `groups[].label` / `note` (the
investigations panel) and 5 `opening` labels. Selected by pattern over 2,334 such texts. 149 changed, 30 left as they were.

Method as in r8 (brief: conditions, chances and timing kept, plumbing dropped, game names from the localisation, captions
at most 16 words / 32 characters, group notes 30 / 60): six Haiku rewriters over disjoint batches by story, one Sonnet
verifier per batch (30 corrections: localised names such as 星系扩张, 无明的意愿, 勾陈一, Hull Hardening, Signal Trace; lost
conditions restored; lengths). Coordinator: 25 substituted names checked against the localisation (all match, e.g.
ring_world_2_intermediate = Ring World Frame / 环形世界框架); two Ring World captions that lost their stage restored; one
wording for the eleven first-contact stage captions ("The encounter's next stage (difficulty N)"); the last option letter
removed. Applied in place by JSON path (each file keeps its layout); the evidence fields are unchanged.

Follow-up (v4.17.2): the 33 captions over 20 words or 40 Chinese characters (9 stories; 1,890 captions in all, 95% at most 16
words) shortened to one line by two Haiku rewriters and two Sonnet verifiers (13 corrections: 'your system', 'transport ship',
approach names Organized Meditation / 组织冥想 and Distribute Zro / 分发泽珞); each rewriter listed what it left out.
