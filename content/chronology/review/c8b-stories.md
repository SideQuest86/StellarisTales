## Requests

### Compiler features (scripts/story_tree.py — not edited)

1. **Suppress outcomes of hidden entries.** `suppress` only filters edges of placed docs (`expand`). Outcomes of a hidden
   entry/root or hidden family member are placed by `build_story` without consulting `suppressed`, so false outcomes cannot
   be removed. Case: `event-fallen-empires-awakening-4` (Guardians of the Galaxy) shows `bio.3400` "Unity", which
   `fallen_empires_awakening.10` only sends for the Control fragment (`switch` on `has_country_flag`,
   events/fallen_empire_awakening_events.txt:437-476), while this path awakens the War fragment (L363-366).
   Proposal: apply `suppressed[hidden_rid]` (or a new `suppressEntry`) to `composite()` results used for entries.
2. **Skip `is_test_event = yes` events.** `fallen_empires_awakening.0` (is_test_event, L11-23) produced a false story
   `event-fallen-empires-awakening-0`; it is currently absorbed into the Guardians family as a hidden member to hide it.
3. **`switch` on flags is not evaluated.** `fallen_empires_awakening.10` reaches `bio.3400` in every awakening path
   (War in Heaven, Sleepers Awake, Guardians) although only the Control-fragment branch sends it.
