# 晶格 / The Lattice

- 分类 Category：星界裂隙 Astral rifts (C4) · DLC：星界位面 Astral Planes

## 如何开始 How it starts

裂隙类型从探索帝国的裂隙池中按权重抽取，本裂隙权重 1，没有额外条件（`00_astral_rifts.txt:42–51`）。每阶段结束掷 1–10 的骰子，加技能与线索、减难度，≥14 进入下一章；第一章 300 难度为 0。

The rift type is drawn by weight from the explorer's pool; this one has weight 1 and no extra conditions (`00_astral_rifts.txt:42–51`). Each phase ends with a d10 plus skill and clues minus difficulty; 14+ opens the next chapter. Chapter 300 has difficulty 0.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 怪异几何（300）：充满无限重复几何图样的空间。进一步探索（→ 305 多维度图样，难度 3）或原地研究（→ 310 多维度分析，难度 2）——两者都发现这个宇宙拥有三个以上的空间维度。
2. 瓶中信（315）：按四维地图找到“中央舱室”，那里有一份用本帝国语言写成的指引——署名正是这位科学家，来自未来的自己（格式塔帝国的文本省略署名一句）。
3. 时间旅行（320）：照着指引穿越超维空间，途中看到其他时间线上的自己，有些已迷失许久；科学家也为后来者留下指引。
4. 分形之种（325）：迷宫尽头漂浮着三个各自沿一个轴无限重复的球仪；这里没有来自未来的指示，可能意味着危险。

1. Strange Geometry (300): a space of endlessly repeating geometric patterns. Explore further (→ 305 Multi-Dimensional Patterns, difficulty 3) or study in place (→ 310 Multi-Dimensional Analysis, difficulty 2) — both reveal more than three spatial dimensions.
2. Message in a Bottle (315): a four-dimensional map leads to a “central chamber” holding instructions in the empire's own language — signed by the scientist, from the future (the gestalt text omits the signature line).
3. Time Traveling (320): following the instructions through super-spatial dimensions, the crew glimpses other versions of itself, some long lost, and leaves instructions behind in turn.
4. Fractal Seeds (325): at the maze's end float three globes, each repeating infinitely along one axis; there are no instructions here, which may mean danger.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 取回球仪（→ 330 维度容器，难度 6）：返回时另两个“自己”的探球与我们融合，三个球仪变成九个时间版本 → 9 颗分形之种。
- 到此为止（→ 335 亲密接触，难度 2）：另一个自己替我们带回了球仪 → 3 颗分形之种。
- 两者都结束探索并给予大量星界丝缕；分形之种通过殖民地决议放置在殖民地上。

- Retrieve the globes (→ 330 Dimensional Containers, difficulty 6): on the way back two other Spheres crewed by ourselves merge with ours, and the three globes become nine temporal copies → 9 Fractal Seeds.
- We are done here (→ 335 Close Encounters, difficulty 2): another self brought the globes back anyway → 3 Fractal Seeds.
- Both end the exploration with a large astral thread reward; Fractal Seeds are placed on colonies through colony decisions.
