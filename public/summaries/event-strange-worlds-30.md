## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满3年时随机事件池以20/739抽中 strange_worlds.30（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3839）。触发前提：BioGenesis DLC，人类玩家的非首都、非母星、仍由原建立者持有的人造以外殖民星球，没有地面战斗，也未被占领；并且该殖民地尚未触发过其他“奇异世界”事件（colony_event 旗标），每个国家每条故事只触发一次。

A colony turning 3 years old can draw strange_worlds.30 (20/739, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3839). Preconditions: BioGenesis DLC, a human player's non-capital, non-homeworld, original-owner, non-artificial colony without ground combat or occupation, which has not yet had another Strange Worlds event (colony_event flag); once per country per chain.
