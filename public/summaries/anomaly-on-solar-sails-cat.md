# 在恒星帆上 / On Solar Sails

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：上古遗物 Ancient Relics（ancrel 考古事件 archaeology events）

## 如何开始 How it starts

科学家领袖存在的非集体意识国家，在恒星帆残骸上可能生成（2级，全局一次 max_once_global；权重0.1，领袖物种有水栖特质时0.3；集体意识所有者权重为0）。

A non-gestalt country with a scientist leader can find it (level 2, once globally via max_once_global; weight 0.1, or 0.3 when the leader's species is aquatic; weight 0 for gestalt owners).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

anomaly.7020“在恒星帆上”清除矿藏，唯一选项创建考古遗址 site_space_shanty_dig“瓶中信”（5个阶段，需要科学家；30天后 story.5 报告；介绍文本有生物舰船变体）。阶段依次为 ancrel.15021“瓶中信”→ 15022“太空船歌”→ 15023“深空猛兽”→ 15024“或许是饮料”→ 15025“抛开锚链！”；前两个阶段各给一个小型遗物，后三个无遗物奖励；最后阶段给永久修正 harmonious_crew，并记录标本“水中的鱼”乐谱。

anomaly.7020 "On Solar Sails" clears the deposits and its only option creates the archaeological site site_space_shanty_dig "Message in a Bottle" (5 stages, needs a scientist; a story.5 report 30 days later; the intro text has a bio-ship variant). The stages run ancrel.15021 "Message in a Bottle" → 15022 "Space Shanty" → 15023 "Cosmic Beasties" → 15024 "Potent Potables" → 15025 "Cast Off the Hawser!"; the first two give a small artifact each, the last three none; the final stage gives the permanent modifier harmonious_crew and records the 'fish in the sea' sheet-music specimen.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

线性：五个阶段顺序固定，没有分支；失败事件（ancrel.8200–8215）是共用事故。

Linear: five stages in a fixed order, no branches; the failure events (ancrel.8200-8215) are shared incidents.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

遗址介绍文本按生物舰船帝国变化；失败事件文本对机器人物种有变体。

The site intro text varies for bio-ship empires; the failure events have robotic-species variants.
