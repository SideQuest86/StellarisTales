接近目标8010 → 系统已渗透8015 → 抢夺驿站8020；“开始行动”一天后“控制驿站”8025 结算（按我方谍报资产可选掠夺、摧毁或窃取），3天后驿站主人收到“驿站被渗透”8030。

Approaching the Target 8010 -> Systems Compromised 8015 -> Waystation Heist 8020; one day after launch, Waystation Seized 8025 resolves (loot, destroy or steal depending on our assets), and 3 days later the waystation's owner gets Waystation Compromised 8030.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

8025 的选项互斥；8030 按被掠夺或被毁/被窃给受害方不同回答。

8025's options are exclusive; 8030 gives the victim different replies for looting vs destruction/theft.
