## 如何开始 How it starts

迷信者（Superstitious Beliefs）文明的周期事件触发：每个13年周期开始（首个周期除外）掷出吉或凶（shroud.210/220/230 → 410/415，events/shroud_events.txt:961-1221），再从 on_action 的16个等权事件中抽取一个（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5749-5789）；本事件每个帝国至多一次（国家旗标 shroud_575_triggered）。吉兆事件。拥有“大档案馆”DLC和大档案馆、至少一个启用的历史物品展品。

Triggered by the cycles of a Superstitious Beliefs empire: each 13-year cycle (except the first) rolls good or bad luck (shroud.210/220/230 → 410/415, events/shroud_events.txt:961-1221) and one of the 16 equally weighted events of that on_action is drawn (common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5749-5789); this event happens at most once per empire (country flag shroud_575_triggered). A good luck event. Grand Archive DLC and Grand Archive, with at least one active historical-item exhibit.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单窗口通知，无后续剧情；唯一的选项直接生效。唯一选项：每月增加次级文物产出（二级奖励）。

A single notification window with no continuation; its only option applies directly. Only option: a monthly minor-artifact income bonus (tier 2).

No branching; independent of the other 15 events of the same draw pool, each shown as its own card.
