# 深层洞窟 / Deep Caverns

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。无战略资源的不宜居常规星球可能生成，但权重只有0.1（7级），且 AI 与玩家都可获得（为让 AI 也能找到活体金属）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on an uninhabitable regular planet without strategic resources, but with weight only 0.1 (level 7); AI and players can both get it (so the AI can find living metal too).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.150“活体金属湖”：地下洞窟中流淌着具有类生命特性的银色液态金属。星球获得活体金属矿藏，帝国获得（或推进25%）开采活体金属的科技。30天后的通用接触报告 story.1 是共享事件，不属于本故事。

distar.150 "Living Metal Lakes": the caverns hold silvery liquid metal with life-like properties. The planet gains a living metal deposit and the empire gains the living-metal mining tech option (or 25% progress). The generic contact report story.1 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.
