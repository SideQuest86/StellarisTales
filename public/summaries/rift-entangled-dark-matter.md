# 纠缠的暗物质 / Entangled Dark Matter

- 分类 Category：星界裂隙 Astral rifts (C4) · DLC：星界位面 Astral Planes

## 如何开始 How it starts

裂隙类型从探索帝国的裂隙池中按权重抽取，权重 1，无额外条件（`00_astral_rifts.txt:322–331`）。每阶段结束掷 1–10 的骰子，加技能与线索、减难度，≥14 进入下一章；第一章 2400 难度为 0。

Drawn by weight from the explorer's rift pool, weight 1, no extra conditions (`00_astral_rifts.txt:322–331`). Each phase ends with a d10 plus skill and clues minus difficulty; 14+ opens the next chapter. Chapter 2400 has difficulty 0.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 奇怪的网（2400）：探球身处一张巨大的能量网中，网的深处困着一团暗物质云，它的触须一碰到网就迸出火花。检查网（→ 2405）或接近暗物质团（有机科学家 → 2410，机械科学家 → 2411）。
2. 有限电流（2405）：这张网是为捕获非实体物质而建造的，中央装置限制着电流。拆除装置（→ 2425，结束）或调查被困的团块（→ 2410 / 2411）。
3. 心灵感应通讯（2410）/ 通讯已建立（2411）：团块是一个有意识的存在，问“你们是什么？”——三个回答都通向 2415。
4. 不祥提案（2415）：它的种族是“构成现实的基石”，同样热爱探索，却被另一种生命当作燃料捕获。它请求借科学家的身体作为容器离开。
   - 非格式塔帝国：献出科学家（非灵能者 → 2430 / 失败 2435；灵能者 → 2431）或拒绝（→ 2420）。
   - 格式塔帝国：同化它（→ 2440）或拒绝同化（→ 2420）。
5. 囚徒的请求（2420）：遭拒后它请求至少给它解脱。安乐死（→ 2426，非“非友好”帝国）、保留以供研究（→ 2445），或直接杀死（→ 2427，非友好帝国）。

1. Strange Mesh (2400): the Sphere is inside a vast energy mesh holding a cloud of dark matter whose tendrils spark against the barrier. Examine the mesh (→ 2405) or approach the mass (organic scientist → 2410, robotic → 2411).
2. Limited Current (2405): the mesh was built to trap non-solid matter; a central device limits its current. Remove the device (→ 2425, ending) or investigate the captive (→ 2410 / 2411).
3. Telepathic Communication (2410) / Communications Established (2411): the mass is a conscious being asking “What are you?” — all three answers lead to 2415.
4. An Ominous Proposal (2415): its kind are “the building blocks of reality”, wanderers like us, captured by another lifeform as fuel. It asks to leave inside the scientist's body.
   - Non-gestalt empires: offer the scientist (non-psionic → 2430 / failure 2435; psionic → 2431) or deny (→ 2420).
   - Gestalt empires: assimilate it (→ 2440) or deny assimilation (→ 2420).
5. A Prisoner's Plea (2420): refused, it asks at least for release. Euthanize it (→ 2426, not-unfriendly empires), keep it for study (→ 2445), or kill it (→ 2427, unfriendly empires).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 2425 / 2426 / 2427：实体被能量网压缩成晶体而死；大量星界丝缕与暗物质。
- 2430 吸收意识：科学家所有原有特质被替换（技能 5，学者、坚韧、位面理论家、灵能、裂隙行者、外来意识）。
- 2431 吸收意识（灵能者）：保留原特质，加坚韧、位面理论家与外来意识。
- 2435 不幸的难题（失败分支，基础 1.5%/次掷骰）：正文写科学家挣脱束缚后自尽，与实体同归于尽；大量星界丝缕与暗物质。（评注：脚本中没有杀死领袖的效果。）
- 2440 同化：格式塔帝国获得永久“外来意识”修正。
- 2445 笼中鸟：留下监视设备，裂隙获得物理与工程研究矿藏；约 3600–3960 天后 2450：实体被其维度中的未知者消灭，矿藏移除。
- 以上都结束探索（2450 在探索结束多年后发生）。

- 2425 / 2426 / 2427: the mesh crushes the entity into a crystal; a large astral thread reward and dark matter.
- 2430 Absorbed Consciousness: all of the scientist's traits are replaced (skill 5; scholar, Resilient, Planar Theorist, Psionic, Riftwalker, Foreign Consciousness).
- 2431 Absorbed Consciousness (psionic): traits kept, plus Resilient, Planar Theorist and Foreign Consciousness.
- 2435 Unfortunate Complications (failure branch, base 1.5% per roll): the text says the scientist broke free and took their own life, killing the entity as well; a large astral thread reward and dark matter. (Commentary: the script has no leader-killing effect.)
- 2440 assimilation: gestalt empires gain the permanent Foreign Consciousness modifier.
- 2445 Life in a Cage: surveillance is left in place and the rift gains physics and engineering research deposits; about 3600–3960 days later 2450: the entity is neutralised by an unknown actor in its own dimension and the deposits are removed.
- All end the exploration (2450 comes years later).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 科学家是有机还是机械、是否灵能者，帝国是否格式塔/平等主义/非友好，都会改变可选项（见上）。2410、2411、2415、2420、2440、2445 有格式塔、蜂巢、机械或灵能版本文字。

- Whether the scientist is organic or robotic or psionic, and whether the empire is gestalt, egalitarian or unfriendly, change the available options (see above). 2410, 2411, 2415, 2420, 2440 and 2445 have gestalt, hive, machine or psionic texts.
