# 来自堕落帝国的通讯 / Communication from a Fallen Empire
- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

异种友好的堕落帝国“索要一名人口”：五年随机脉冲（on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614）把本任务作为候选；各候选权重相对980，抽中后仍须通过其触发条件。（权重100。）条件：人口多于1000，非集群/机器，不在战争中，没问过（fe_xenophile_asked_pop），且有狂热亲外的堕落帝国在保护区（fe_the_preserve）星球上有空余居住空间。

A xenophile fallen empire "asks for a pop": The five-year random pulse (on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614) lists this task among its candidates; weights are out of 980 and the event's own trigger must still pass. (weight 100.) Conditions: more than 1000 pops, non-gestalt and non-machine, not at war, never asked before (fe_xenophile_asked_pop), and a fanatic-xenophile fallen empire with free housing on a preserve planet in contact.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

fallen_empires_tasks.4：堕落帝国请求让出本国一名人口放进它的保护区。a 同意（对方好感，我方获得持续3600天的“让出人口”修正）；b/c/d/e 拒绝，分别仅限排外、平等主义、威权、其他立场，对方好感下降。

fallen_empires_tasks.4: the fallen empire asks for one of our pops to place in its preserve. a agrees (the empire is pleased, we get a 3600-day "gave up a pop" modifier); b/c/d/e refuse, each limited to xenophobes, egalitarians, authoritarians or the rest, and the empire is displeased.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

同意与四种拒绝互斥，拒绝措辞由我方伦理决定。

Agreeing and the four refusals are exclusive; the refusal wording is set by our ethics.
