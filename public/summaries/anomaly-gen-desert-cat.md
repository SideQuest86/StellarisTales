# 沙丘 / Dunes

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration (C3)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 沙漠行星上的3级异常，生成权重3。  
  Level-3 anomaly on desert planets, spawn weight 3.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 三个结局：3035“盘点”（基础0；领队有严谨/粗心特质且能量储备≥50时+1）、3135“保持冷静”（权重1）、3060“沙中缩头”（权重1）。  
  Three outcomes: 3035 Taking Stock (base 0; +1 if the leader is meticulous/carefree and energy stock >= 50), 3135 Stay Calm (weight 1), 3060 Head in the Sand (weight 1).
- 3060选项a/c→HEAD_IN_SAND_PROJECT（需军事舰队）→随机三选一：3061得到空舰/3062敌对战舰/3063失败；失败后可重试（树中的回环）。  
  3060 options a/c → HEAD_IN_SAND_PROJECT (military fleet) → one of 3061 empty ship / 3062 hostile ship / 3063 failure, 33% each; the project can be restarted (loop in the tree).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 结局互斥；3061/3062/3063为项目随机结果。  
  Outcomes are exclusive; 3061/3062/3063 are random project results.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 3035的权重依赖领袖特质。  
  3035 weight depends on leader traits.
