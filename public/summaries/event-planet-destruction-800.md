# 行星被毒化 / Planet Toxified

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/planet_destruction_events.txt:2267-2578; common/on_actions/01_planet_destruction.txt:146-150 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

玩家用巨像的“毒流天降”武器毁掉/改造一颗行星时（common/on_actions/01_planet_destruction.txt:146-150），隐藏事件触发；若星球有所有者且不是自己，所有者的对手即玩家获得叙事窗口 planet_destruction.801。星球不能是栖息地或环世界。

When the player's Colossus fires "Toxifier" at a planet (common/on_actions/01_planet_destruction.txt:146-150) the hidden dispatcher fires; if the planet has an owner that is not the firing country, a narrative window planet_destruction.801 is shown to the firing player. The planet may not be a habitat or ringworld.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单窗口 planet_destruction.801，每个国家只显示一次（国家旗标 fired_toxifier）。若星球上有观测前哨，一天后另有 planet_destruction.1000 通知；若是机械意识（Contingency）的世界则触发 crisis.2040/2046（属危机故事）。

A single window planet_destruction.801, shown once per country (country flag fired_toxifier). If the planet had an observation outpost, planet_destruction.1000 follows one day later; Contingency machine worlds trigger crisis.2040/2046 (crisis stories).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

需要巨像武器；狂热净化者与终结者机械有专属选项（获得团结）。

Needs a Colossus weapon; Fanatic Purifiers and Terminator machines have their own option (unity).
