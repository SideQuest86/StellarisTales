# 精神减负 / Mental Offload

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 网络化局势（Cyberization）每月运行隐藏事件 cyber.5，从 on_cyberization_situation_random_events_list 中等权重（各1）抽取一个满足条件的随机事件（共7个：120、160、210、220、260、320、360）；事件期间设置 situation_cyberization_pause 暂停局势，结束后移除。
  The Cyberization situation runs hidden cyber.5 monthly, which draws one valid event with equal weight (1 each) from on_cyberization_situation_random_events_list (seven events: 120, 160, 210, 220, 260, 320, 360); the event sets situation_cyberization_pause while open and removes it afterwards.
- 触发：局势进度>450；每局至多一次（cyber_120_happened）。
  Trigger: situation progress > 450; at most once (cyber_120_happened).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 「精神外包」（机会）：研究员提议把AI监督者集成进设备和植入物。选项（非灵性与灵性各一对）：优先生产力 → AI_control 修正（灵性者需付500团结）；拒绝自主性损失；附开关则每月团结；唯物主义者得工程研究并取得 moderate_AI_control；威权者得工程研究并取得 AI_forced_control。
  "Mental Offload" (opportunity): researchers propose AI supervisors in devices and implants. Options (spiritualist and non-spiritualist pairs): productivity first -> AI_control modifier (spiritualists pay 500 unity); refuse the loss of autonomy; with an on/off switch, monthly unity; materialists gain engineering research and moderate_AI_control; authoritarians gain engineering research and AI_forced_control.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 单个窗口（无后续或只通向共享结局）；选项互斥，只能选一个。
  A single window (no follow-up, or only into the shared ending); options are exclusive, only one is chosen.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 选项集合随帝国的伦理、权威、政体特质而变，见条件。
  The available options vary with ethics, authority and civics, see the conditions.
