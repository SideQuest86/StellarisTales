# 贾巴蒂尼数据缓存：尤特母星的后续

## 故事
调查尤特母星后，研究者从旧数据的背景噪音中辨认出重复能量信号。信号来自一颗小行星，其中藏着贾巴蒂尼人的监听站与数据缓存：他们曾监视尤特母星系，防备尤特人归来。几个世纪后，这座哨站被改成超光速航行实验场，留下了仍可利用的理论资料。

After surveying the Yuht homeworld, researchers picked out a repeating energy signal from the background noise of old data. The signal came from an asteroid that held a Jabbardeeni listening post and data cache: they had watched the Yuht home system to guard against the Yuht returning. Centuries later the outpost was turned into a superlight travel test site, leaving theoretical data that can still be used.

## 玩家经历的顺序
1. 尤特完整母星报告 precursor.601 在拥有《四海皆臣》时，调度720–1800天后的 over.50。另一母星结果602没有这条调度。  
  The complete Yuht homeworld report (precursor.601), when you own Overlord, schedules over.50 720–1800 days later. The other homeworld outcome, 602, has no such schedule.
2. over.50“异常读数”说明新发现的信号。回应开启 YUHT_JABBARDEENI_CACHE 项目。  
  over.50 "Anomalous Readings" describes the newly found signal. Responding starts the YUHT_JABBARDEENI_CACHE project.
3. “微弱能量信号”项目要求科研船与科学家，基础研究时间360天，没有到期限制。  
  The "Faint Energy Signature" project requires a science ship and a scientist; base research time is 360 days, with no deadline.
4. 完成后进入 over.60“贾巴蒂尼数据缓存”，揭示监听站用途，领取研究成果。  
  Once complete, over.60 "Jabbardeeni Data Cache" opens, reveals the purpose of the listening post and grants the research results.

这段后续与花费文物开启的“尤特的秘密”是不同研究，不应串成彼此前置。

This follow-up is separate research from "Secrets of the Yuht", which is started by spending artifacts; the two should not be chained as prerequisites.

## 奖励与关联内容
结局给予物理学研究奖励，再依次检查科技状态，只执行首个符合的科技分支：尚无超空间中继器技术及其研究选项时开放该选项；否则依次检查二级超空间引擎、三级超空间引擎、虫洞稳定、实验性亚空间导航。后三类及二级引擎有各自的研究进度，不能解释成所有科技同时获得。

The ending grants a physics research reward, then checks tech status in order and runs only the first matching tech branch: if the hyperspace relay tech and its research option are not yet owned, that option opens; otherwise it checks the level 2 hyperspace engine, the level 3 hyperspace engine, wormhole stabilisation and experimental subspace navigation in turn. The last three and the level 2 engine each have their own research progress and must not be read as all being granted at once.

结局还调用 generate_lcluster_clue。在《遥远繁星》、L星团尚未开放且对应事件链存在时增加线索；满足线索完成条件时可能推进L星门研究。这是另一事件链的状态关联，不意味着必然进入L星团。该关联尚未在网站中完整实现。

The ending also calls generate_lcluster_clue. With Distant Stars, when the L-Cluster is not yet open and its event chain exists, it adds a clue; meeting the clue completion condition may advance L-Gate research. This is a state link to another event chain and does not mean the L-Cluster is necessarily entered. The link is not yet fully implemented on the site.

## 中英文差异
over.60中文把哨站建造时间描述为开战后不久，英文写作战争之后。两者都确认它用于防备尤特人返回，数世纪后改作实验场；这里不擅自消除时间表述的差异。

The Chinese text of over.60 describes the outpost as built shortly after the war began; the English says after the war. Both confirm it was used to guard against the Yuht's return and turned into a test site centuries later; the difference in timing is not smoothed over here.
