# 售卖天空 / For Sale: The Sky

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- “未来冲击”事件链后段：工作/家庭事件结束后隐藏事件 cyber.1000 从 on_futureshock_chain_leisure_events_list（7个事件，权重各5：休闲 1005/1010/1030/1055，社会 1205/1210/1255）抽取一个。
  Late part of the "Future Shock" event chain: after the work/family event hidden cyber.1000 draws one event from on_futureshock_chain_leisure_events_list (7 events, weight 5 each: leisure 1005/1010/1030/1055, society 1205/1210/1255).
- 触发：与某巨型企业有商业协定，且自己不是地下帝国。
  Trigger: a commercial pact with a megacorp, and the empire is not a subterranean empire.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 1010「出售天空」：a/b 签约（sold_sky_1 / 巨型企业版）→ 180+随机90天后 1015；c 团结奖励；d 拒绝；b/d 路径随后进入 cyber.1500。
  1010 "For Sale: The Sky": a/b sign (sold_sky_1 / megacorp variant) -> 1015 after 180 days (+ random 90); c unity reward; d refuse; b/d paths continue to cyber.1500.
- 1015：升级为 sold_sky_2 并置天空广告牌，或撤回；之后 cyber.1500。
  1015: upgrade to sold_sky_2 with sky billboards, or withdraw; then cyber.1500.
- 事件结束后180+随机90天由隐藏事件 cyber.1500 按政体权威选出最终事件 1505–1525 之一（跨故事的共享结局，视为枢纽）。
  After 180 days (+ random 90) hidden cyber.1500 picks one of the final events 1505-1525 by government authority (a shared ending across stories, treated as a hub).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 1015 只在签约后出现。
  1015 appears only after signing.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 巨型企业与商会政体有专属选项。
  Megacorps and Merchant Guilds civic have their own options.
