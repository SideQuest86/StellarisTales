# 四十二年零三天 / 42 Years and 3 Days

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。没有站点矿藏、且所在星系有前超光速文明星球（非环形世界）或未殖民的宜居星球时可能生成（3级，权重3，每个帝国一次）。卡片标题与“残骸场”相同。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on a body without station deposits in a system that has a primitive (non-ringworld) planet or an uncolonised habitable planet (level 3, weight 3, once per empire). The card shares the title "Debris Field".

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.1045“四十二年零三天”：残骸中的密封舱里有一台极精确的原子钟，正倒数42年零3天后的某个日期。注意观察 → 15323天（42年3天）后隐藏 distar.310 在当时满足条件的结果中等概率选一（各基础0，满足条件+1）；认定是炸弹拆掉 → 只有研究奖励。
2. 结果：distar.311 纳米机器人喷向星系内一颗不宜居星球——等待 → distar.312 星球被改造成盖亚世界；开火（星系内有我方舰船时）→ distar.313 漏网的纳米机器人把星球变成纳米之海。distar.315 纳米机器人组成一艘可接收的无人战舰（我方舰队可进入星系时）。distar.320 在原地建成计算网络。distar.330 信号引爆前超光速文明星球（核平）。distar.335 引爆一颗无人宜居星球。312、313、315 还提供L星团线索。

1. distar.1045 "42 Years and 3 Days": a sealed capsule holds an ultra-precise atomic clock counting down to a date 42 years and 3 days away. Keep an eye on it → after 15323 days the hidden distar.310 picks with equal chance among the outcomes valid then (each base 0, +1 if its condition holds); declare it a bomb and snip the wires → research reward only.
2. Outcomes: distar.311 nanites stream to an uninhabitable planet in the system — wait → distar.312 the planet is terraformed into a Gaia world; fire (if we have ships there) → distar.313 surviving nanites cover it in a nanite slurry. distar.315 the nanites build an unmanned warship we can take (if our fleets can access the system). distar.320 a computational network appears. distar.330 a signal nukes the primitive planet. distar.335 an empty habitable planet is nuked. 312, 313 and 315 also give an L-Cluster clue.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.310 的五个结果互斥，权重取决于42年后星系的状况；distar.311 的等待/开火互斥。

distar.310's five outcomes are exclusive, weighted by the system's state 42 years later; distar.311's wait/fire are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.330 has xenophobe/xenophile wordings.
