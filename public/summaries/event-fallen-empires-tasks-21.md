# 来自堕落帝国的通讯 / Communication from a Fallen Empire
- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

唯物堕落帝国“索要科学家”：五年随机脉冲（on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614）把本任务作为候选；各候选权重相对980，抽中后仍须通过其触发条件。（权重50。）条件：非AI默认国家，人口多于1000，非集群，不在战争中，没问过（fe_materialist_asked_scientist），拥有技能高于4且不在探索星门的科学家，且有狂热唯物的堕落帝国与我方有通讯并“庇护”或“轻视”我方。

A materialist fallen empire "asks for a scientist": The five-year random pulse (on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614) lists this task among its candidates; weights are out of 980 and the event's own trigger must still pass. (weight 50.) Conditions: a human default country with over 1000 pops, non-gestalt, not at war, never asked (fe_materialist_asked_scientist), owning a scientist with base skill above 4 not exploring an astral rift, and a fanatic-materialist fallen empire in contact that is patronizing or dismissive.
