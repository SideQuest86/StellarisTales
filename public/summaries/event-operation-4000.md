# 天灾信标 / Crisis Beacon

- 分类 Category：银河轶事 C8 stories · DLC：Nemesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

玩家发起“天灾信标”（common/espionage_operation_types/operations.txt:831-916）：需要正在进行的危机、近期未用过；3个阶段，难度最高（@diff_t8）。
Crisis Beacon needs an ongoing crisis and no recent use; three stages at the highest difficulty.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. operation.4000 “小队待命”：选择要吸引的天灾势力（最多三个）。 Choose which crisis to draw.
2. operation.4001 “前进暗号”：小队出发。 3. operation.4002 “大鱼上钩”：“开始行动！”→ operation.4005：天灾舰队被引向目标国；目标国收到 operation.4010“[舰队]朝首都行动”。
   Launch -> 4005 (the crisis fleet is lured toward the target); the target is warned in 4010.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 4000 的三个目标互斥。 The three targets of 4000 are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 失败的检定走“深度潜伏”事件池（扼杀 / 特工叛逃 / 信任危机）。 Failed rolls use the deep-cover pool.
