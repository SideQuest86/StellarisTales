# 不应拒绝的交易 / A Deal We Should Not Refuse

- 分类 Category：巨兽与城邦 · DLC：霸主 Overlord（需“大可汗”出现）

## 如何开始 How it starts
每两年检查一次：大可汗（觉醒掠夺者）存在且未死，并有某个佣兵城邦的舰队未出租。若该城邦所在星系属于 AI，可汗直接雇走舰队，不出现窗口；若属于玩家，则该玩家收到本通知。
Bi-yearly check: the Great Khan exists and lives, and some mercenary enclave has no leased fleet. If the enclave's system belongs to an AI the Khan simply hires the fleet; if it belongs to a player, that player gets this notice.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline
城邦告知：可汗提出了“无法拒绝”的报价，除非两个月内另有雇主。
The enclave reports the Khan's offer and asks for a counter-hire within two months.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes
- 联络该城邦：打开“佣兵城邦”联络菜单（跨故事链接），可抢先租下舰队；60天后检查。Contact them: opens the Mercenary Enclave menu to hire the fleet first; checked after 60 days.
- 暂时负担不起：60天后检查。Cannot afford it: checked after 60 days.
- 斥为叛徒：7天后检查。Call them traitors: checked after 7 days.
- 检查（隐藏 enclave.2051）：若舰队仍未被租用，主力舰队交给可汗1510天并跟随可汗舰队。The hidden check gives the still-unleased main fleet to the Khan for 1510 days.
