# 漂泊胶囊 / Drifting Capsule

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/cosmic_storms_events_1.txt:4008-4262; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5509; common/special_projects/03_projects_cosmic_storms.txt:839 · DLC: Cosmic Storms · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

首次遭遇风暴后约1800天（5年），隐藏事件 cstorms.1815 在境内选一个系统（游牧帝国则在驿站所在系统）弹出“漂泊胶囊”。

About 1800 days (5 years) after the first storm encounter, hidden cstorms.1815 picks a system inside the borders (the waystation system for nomads) and shows the "Drifting Capsule".

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

cstorms.1816/1817 启用“研究胶囊”项目；完成后 cstorms.1820“风暴陷阱”：选a获得工程研究并启用“打开风暴陷阱”项目；选b获得工程研究、团结和影响力后结束。陷阱项目成功后隐藏事件 cstorms.1825 随机生成六种风暴之一，cstorms.1826“风暴释放”给工程研究、风暴破坏者船体科技进度和洞察修正。

cstorms.1816/1817 enable the "Study the Capsule" project; when done cstorms.1820 "Storm Trap": option a gives engineering research and enables the "Open the storm trap" project; option b gives engineering research, unity and influence and ends. When the trap project succeeds hidden cstorms.1825 spawns one of six storm types at random and cstorms.1826 "Storm Released" grants engineering research, storm-breaker hull tech progress and an insight modifier.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

1816/1817 二选一取决于是否为游牧；1820 的 a/b 互斥；1825 在六种风暴中等权随机。

1816/1817 depend on nomad status; 1820 a/b are exclusive; 1825 is an equal-weight roll over six storm types.
