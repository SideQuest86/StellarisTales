# 天选民 / The Chosen

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 隐藏事件 fircon.3500 由古遗迹事件 ancrel.12050 的一个随机分支触发，生成“天选民”家园系统。其后：天选民舰队通过虫洞抵达某帝国（3505，隐藏），或在天选民系统发生首次接触（on_first_contact → 3515）。
  Hidden fircon.3500, fired by a random branch of ancient-relics event ancrel.12050, spawns the Chosen home system. Then either the Chosen fleet emerges through a wormhole into some empire (hidden 3505) or first contact occurs in the Chosen system (on_first_contact -> 3515).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 3505（虫洞）：对拥有情报的其他帝国发出 3510「天选民」并建立通信；若目标是普通帝国或觉醒者，天选民对其发动灭绝战争。
  3505 (wormhole): every other empire with intel gets 3510 "The Chosen" with comms established; if the target is a normal empire or awakened fallen empire the Chosen declare a cleansing war.
- 3515（首次接触）：在天选民系统建立通信后，天选民对接触方宣战（wg_cleansing）。
  3515 (first contact): once contact is made in the chosen system the Chosen declare a cleansing war on the contacting empire.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 3510 与 3515 是同一冲突的两个入口，互斥。
  3510 and 3515 are two entries to the same conflict and exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 触发限于普通帝国与觉醒的堕落帝国。
  Only normal empires and awakened fallen empires qualify.
