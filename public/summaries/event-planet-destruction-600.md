# 圣地 / Holy World

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/planet_destruction_events.txt:1635-1873; common/on_actions/01_planet_destruction.txt:20,41,126,149,198; events/toxoids_events.txt:380-413; events/colony_events_1.txt:4598; events/fallen_empire_awakening_events.txt:143 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

毁坏、屏蔽或淹没一颗“圣地”（holy_planet 修正），或把圣地改成墓地世界（toxoids.50），而某个信仰圣地的堕落帝国（AI 性格 holy_planets，未觉醒）与施放者没有交战时，隐藏事件 planet_destruction.600/605/607（或 toxoids.50）选一个“被激怒的堕落帝国”，5天后给施放者 planet_destruction.610（淹没则 .615）。

Fragging, shielding or flooding a holy world (holy_planet modifier), or terraforming one into a tomb world (toxoids.50), while a holy-world fallen empire (AI personality holy_planets, not awakened) is not at war with the perpetrator: hidden planet_destruction.600/605/607 (or toxoids.50) pick an outraged fallen empire and, 5 days later, show planet_destruction.610 (planet_destruction.615 for a flood) to the perpetrator.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

窗口 610/615 按施放者的伦理/文明显示不同选项（灵性、唯物、军国……、集群、吞噬蜂群、终结者、净化者），选项本身无效果；其 after 块设置 holy_world_killed 并向该堕落帝国触发 fallen_empires_awakening.1，使其苏醒（fallen_empires_awakening.1 令所有玩家收到“睡狮的苏醒”fallen_empires_awakening.2，并在5天后由 planet_destruction.611 宣战，战争名为“…圣地之战”，战争目标为征服，events/fallen_empire_awakening_events.txt:138-144）。后续见“睡狮的苏醒”等堕落帝国觉醒故事。

Windows 610/615 offer options by the perpetrator's ethic or civic (spiritualist, materialist, militarist ... gestalt, devouring swarm, terminator, purifier); the options themselves have no effect. Its after block sets holy_world_killed and fires fallen_empires_awakening.1 at that fallen empire, which awakens (every player gets fallen_empires_awakening.2 "Sleepers Awake" and 5 days later planet_destruction.611 declares the "... Holy World War" with a subjugation war goal, events/fallen_empire_awakening_events.txt:138-144). Continues in the fallen-empire awakening stories.
