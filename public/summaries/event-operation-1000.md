# 招募线人 / Acquire Asset

- 分类 Category：银河轶事 C8 stories · 来源 Sources：events/nemesis_operations_events_1.txt, common/espionage_operation_types/operations.txt · DLC：Nemesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

玩家对一个国家发起间谍行动“招募线人”（common/espionage_operation_types/operations.txt:280-337）：3个阶段（需要灭绝/新霸权DLC，对象为普通国家），花费 45 影响力和 6 能量/月，一次只能进行一个行动；每个阶段完成时触发对应阶段事件。
The operation Acquire Asset has three stages (45 influence, 6 energy upkeep, one operation at a time); each completed stage fires its event.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. operation.1000 “动员特工”：特工加紧监视潜在目标。 Operatives step up surveillance.
2. operation.1001 “收缩势力范围”：拟定候选名单。 A shortlist is drawn up.
3. operation.1002 “建立线人联系”：选项“开始行动！”→ operation.1006 “线人已获取”：在目标国官僚体系中获得一名线人，供以后的行动使用（排在最后）。 Launch -> 1006 Asset Acquired.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 阶段依次发生；失败的检定可能引出“行动失手”随机事件（见各失手故事）。 Stages in order; failed rolls may trigger the mishap events.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 需要新霸权（Nemesis）DLC。 Needs Nemesis.
