# 外星生命 / Alien Life

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。没有危险野生动物修正的宜居星球可能生成（3级，权重3）；荒野帝国不会遇到。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on habitable planets without the dangerous wildlife modifier (level 3, weight 3); not for Wilderness empires.

The planet gains a dangerous wildlife blocker, a savage wildlands deposit and dangerous_wildlife. The generic contact report story.1 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.
