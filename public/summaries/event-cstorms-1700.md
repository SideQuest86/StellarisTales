# 超磁化行星核心 / Hyper-Magnetized Planetary Core

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

磁暴离开拥有殖民地的星系时（on_storm_left_system，00_on_actions.txt:5432），隐藏事件 cstorms.1700 有25%概率（已有强磁场则50%）在一个未受影响的非人造殖民星球上排程，90天后 cstorms.1705。

When a magnetic storm leaves a system with a colony (on_storm_left_system, 00_on_actions.txt:5432) hidden cstorms.1700 has a 25% chance (50% on a planet with a strong magnetic field) of scheduling cstorms.1705 on an unaffected non-artificial colony after 90 days.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

cstorms.1705“超磁化行星核心”：星球获得 pm_magnetized_planetary_core 修正（每国一次，已有弱磁场的星球不会被选中）。

cstorms.1705 "Hyper-Magnetized Planetary Core": the planet gets the pm_magnetized_planetary_core modifier (once per country; planets with a weak magnetic field are not chosen).
