Single result anomaly.2560 "Gas Giant Ship": option a enables project GAS_GIANT_SHIP_PROJECT (science ship and scientist, 180 days); option b takes 50 influence instead. On completion the project picks at random: 70% anomaly.2561 (creates a derelict "Salvage" fleet, leader +200 experience) or 30% anomaly.2562 ("Unfortunate", only leader +200 experience).
