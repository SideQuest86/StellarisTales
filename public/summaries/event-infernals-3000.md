## 如何开始 How it starts

“地狱”物种包：殖民地满1年时从 on_colony_1_year_old 的随机池（权重20/510）抽到；要求行星为火山星球且有地狱族（has_infernals），所属国为默认类型、本国此前未触发过（triggered_imminent_erruption），行星没有其他殖民地事件。

Infernals: drawn from the on_colony_1_year_old random pool (weight 20 of 510) when a colony turns one year old; the planet must be volcanic with Infernals, the owner a default-type country that has not had it yet (triggered_imminent_erruption), and the planet without another colony event.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

3000 四个选项，5天后各自得到结果：疏散（花费500能量，3005 “及时”）；观望（50/50：3010 “火山之怒”损失惨重 或 3015 平安）；专注发电（3020 “增强电网”）；采集岩浆（3025 “岩浆采集”）。

3000 offers four options with results five days later: evacuate (costs 500 energy, 3005 "Just in time"); wait and see (50/50: 3010 "Volcanic Wrath" heavy losses or 3015 safe); focus on power (3020 "Enhanced Power-Grid"); harvest the magma (3025 "Magma Harvesting").

One of four; with "wait and see" 3010 and 3015 are random alternatives.
