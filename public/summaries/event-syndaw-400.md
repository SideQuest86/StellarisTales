# 计算超频 / Computational Overclocking

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany
- DLC：Synthetic Dawn

## 如何开始 How it starts

拥有大量机器人人口的帝国（机器人人口>75，或比例>25%）：每十年的脉冲 syndaw.400 → 401 以30%概率检查，通过后得到“超频”窗口 500，或直接得到“一个问题” 545。

An empire with many robot pops (more than 75, or more than 25%): the decade pulse syndaw.400 -> 401 checks with 30% and then gives the "Overclocking" window 500, or goes directly to "A Question" 545.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

500“计算超频”（可选保守或利用）→ 501 → 502 逐步揭示 AI 的苏醒 → 545“一个问题”（机器人质问灵魂：三种回应）→ 启动情势“AI相关事件”（事件链“抑制自决AI”、两个专项“中和失控AI”“慌乱措施”可提前终结，成功后 550 / 555）；情势期间每月可能出现八个AI事故事件（505–540）；情势走满则 1020“机器起义”：起义者作为新国家出现并与主人开战，通知旁观者 1025。战争结束时 1200“前主人被征服”（→ 1030 通告）或 1205“机器起义被镇压”。

500 "Computational Overclocking" (cautious or exploit) -> 501 -> 502 gradually reveal the awakening AI -> 545 "A Question" (the robots ask about souls; three replies) -> starts the situation "AI-Related Incidents" (event chain "Inhibit Self-Deterministic AIs", two projects "Neutralize Rampant AI" / "Panicked Measures" can end it early, with 550 / 555 on success); during the situation eight AI-incident events (505-540) may appear each month; if the situation completes, 1020 "The Machine Uprising": the uprising appears as a new country at war with its master and others are told by 1025. When the war ends, 1200 "Former Master Conquered" (-> 1030 announcement) or 1205 "Machine Uprising Quashed".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

超频：保守 / 利用；545 的三种回应各以不同方式进入情势；两个专项提前化解（550 补丁 / 555 清除，各带“任其发展”的选项）；起义的两种战争结局互斥。

Overclocking: cautious / exploit; the three replies of 545 enter the situation differently; two projects defuse it early (550 patch / 555 wipe, each with a "let it ride" option); the two war outcomes are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

嵌入的“起义者为游牧民”分支（nomads.20）已移出本故事；个别事件按帝国类型而异。

The nomadic-originator branch (nomads.20) was removed from this story; some events vary by empire type.
