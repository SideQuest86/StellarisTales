# 失去殖民地！ / Colony Lost!

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/on_action_events_1.txt:7414-7565; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:94,3354-3356 on_monthly_pulse / on_colony_ownerless · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

每月脉冲或殖民地失去所有者时（00_on_actions.txt:94,3356），隐藏事件 action.85 检查多国共有系统：星球所属国不是星系哨站所有者、双方又不交战时，星球转归哨站所有者。

On the monthly pulse or when a colony becomes ownerless (00_on_actions.txt:94,3356) hidden action.85 checks mixed-ownership systems: if the planet's owner is not the starbase owner and they are not at war, the planet flips to the starbase owner.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

action.86（隐藏）翻转星球，前主人收到 action.87“失去殖民地！”，新主人收到 action.88“获得殖民地！”。若新主人是心灵守望者且星球是被流放的星球，一天后触发 shroud.6700。

Hidden action.86 flips the planet; the former owner gets action.87 "Colony Lost!" and the new owner action.88 "Colony Gained!". For a Mindwardens new owner and an exiled planet, shroud.6700 follows one day later.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

87 与 88 是同一事件的两方视角。

action.87 and action.88 are the two perspectives of one flip.
