# 银河市场的诞生 / Birth of the Galactic Market

- 分类 Category：银河轶事 C8 stories

## 如何开始 How it starts

决议 resolution_galactic_market_form 通过（common/resolutions/00_resolutions.txt:13216）后对提议国触发 action.96（events/on_action_events_2.txt:13-69）。
Passing the founding resolution fires action.96 on the proposer.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. action.96 “银河市场的诞生”：开启全局提名期（ongoing_market_nomination）和事件链“创建银河市场”；向其余成员触发 action.97 “银河市场的诞生”；选项按是否商业联合体（a “让贸易流动！”/ b “明白了”）。解锁决定“提名银河市场主办地”。
   action.96 opens the nomination period and the chain "Founding the Galactic Market", informs the others with action.97, and unlocks the nomination decision.
2. 提名期结束（@galactic_market_nomination_period）后全局隐藏 action.98：有被提名的合格星球 → 选出主办星球 → action.99 → action.104 “银河市场：设立于…”→ action.101 通知所有成员；无合格提名 → 提议国 action.1000、其他成员 action.1005 “银河市场未能建立”。（这几页属于故事“银河共同体·重新安置/建立市场”）
   After the period, hidden action.98 either picks the host (-> 99 -> 104 -> 101) or announces failure (1000 / 1005); those pages belong to the Galactic Community market story.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 提名结果互斥：设立 / 无人提名失败。 Founding and failure are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 正文按是否排外、是否商业联合体而变。 Text varies with xenophobia and megacorp status.
