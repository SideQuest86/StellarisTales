# 研究军事方面的应用 / Find Military Applications

- 分类 Category：先驱者与考古 Precursors · DLC：《远古遗物》Ancient Relics

## 如何开始 How it starts

稀有文物行动“研究军事方面的应用”（artifact_find_military_applications，common/artifact_actions/artifact_actions.txt:352）：需要 tech_arcane_deciphering，花费150稀有文物，之后有900天冷却（与民用版共用修正 artifact_find_applications_cooldown）；触发 ancrel.10010。

The artifact action "Find Military Applications" (artifact_find_military_applications, common/artifact_actions/artifact_actions.txt:352): needs tech_arcane_deciphering, costs 150 minor artifacts, then a 900-day cooldown shared with the peaceful version (modifier artifact_find_applications_cooldown); it fires ancrel.10010.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

ancrel.10010 “我们该聚焦哪一方面？”四个互斥的选择，各自给一个持续1800天的修正：战斗药物（陆军，非机械帝国）、修改安全阈值（机器人陆军，机械帝国）、谐波隔离（盾伤害）、弱点分析（装甲伤害）；“算了”则退还50稀有文物并移除冷却。

ancrel.10010 "What aspect shall we focus on?" Four exclusive choices, each a 1800-day modifier: Combat Drugs (armies, non-machine empires), Modified Safety Thresholds (machine armies, machine empires), Harmonic Isolation (shield damage), Weak Point Analysis (armor damage); "Actually, never mind" refunds 50 minor artifacts and removes the cooldown.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 护盾/装甲伤害两项的 AI 选择权重取决于偏好武器类型和是否正遭遇入侵危机。 / The AI weights of the shield and armor options depend on the preferred weapon type and on whether an invasion crisis is under way.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 第一个选项仅非机械帝国可见，第二个仅机械帝国可见。 / The first option is shown to non-machine empires only, the second to machine empires only.
