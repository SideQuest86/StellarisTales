# 行商舰队：拉奇工业公司 / Caravan Fleet: The Racket Industrial Enterprise

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

风暴离开拥有者的星系时（on_storm_left_system，00_on_actions.txt:5439），隐藏事件 cstorms.1830 检查：玩家是公司（megacorp）、与“行商”第三舰队国家有通讯且不敌对、尚无“风暴预测2”科技，且每国一次。

When a storm leaves an owned system (on_storm_left_system, 00_on_actions.txt:5439) hidden cstorms.1830 checks: the owner is a megacorp, has communications with the caravaneer fleet 3 country without hostility, lacks tech_storm_prediction_2, and the event has not fired for it before.

cstorms.1835 "Caravan Fleet: The Racket Industrial Enterprise": the caravaneers offer to sell storm-prediction technology.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

选项a：花费1500能量得到“风暴预测1”；若已有则选项a（2000能量）得到“风暴预测2”；选项b：拒绝。选项按已有科技互斥。

Option a: pay 1500 energy for Storm Prediction I; if owned, the second option a (2000 energy) gives Storm Prediction II; option b declines. The options are exclusive by technology owned.

Needs the Cosmic Storms DLC and contact with the Caravaneers.
