## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。宜居星球的不宜居卫星（无站点矿藏）可能生成（3级，权重3，全局一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on an uninhabitable moon (no station deposits) of a habitable planet (level 3, weight 3, once per game).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.2025 "Artificial Moon": the whole moon is an artificial body of mineral-rich rock around a steel frame, built to slow its planet's rotation, lengthen days and raise tides for habitation; nobody ever settled there. The moon gains a minerals deposit.
