# 逆行轨道 / Backwards Orbit

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：Shadows of the Shroud（has_shroud_dlc）

## 如何开始 How it starts

毒性、荒芜、寒冷荒芜、熔岩或冰冻星球，所在恒星系至少3颗行星且有已勘测的相邻系统时可能生成（3级，权重3；需要 has_shroud_dlc）。

Can spawn on a toxic, barren, cold barren, molten or frozen planet in a system with at least 3 planets and a surveyed neighbouring system (level 3, weight 3; needs has_shroud_dlc).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单一结果 extreme_frontiers.390“流浪行星”：在已勘测的相邻系统中记录流浪行星的来源（wandering_planet_origin），星球获得 d_physics_5 矿藏，帝国获得每月物理研究加成。

Single result extreme_frontiers.390 "Wandering Planet": records a surveyed neighbouring system as the wandering planet's origin (wandering_planet_origin), the planet gains a d_physics_5 deposit and the empire monthly physics research.
