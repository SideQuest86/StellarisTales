# 生命迹象 / Life Signs

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

没有战略资源的冰冻星球可能生成（2级，权重3）。

Can spawn on a frozen planet without a strategic resource (level 2, weight 3).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

两个结果各占50%。anomaly.2510“冰下海洋”：d_society_3 矿藏与 subterranean_ocean 修正，并（非机械帝国或机械奴仆公民）启用项目 SEA_EXPEDITION_PROJECT“组织深海探险”（需科学船和科学家，研究180天），完成后随机（各50%）：anomaly.2512（研究加成、活金属矿藏并推进 tech_mine_living_metal）或 anomaly.2513（仅领袖+200经验）。anomaly.6900“冷酷的思绪”：清除矿藏后多个选项（物理研究、社会研究加遗物、符合条件时交出遗物、团结、演化掠食者 DNA、d_engineering_4 矿藏）。

Two results, 50% each. anomaly.2510 "Ocean Beneath the Ice": d_society_3 deposit plus subterranean_ocean modifier, and (for non-machine empires or Machine Servitor) it enables project SEA_EXPEDITION_PROJECT (science ship and scientist, 180 days) which then picks at random (50% each) anomaly.2512 (research bonus, living-metal deposit, tech_mine_living_metal progress) or anomaly.2513 (leader +200 experience only). anomaly.6900 "Cool Thoughts": deposits cleared, then several options (physics research; society research plus artifacts; artifact surrender when an unfinished artifact chain applies; unity; Evolutionary Predators DNA; d_engineering_4 deposit).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

两个起始结果互斥；2510 的项目结果 2512/2513 也互斥。机械帝国（非奴仆）没有项目，故 2510 在其上无后续。

The two starting results are exclusive; the project results 2512/2513 are also exclusive. Machine empires without Servitor get no project, so 2510 ends there for them.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

2510 的项目按机械帝国状态启用；2512 的描述有机械变体；6900 的选项按起源与遗物事件链进度变化。

The project in 2510 depends on machine-empire status; 2512 has a machine description variant; 6900's options depend on origin and artifact-chain progress.
