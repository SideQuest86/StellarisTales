# 破坏恒星基地设施 / Sabotage Starbase Facility

- 分类 Category：银河轶事 C8 stories · 来源 Sources：events/nemesis_operations_events_1.txt, common/espionage_operation_types/operations.txt · DLC：Nemesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

玩家对一座有非船坞模块或建筑的恒星基地发起“破坏恒星基地设施”（common/espionage_operation_types/operations.txt:582-689）：3个阶段，目标必须有可破坏的设施。
Sabotage Starbase Facility needs a target starbase with non-shipyard modules or buildings; three stages.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. operation.2000 “接近目标”：特工假扮劳工出发。 2. operation.2001 “特工就位”：被基地接收，其余转入周边。 3. operation.2002 “就绪信号”：选项“开始行动！”→ operation.2005：成功破坏军需设施；a 干净撤出 / b 彻底摧毁这座基地 / c 同时袭击防御平台（摧毁一个）；目标国收到 operation.2010 “[基地]遭破坏”；
   2010 之后 10% 的几率（15天后）出现 espionage.1040 “意外收获”（获得新线人）。
   Stages -> launch -> 2005 (clean exit / wreck the starbase / hit the defense platforms); the target is told in 2010; 10% chance of the lucky catch afterwards.
- operation.2006 “破坏行动终止”：任何阶段中目标基地发生重大变化时中止。 2006: the operation is aborted if the starbase changes drastically.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 2005 的三个选项互斥。 The three options of 2005 are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 2006 是三个阶段共有的中止出口。 2006 is the shared abort exit.
