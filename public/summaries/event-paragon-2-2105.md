# 卓越个体 / Exceptional Individuals

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany
- DLC：Galactic Paragons

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要银河巨影DLC；每当科技升级（on_tech_increased）时检查，最近升级的科技是“异星语言学”（tech_xeno_linguistics）才触发。

Needs the Galactic Paragons DLC; checked on every technology increase (on_tech_increased) and fires only when the technology just increased is Xeno-Linguistics (tech_xeno_linguistics).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单窗口通知，无后续：宣布银河中散布着杰出个体。选项“发出召唤”（非排外且非集群）或“可以利用，一时而已”（排外者）都会让“知名领袖”从此可以联系我们，并获得随接触数量变化的每月影响力加成（tier2influencecontact）。

A single notification with no continuation: the galaxy is full of exceptional individuals. The option "Send out the call" (non-xenophobe, non-gestalt) or "They might prove useful... for a time" (xenophobes) both mean Renowned Leaders can now contact the empire, and a monthly influence bonus scaled by contacts (tier2influencecontact) is added.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

没有分支；两个选项按伦理互斥出现，效果相同。

No branches; the two options are exclusive by ethic and have the same effect.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

正文和选项分排外/非排外；集群共识没有选项一（events/paragon_events_2.txt:729-760）。

Text and options differ for xenophobes and non-xenophobes; gestalt consciousness lacks the first option (events/paragon_events_2.txt:729-760).
