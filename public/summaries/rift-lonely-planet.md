## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 巨卵（1100）：一颗荒芜行星上有一窝与探球一样大的巨卵，即将孵化；成体显然在星球之外。
2. 饮星者（1105）：有翼的巨大幼雏破壳而出，追逐星光飞向高空。靠近（→ 1110 友好的幼雏，难度 6：取得样本，它们以光伏鳞片获取能量，能耐受真空）或保持距离（→ 1115 恒星帆航，难度 2：观察它们像太阳帆一样飞行，获得 15 年“恒星帆航洞见”）。
3. 结局：一只比战列舰还大的成体忽然出现，带着幼雏穿过新撕开的裂缝离去，留下大量星界丝缕——靠近路线为 1125“星界孵育”，保持距离路线为 1120“星界迁徙”（两段正文相同）。

1. Giant Eggs (1100): on a barren world sits a clutch of eggs as big as the Sphere, about to hatch; the adults are evidently elsewhere.
2. Star Drinkers (1105): huge winged hatchlings emerge and climb toward the starlight. Get closer (→ 1110 Friendly Hatchlings, difficulty 6: samples show photovoltaic scales and vacuum resistance) or keep a safe distance (→ 1115 Solar Sailing, difficulty 2: they fly like solar sails; 15 years of Solar Sailing Insight).
3. Ending: an adult larger than a battleship phases in and leads the hatchlings through a fresh tear, leaving astral threads behind — 1125 “Astral Spawning” on the close route, 1120 “Astral Migration” on the distant route (same text).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 1120 / 1125：都结束探索，获得大量星界丝缕与凝聚力。差别只在途中奖励：1110 的研究（工程，生物舰船帝国为社会学）或 1115 的 15 年修正。

- 1120 / 1125: both end the exploration with astral threads and unity. They differ only in the reward on the way: research in 1110 (engineering, or society for bio-ship empires) or the 15-year modifier in 1115.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 1110 的选项：使用生物舰船的帝国为“这和我们的舰船可差不多！”；“演化猎手”起源只看到专属选项“既非鸟类亦非节肢动物”，另得该起源局势进度与 DNA。

- 1110 options: bio-ship empires get “They are comparable to our ships!”; Evolutionary Predators empires see only “Neither Avian or Arthropoid. Very peculiar.”, with extra origin-situation progress and DNA.
