## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每年脉冲 galcom.1（隐藏）：银河社群未成立且未开始，至少3个非灭绝国家，且有一个非灭绝非格式塔国家与70%以上的可玩国家有通信。
  Yearly pulse galcom.1 (hidden): the Galactic Community is neither founded nor begun, at least 3 non-homicidal countries exist and one non-homicidal, non-gestalt country is in contact with 70%+ of playable countries.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 2「银河社群」：向相互通信的国家提议成立；选项设赞成（galcom_form_aye）或反对（galcom_form_nay）。
  2 "A Galactic Community": the proposal goes to all countries in contact; options set aye (galcom_form_aye) or nay (galcom_form_nay).
- 360天后 galcom.3 点票：≥3票赞成 → 赞成国入会并收到 5「银河社群的诞生」，反对国收到 6；20天后向其他潜在申请者发出邀请（galcom.15 → 16）；不足 → 10「银河社群无人支持」并在50年后（18000天）由 galcom.20 重试。
  360 days later galcom.3 tallies: 3+ aye -> aye countries join and get 5 "The Birth of the Galactic Community", nay countries 6; 20 days later invitations go to other potential applicants (galcom.15 -> 16); otherwise 10 "No Support for Galactic Community" and galcom.20 retries 50 years later (18000 days).
- 此后每十年（galcom.14）向尚未加入的国家再次发出 16 邀请；首个银河议会成立后 galcom.50（隐藏）→ 5天后 51「银河议会成立」。
  Afterwards every decade (galcom.14) 16 invites countries that have not joined; once the first Galactic Council exists galcom.50 (hidden) -> 51 "Formation of the Galactic Council" 5 days later.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 5 / 6 / 10 视玩家的表态与票数而定，互斥。
  5 / 6 / 10 depend on the player's vote and the count, and are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

  16 has variants for the Heir of the Khan and Solarpunk.
