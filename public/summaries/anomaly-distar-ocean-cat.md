## 如何开始 How it starts

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on ocean planets (level 1, weight 1).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.120“水下坟墓”：水下的地道网是一种很可能具有智慧的陆生节肢动物所筑的蚁丘群；行星变暖、冰盖融化后，它们被淹没灭绝。星球获得 watery_grave 修正和被淹没的蚁丘矿藏，记录标本。

distar.120 "Watery Grave": the tunnels are termite-like mounds of a probably sapient land-bound arthropod species, drowned when the warming planet melted its icecaps. The planet gains watery_grave and a flooded mounds deposit; a specimen is recorded.
