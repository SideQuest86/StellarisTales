## 剧情脉络 Story outline

特使爱上了第三方物种：选择向对方施压 / 以理服人 / 不管。选“以理服人”则15天后收到对方通牒 envoy_events.8，可交出或拒绝；拒绝后对方派出特种部队：63天后 11（67%）成功带走特使或 13（33%）失败被俘，再分别接 12 / 14。

The envoy falls for a third-party species: pressure them / appeal to reason / let it be. "Appeal to reason" brings the other party's transmission envoy_events.8 after 15 days, where one can hand the envoy over or refuse; after a refusal their special forces come: after 63 days 11 (67%) succeeds in taking the envoy or 13 (33%) fails and is captured, followed by 12 / 14.
