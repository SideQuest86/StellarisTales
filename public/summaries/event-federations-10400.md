# 措辞强烈的来信 / A Strongly Worded Letter

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 银河社群决议通过后，其 effect 调用对应的 federations.10xxx 事件，发给正处于违规的成员国；之后每十年 federations.10400（隐藏）检查是否仍违规，30+随机330天后发出 10401「措辞强硬的信」。
  When a Galactic Community resolution passes, its effect fires the matching federations.10xxx event at members in breach; every decade hidden federations.10400 checks for continuing breach and, 30 days + random 330 later, sends 10401 "A Strongly Worded Letter".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 各决议窗口并行；它们的“保留/拒绝”选项汇入同一封信。
  The resolution windows are parallel; their retain / refuse options converge on the same letter.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 选项取决于伦理（排外、威权、平等、灵性）和政体（奴役、机器同化者等）。
  Options depend on ethics (xenophobe, authoritarian, egalitarian, spiritualist) and civics (slavery, machine assimilator …).
