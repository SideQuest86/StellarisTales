# 黑夜生灵 / Life in Darkness

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：Shadows of the Shroud（has_shroud_dlc）

## 如何开始 How it starts

无矿藏、不是地形改造候选的荒芜或寒冷荒芜星球可能生成（2级，权重3；需要 has_shroud_dlc）。

Can spawn on a barren or cold barren planet with no deposit that is not a terraforming candidate (level 2, weight 3; needs has_shroud_dlc).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单一结果 extreme_frontiers.240“半透明演化”：三选一的选项由帝国类型决定——非杀戮帝国：每月社会研究加成与 d_society_5 矿藏；吞噬蜂群：每月食物加成与 d_food_5 矿藏；其他杀戮帝国：每月团结加成。

Single result extreme_frontiers.240 "Translucent Evolution": which option appears depends on the empire — non-homicidal: monthly society research and a d_society_5 deposit; Devouring Swarm: monthly food and a d_food_5 deposit; other homicidal empires: monthly unity.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

唯一结果；选项按杀戮状态与公民决定，互相排斥。

Single outcome; the options are mutually exclusive by homicidal status and civic.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

240 的描述与选项按杀戮帝国状态和“吞噬蜂群”公民变化。

240's description and options vary with homicidal status and the Devouring Swarm civic.
