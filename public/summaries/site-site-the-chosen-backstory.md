## 剧情脉络 Story outline

观测站里的数据水晶是先知伊蒂卡里奥斯留给后人的“夏瑞诺伊人纪事”，讲述这个好战民族为何自称“被选中者”，以及她如何打开虫洞并试图用护盾保护同胞。

1. 夏瑞诺伊博物馆：被改作博物馆的观测站和数据水晶（小型文物）
2. 民族志记录：第一块水晶：民族志记录
3. 转折点：第二块水晶：他们如何发现太空旅行并自认神授（中型文物）
4. 叛徒的诡计：伊蒂卡里奥斯揭示她打开虫洞的罪（三选一）

The data crystals are the Prophetess Ithikarios' "record of the Charynoi people": why the warlike Charynoi called themselves the Chosen, how she opened the Wormhole, and her attempt to shield her people.

1. The Charynoi Museum: the outpost turned museum, with data crystals (small artifact)
2. Ethnographic Records: the first crystal: ethnographic records
3. The Turning Point: the second crystal: how they found spaceflight and claimed it as a birthright (medium artifact)
4. The Traitor's Gambit: the Traitor's Gambit: Ithikarios confesses to opening the Wormhole (three choices)
