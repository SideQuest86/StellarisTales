## 如何开始 How it starts

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.125“杞人忧天”：那座站点曾是远超我们的粒子加速器。外星人相信宇宙处于“假真空”，高能实验可能引发真真空衰变，于是放弃了这项技术，退回简单生活，成为如今星球上的前超光速种族。奖励社会学与物理学研究，并给科学家（或研究节点）“粒子”专长。

distar.125 "Particular Fears": the station was a particle accelerator far beyond ours. Its builders came to believe the universe sits in a false vacuum that high-energy experiments could collapse, renounced the technology and regressed into the pre-FTL species now on the moon. Rewards society and physics research and a Particles expertise for the scientist (or research node).
