## 如何开始 How it starts

勘察天体时，隐藏事件 ancrel.9999（on_survey_planet）以 5/405 的机会生成一个随机考古遗址（AI帝国或5年内刚发现过遗址者机会×0.2），再按各遗址的权重抽取；本遗址要求拥有《远古遗物》，权重100，出现在沙漠、热带、干旱、大陆、海洋、苔原、极地、高山、草原、盖亚或遗迹星球上。全银河最多一处。

When a body is surveyed, hidden ancrel.9999 (on_survey_planet) has a 5/405 chance to create a random dig site (×0.2 for AI empires or if a site was found in the last 5 years), then picks a site type by weight; this site needs Ancient Relics and has weight 100 on desert, tropical, arid, continental, ocean, tundra, arctic, alpine, savannah, gaia or relic planets. At most one per galaxy.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

星球地壳里螺旋状的巨大空洞是一条史前巨型节肢动物的化石；一座前青铜时代的城市就建在它的消化道中。

1. 地下巨兽：化石是巨型节肢动物，体内嵌着人造物的痕迹
2. 失落城镇：体内是数十万身高一米的工具使用者的城市
3. 宿主：宿主与居民的关系：腐蚀性气体缩短了居民寿命（殖民科技、突击艇科技选项、社会学研究）

The giant spiralling hollows in the crust are the fossil of a huge arthropod, and a pre-Bronze-Age city once grew inside its digestive tract.

1. Subterranean Giant: a fossil arthropod with imprints of artificial components
2. Lost Boroughs: a city of hundreds of thousands of metre-tall tool users inside it
3. The Host: host and city-dwellers: the corrosive gases shortened their lives (colonisation tech, strike-craft tech option, society research)
