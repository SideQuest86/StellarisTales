# 野性呼唤 / Primal Calling

- 分类 Category：起源 Origins · DLC：Grand Archive

## 如何开始 How it starts

起源卡片描述一个与母星野生动物共生的文明，把目光投向太空中的生物（游牧版为方舟舰上携带这些生物）。开局隐藏事件 1000 在母星系补一颗气态巨行星、把恒星基地改为生物舱与虚空诱饵、给予“异种生物学”进度，并在 30 天后弹出 7010“虚空诱饵已开发”。
The card describes a civilisation bound to its homeworld's wildlife, now looking to the creatures of space (nomads carry them aboard their arkship). Hidden 1000 at game start adds a gas giant if needed, fits the starbase with a vivarium and a voidlure, grants Alien Life Studies progress, and shows 7010 *Voidlures Developed* 30 days later.

此后没有单一主线，而是几条由游戏状态各自触发的支线。
After that there is no single main line, but several branches each started by game state.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. **太空生物接触的三个里程碑**：每完成一次可捕获太空生物（提亚基、阿米巴、晶体、虚空虫、刺针虫）的首次接触，通用计数事件 200 都会为本起源调用 1001：
   **Three fauna-contact milestones**: after every first contact with a capturable fauna species, the shared counter 200 calls 1001 for this origin:
   - 第一种：30 天后 1002“繁星中的众生”，选择对太空生物的长期立场（观赏、牧养、庇护、狩猎；格式塔为同化、共生、进食）。
     First species: 1002 *Life in the Stars* after 30 days — choose a lasting stance (exhibit, exploit, shelter, hunt; gestalts assimilate, symbiosis, feed).
   - 第三种：1 天后 1060“腐肉”，在接触星系留下尸骸 → 解剖项目 → 1065 取得标本与科技进度。
     Third species: 1060 *Carrion* a day later leaves a carcass → autopsy project → 1065 (specimen and research progress).
   - 全部种类：180 天后出现一位传奇人物——猎手（1069 嗜杀版直接给标本；1070 → 1075 可招募）或研究者（1080 → 1085 可招募）。
     All species: 180 days later a paragon — the hunter (1069 homicidal version gives a specimen; 1070 → 1075 recruitable) or the researcher (1080 → 1085 recruitable).
2. **首次克隆**：首次建成太空生物舰船时 9970，给予“受控突变”进度。 **First cloning**: the first fauna ship built triggers 9970.
3. **泰坦巨兽殖民地**：勘察宜居行星时可能获得“泰坦巨兽”修正（1015）；这样的殖民地建立满一年后再过 30–120 天出现 1010，取代通用的同名殖民地事件。
   **Titanic Life**: surveying habitable worlds may add Titanic Life (1015); 30-120 days after such a colony's first anniversary, 1010 replaces the generic colony event.
4. **狂暴的虚空虫（中期）**：中期后、遇见全部太空生物时，年度检查触发 1100“野兽的锐鸣” → 180 天后 1105 找到隐藏星系 → 局势“狂暴的虚空虫”（进度0→300，每月+1，选择应对方式再+3；应对方式改变敌意）。
   **The frenzied voidworms (mid-game)**: once mid-game has begun and every species has been met, a yearly check fires 1100 → 1105 after 180 days finds a hidden system → situation *Frenzied Voidworms* (0→300, +1 monthly, +3 with an approach; approaches change hostility).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 局势完成时按累积敌意：安抚 1220（虫群舰队归你，获得遗珍）或攻击 1200（虫群可轰炸你的星球）。
  At completion, accumulated hostility decides: pacified 1220 (the fleets join you, relic) or attack 1200 (they may bombard your worlds).
- 局势中由你摧毁巢穴 → 1165（中止局势）；他国摧毁 → 你看到 1185、对方看到 1180。
  You destroy the nest mid-situation → 1165 (aborts it); another empire does → you see 1185, they see 1180.
- 攻击之后：轰炸使行星毁灭 → 1205；之后摧毁巢穴 → 1210（复仇）；未失去星球时摧毁巢穴 → 1170。
  After the attack: a planet shattered by bombardment → 1205, then destroying the nest → 1210 (revenge); destroying it before any world is lost → 1170.
- 除被他国摧毁外，各结局都给予“变异虚空虫巢穴”遗珍。 Every ending except the third-party kill gives you the Mutated Voidworm Nest relic.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 1002 的立场选项受思潮限制：庇护需亲外或和平，狩猎需排外或尚武；格式塔另有三项；吞噬虫群不能选同化/共生。
  1002 stances are ethic-gated: shelter needs xenophile or pacifist, hunt needs xenophobe or militarist; gestalts have three others; Devouring Swarm cannot pick assimilate/symbiosis.
- 嗜杀帝国必得猎手；其余按已猎杀种类数（少于2 研究者、多于2 猎手、恰为2 各半）。
  Homicidal empires always get the hunter; others by hunted species (<2 researcher, >2 hunter, exactly 2 half and half).
- 中期起每五年隐藏事件 1005 会在边境生成一种尚未接触的太空生物，便于集齐接触；若生成的是虚空虫且银河中尚无虚空虫国家，会启动通用的“虚空虫行为”局势（独立故事，链接）。
  From mid-game a hidden five-year check (1005) spawns a not-yet-contacted fauna near the border; if that creates the voidworm country, the generic Voidworms Behavior situation starts (a separate, linked story).
