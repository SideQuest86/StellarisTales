# 暗物质爆发 / Dark Matter Eruption

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/strange_worlds_events.txt:1521-1850; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3733 on_colony_1_year_old (random 20/510) · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满1年时随机事件池以20/510抽中 strange_worlds.125（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3733）；1080天后 strange_worlds.130。触发前提：BioGenesis DLC，人类玩家的非首都、非母星、仍由原建立者持有的人造以外殖民星球，没有地面战斗，也未被占领；并且该殖民地尚未触发过其他“奇异世界”事件（colony_event 旗标），每个国家每条故事只触发一次。

A colony turning 1 year old can draw strange_worlds.125 (20/510, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3733); strange_worlds.130 follows after 1080 days. Preconditions: BioGenesis DLC, a human player's non-capital, non-homeworld, original-owner, non-artificial colony without ground combat or occupation, which has not yet had another Strange Worlds event (colony_event flag); once per country per chain.
