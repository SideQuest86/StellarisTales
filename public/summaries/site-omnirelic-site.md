## 剧情脉络 Story outline

覆盖整颗行星的废弃巨型城市曾是机器帝国“有机全典”（Omnicodex）的首都；他们的行星数据中心里保存着一台能由基本元素复原所有被采样种族的装置——“有机全典”。

1. 废弃星球：遍布全星球的破败建筑，需要详细地表调查（小型文物）
2. 废弃星球：线索指向行星数据处理中心（工程学与社会学研究）
3. 废弃星球：重新启动数据中心，得知这里是机器帝国“有机全典”的首都（小型文物）
4. 废弃星球：有机全典出土：获得遗珍“有机全典”与大型文物

A planet-wide fallen megalopolis was the capital of the machine empire "the Omnicodex"; its planetary data centre holds the device that can recreate every race the Omni sampled — the Omnicodex.

1. Ruined World: dilapidated structures across the entire planet (small artifact)
2. Ruined World: clues lead to a planetary data centre (engineering and society research)
3. Ruined World: the data centre reactivates; this was the capital of the machine empire Omnicodex (small artifact)
4. Ruined World: the Omnicodex unearthed: the relic r_omnicodex and a large artifact
