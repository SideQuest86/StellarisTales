# 残毁行星 / Ruined Planet

- 分类 Category：星界裂隙 Astral rifts (C4) · DLC：星界位面 Astral Planes · 起源 Origin：裂隙世界 Riftworld
- 来源 Sources：`common/astral_rifts/00_astral_rifts.txt:1`；`events/astral_rifts_0_events.txt:11–1237`（1–57）；`events/origin_events_astral_planes.txt:12–65`（生成 spawn）
- 审阅 Reviewed：2026-10-12（全篇 whole story；原独立的失败事件 30 已并入 / former standalone failure event 30 merged in）

## 如何开始 How it starts

这道裂隙不在随机裂隙池中：只有“裂隙世界”起源在开局时于母星系生成它，并在裂隙旁建好研究站（`origin_events_astral_planes.txt:28–40`）。帝国取得裂隙探球科技后即可派科学家进入。第一章 1 难度为 0；每阶段结束掷 1–10 的骰子，加技能与线索、减难度，≥14 取得突破。

This rift is not in the random pool: only the Riftworld origin creates it at game start in the home system, with a research station beside it (`origin_events_astral_planes.txt:28–40`). Once the empire has the Rift Sphere technology a scientist can enter. Chapter 1 has difficulty 0; each phase ends with a d10 roll plus skill and clues minus difficulty, and 14 or more is a breakthrough.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 残毁行星（1）：裂隙通向一颗战火中的星球，居民说着本帝国语言的古老版本——他们是本族的先祖，正在输给被称作“侵略者”的异次元敌人。
2. 相反派系（3）：先拜访军国主义的“星光先锋”或宗教性的“黑幕派”（灭绝主义帝国不能选后者）。
3. 失落先祖（5）：先祖讲述“救生艇”行动——用一座只能单程的裂隙探球把一部分人送往另一侧，那就是本帝国的起源。
4. 拜访派系：在先访问的一派（星光先锋 10 / 黑幕派 15）处可以提供援助、转去另一派一次，或在第二派处放弃（16）。星光先锋要合金（100）或星界丝缕（100）；黑幕派要食物（岩石帝国为矿物、合成帝国为能量，1000）或星界丝缕（100）。用星界丝缕会使下一章难度 +2，改道去另一派难度 −2。
5. 世界议会（20）：最后之战前，可再投入星界丝缕（100）、能量（1000）或什么都不给；前两者使下一章难度 −3。
6. 结果互斥：突破则先祖击退侵略者（25 欢庆）；若该阶段任一次掷骰失败（基础 1.5%/次），则改为 30“荒废”。

1. Ruined Planet (1): the rift opens on a war-torn world whose people speak an archaic form of the empire's own language — the empire's ancestors, losing a war against extradimensional “Invaders”.
2. Opposing Factions (3): visit the militarist Starlight Vanguard or the priestly Black Curtain first (exterminator empires cannot choose the latter).
3. Lost Ancestors (5): the ancestors describe Operation “Life Boat”, a one-way Rift Sphere that carried a group through the rift — the origin of the empire.
4. Faction visits: at the first faction (Vanguard 10 / Curtain 15) the player can give aid, switch to the other faction once, or at the second faction abandon the ancestors (16). The Vanguard asks for alloys (100) or astral threads (100); the Curtain for food (minerals for lithoid, energy for synthetic empires, 1000) or astral threads (100). Paying threads raises the next chapter's difficulty by 2; switching factions lowers it by 2.
5. World Council (20): before the final battle, offer astral threads (100), energy (1000) or nothing; the first two lower the next chapter's difficulty by 3.
6. Exclusive outcome: a breakthrough means the ancestors repel the Invaders (25, Celebration); if any roll of that stage fails (base 1.5% per roll) the result is 30, Devastation.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 16 抛弃先祖：探索结束，获得大量星界丝缕与凝聚力；360 天后接怪异通讯。
- 25 欢庆：选择带回一位带“裂隙扭曲”特质的科学官、指挥官或行政官；探索结束，立即“先祖之魂”（40），360 天后怪异通讯。
- 30 荒废（失败分支）：带走难民（凝聚力）、接受贵族报酬（能量并获得一名行政官）、保存档案或索取知识（多类研究与凝聚力）；探索结束，随后“先祖之魂”与 360 天后的怪异通讯。
- 40 先祖之魂：按援助过的派系与世界议会上的投入给予不同的永久修正。
- 50 → 55 → 56 → 57 怪异通讯：裂隙彼端的声音称本帝国为“裂隙之子”，说正是它们打开了那扇门，并将很快找到你们；可在 55/56 关闭通讯。这是无形异使线索的伏笔（见“水晶球体”篇）。

- 16 Abandoning Our Ancestors: the exploration ends with astral threads and unity; the strange transmission follows 360 days later.
- 25 Celebration: bring back a Rift Warped scientist, commander or official; the exploration ends, Spirit of our Ancestors (40) follows at once and the strange transmission after 360 days.
- 30 Devastation (failure branch): take refugees (unity), accept a noble's offer (energy and an official leader), preserve the library or claim the knowledge (research and unity); the exploration ends, then Spirit of our Ancestors and the transmission.
- 40 Spirit of our Ancestors: a permanent modifier chosen by which faction was aided and what was offered at the council.
- 50 → 55 → 56 → 57 Strange Transmission: a voice from beyond the rifts calls the empire “children of rifts”, claims to have opened the door, and says it will find them soon; the player can close the channel at 55/56. Commentary: this foreshadows the Formless (see “The Crystal Sphere”).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 1、5、55 的文字随裂隙世界起源的考古遗址是否完成而变；3、5、20、25 有蜂巢/机械/格式塔版本；15 有岩石/合成版本；16、30 有灭绝主义版本；40 按格式塔、吞噬虫群、终结者、非友好帝国变化。
- 派系名：一般为“星光先锋”，灭绝主义帝国为“星光征服者”（`common/scripted_loc/09_scripted_loc_astral_planes.txt:210`）。
- 原文差异：5 中英文说裂隙这一侧的探球发射“不到五十年前”，中文作“短短的15年后”。

- 1, 5 and 55 change if the Riftworld arc site has been completed; 3, 5, 20 and 25 have hive/machine/gestalt versions; 15 has lithoid/synthetic versions; 16 and 30 have exterminator versions; 40 varies for gestalt, Devouring Swarm, Terminator and unfriendly empires.
- Faction name: “Starlight Vanguard”, or “Starlight Vanquishers” for exterminator empires (`common/scripted_loc/09_scripted_loc_astral_planes.txt:210`).
- Localisation difference: in 5 the English says the Sphere was launched “less than fifty years ago” on this side; the Chinese reads “15 years later”.
