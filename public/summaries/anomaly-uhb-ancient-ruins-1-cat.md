# 碑塔 / Monoliths

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration (C3)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 3级异常，权重3；非恒星、非小行星、非环世界的不可居住行星（气态巨行星除外）。  
  Level-3 anomaly, weight 3; uninhabitable non-star, non-asteroid, non-ringworld planets (gas giants excluded).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 单一结局120“埃迪克式独石”（研究）：a得工程研究并在30天后触发联络报告story.5（枢纽）；b（仅限不排外、不亲外、群体意识帝国）2天后story.5，并按非亲外人口数（>1200或>600）设旗标eddic_1/2，4天后触发121。  
  Single outcome 120 (research): a gives engineering research and fires story.5 after 30 days (hub); b (only for non-xenophobe, non-xenophile gestalt empires per the trigger) fires story.5 after 2 days and, by non-xenophile pop count (>1200 or >600), sets eddic_1/2 and fires 121 after 4 days.
- 121：按旗标发放6次或3次亲外伦理偏移。  
  121: shifts 6 or 3 pop groups toward xenophile depending on the flag.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 线性；121仅在人口门槛满足时出现。  
  Linear; 121 only occurs when the pop thresholds are met.
