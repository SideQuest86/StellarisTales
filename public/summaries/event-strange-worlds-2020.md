# 致命变异 / Fatal Mutations

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/strange_worlds_events.txt:2883-3023; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2739 on_tech_increased · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

研究“通用巨噬细胞”科技（tech_universal_marcophage）时（on_tech_increased，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2739），隐藏事件 strange_worlds.2020 在尚未基因飞升、非演化捕食者起源的默认国家上，1800–5400天后排程 strange_worlds.2025，后者在有有机人口的非首都星球上触发 strange_worlds.2030。

When the Universal Macrophage technology (tech_universal_marcophage) advances (on_tech_increased, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2739) hidden strange_worlds.2020 - for default countries that are not genetically ascended and not Evolutionary Predators - schedules strange_worlds.2025 after 1800-5400 days, which fires strange_worlds.2030 on a non-capital planet with organic pops.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

strange_worlds.2030“致命变异”：社会研究奖励；所有有机物种获得 trait_geleboric_mutations（立绘变为类毒素/岩石类），并按人口量杀死部分人口。

strange_worlds.2030 "Fatal Mutations": a society research reward; every organic species gains trait_geleboric_mutations (portrait switched to a toxoid or lithoid look) and part of the population dies.
