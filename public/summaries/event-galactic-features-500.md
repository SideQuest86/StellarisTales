# 繁育之所 / Breeding Grounds

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

500：首次进入太空变形虫的巢穴系统（标记 amoeba_home_system，有 amoeba_garrison 舰队）；502：变形虫群离开巢穴到达本国领土时（hidden 501 → 502）；505：银河共同体通过“太空变形虫保护法案”时。

500: first entering the Space Amoeba home system (flag amoeba_home_system with an amoeba_garrison fleet); 502: when an amoeba wave leaves home and reaches the empire's space (hidden 501 -> 502); 505: when the Galactic Community passes the Space Amoeba Protection Act.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

500 / 502 都是警告（选项“战斗岗位！”，若已有 pacified_amoebas 修正则是“有趣”）；505 在决议通过后让玩家选择遵守（获得永久的 pacified_amoebas 修正，并使所有变形虫国家中立）或拒绝（视为害虫）。

500 and 502 are warnings (option "Battlestations!", or "Interesting" with the pacified_amoebas modifier); 505 after the resolution lets the player comply (permanent pacified_amoebas modifier, all amoeba countries become neutral) or refuse (treat them as pests).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

遵守 / 拒绝法案。

Comply / refuse the act.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

正文依是否与变形虫国家有通讯、是否已安抚而异。

The text differs by contact with the amoeba countries and by pacification.
