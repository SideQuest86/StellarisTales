# 波涛之下 / From Beneath the Waves

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 殖民地2岁（15/958）或3岁（10/739）时的随机事件；需已掌握驱逐舰科技，非荒野帝国，海洋/大陆/热带/盖亚殖民地（非火山）；每个帝国一次（colony_submerged_cruiser）。
  Random colony event at age 2 (15/958) or 3 (10/739); needs destroyer tech, a non-wilderness empire and an ocean/continental/tropical/Gaia colony (not volcanic); once per empire (colony_submerged_cruiser).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 2005「来自水下」：选项a打捞（启用 ANCIENT_OCEAN_SHIP 项目）；选项b拆解取得驱逐舰或巡洋舰科技进度（已有驱逐舰则给巡洋舰）；拾荒者帝国得到专属版本，同时得到项目与科技。
  2005 "From Beneath the Waves": option a salvages (enables the ANCIENT_OCEAN_SHIP project); option b scraps the ship for destroyer (or cruiser, if destroyers are known) tech progress; scavenger empires get their own variants with both project and tech.
- 项目成功后 50% → 2006（舰队或奖励，选项随伦理而异）、30% → 2007、其余（受修正的20%）→ 2008「项目失败」。
  On project success 50% -> 2006 (fleet or reward; options vary by ethics), 30% -> 2007, the rest (modified 20%) -> 2008 "Project Failed".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 2006/2007/2008 互斥。
  2006/2007/2008 are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 2006 的选项按物质主义/信仰主义而异。
  2006 options differ for materialists and spiritualists.
