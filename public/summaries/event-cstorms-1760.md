# 宇宙阻塞 / Cosmic Clogging

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

天体风暴进入拥有殖民地的星系时（on_storm_entered_system，00_on_actions.txt:5412），cstorms.1760 在该星系随机一个非人造殖民星球上排程，90天后弹出 cstorms.1761（每国一次，机械/非机器物种限制见触发）。

When a celestial storm enters a system with a colony (on_storm_entered_system, 00_on_actions.txt:5412) cstorms.1760 picks one non-artificial colony and, 90 days later, shows cstorms.1761 (once per country; the trigger excludes robotic species).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

非岩石种帝国：选a获得“饱和过滤”修正并启用“清理管道”项目，完成后 cstorms.1765 给过滤洞察修正并推进一项农业科技。

Non-lithoid empires: option a adds the "saturated filtration" modifier and enables the "Clear the Pipes" project; when finished cstorms.1765 grants a filtration insight modifier and progress on a farming tech.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

岩石（Lithoid）帝国则看到另一版正文，只有选项b：直接获得矿物与团结，无项目。

Lithoid empires see another text and only option b: minerals and unity at once, no project.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

Cosmic Storms DLC；触发需要星球未受其他风暴事件影响。

Cosmic Storms DLC; the planet must not already be affected by another storm event.
