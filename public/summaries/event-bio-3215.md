# 基因取乐 / Genetic Entertainment

## 如何开始 How it starts

“生长”碎片存在、我方和平、有有机人口且不是荒野帝国时，五年随机脉冲可能抽中 bio.3215，立即弹出 bio.3220。触发后设置2560天的蜂巢失落帝国事件冷却（hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown），期间其他碎片要求不再出现。

With the Growth fragment present, our empire at peace, with organic pops and not a Wilderness empire, the five-year pulse can pick bio.3215, which shows bio.3220 at once. Firing sets a 2560-day cooldown (hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown) shared by the fragment demands.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

生长碎片嫌我方境内某个物种“乏味”，提出三种改造方式供选。

The Growth fragment finds one of our species "boring" and offers three ways to modify it.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

随机生成三种表型改造选项（共14类），接受任一会给该物种一个对应表型的随机特质并取悦碎片；拒绝则使其不满。进化掠食者起源若目标为本物种，另有推进“适应性进化”情势50%的选项。

Three of fourteen phenotype modifications are offered at random; any of them gives that species a random trait of the phenotype and pleases the fragment; refusing displeases it. Evolutionary Predators targeting their own species get an option adding 50% to the Adaptive Evolution situation.
