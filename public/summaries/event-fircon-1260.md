## 如何开始 How it starts

- 被观测的前超光速文明的“科技进步”局势每月有 5/100 的机会运行 preftl.50（之后 1800 天冷却），从每月观测事件池中按权重抽取一个条件满足的事件；本事件权重 100。
  The observed pre-FTL civilization's tech-progress situation has a 5/100 monthly chance to run preftl.50 (then an 1800-day cooldown), which draws one valid event from the monthly observation pool by weight; this event has weight 100.
- 条件：石器时代、被动观察（或游牧中继站观察者）；每个前超光速文明一次。
  Conditions: Stone Age, Passive Observation (or nomad waystation observer); once per pre-FTL civilization.
- 隐藏分派事件设置一次性标记，然后在 0–25 天后向观测哨站拥有者（及游牧中继站观察者）发出可见事件。
  The hidden dispatcher sets a once-only flag and sends the visible event to the observation post owner (and nomad waystation observers) after 0-25 days.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 1261「饥荒！」：原始聚落歉收，冲突一触即发。
  1261 "Famine!": the settlements suffer crop failures and conflict looms.
- 不干涉；或“我们有道义援助”（需 50 食物，岩石生命则 50 矿物）→ 360 天后 1262「饥荒结束了！」，取回部分余粮；或（吞噬虫群、食尸者、进化掠食者且不重视他者）“减少他们对食物的需求”。
  Do not interfere; or "moral obligation to help" (needs 50 food, or 50 minerals for lithoids) → 360 days later 1262 "The Famine is Over!" and we take some surplus; or (Devouring Swarm, Necrophage or Evolutionary Predators that do not value others) "reduce their need for food".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 三个选项互斥；只有援助会引出 1262。
  The options are exclusive; only helping leads to 1262.
- 这是随机观测插曲：只在满足条件时可能出现，彼此之间没有固定顺序。
  This is a random observation interlude: it may appear only when its conditions hold, with no fixed order relative to other interludes.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 第三个选项只对吞噬虫群、食尸者起源或进化掠食者起源（且不重视他者）可见。
  The third option is only for Devouring Swarm, Necrophage or Evolutionary Predators empires that do not value others.
