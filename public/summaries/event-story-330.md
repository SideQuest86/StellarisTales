# 古代矿石处理中心 / Ancient Mineral Processing Hub

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/story_events.txt:2800-3000; common/relics/relics.txt:360-403; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:989,1628 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

舰队进入古代采矿无人机的母系统（drone_home_system 旗标，已与无人机国家通讯，每个国家一次）时（on_entering_system_fleet，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:989），story.330 弹出。

A fleet entering the Ancient Mining Drones' home system (star flag drone_home_system, in contact with the drone country, once per country; on_entering_system_fleet, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:989) shows story.330.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

story.330“古代矿石处理中心”：描述这是银河系里最后的大型采矿加工中心。摧毁无人机基地（在 on_fleet_destroyed_perp，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:1628，击毁带 ore_grinder 旗标的舰队）后 story.332“已摧毁采矿无人机基地”：获得4000矿物与500合金；若拥有 Ancient Relics DLC，则另得遗珍“勘探器”（r_surveyor）。激活该遗珍（relics.txt:373-394）时 story.335“勘探器已激活”：在一颗星球上发现新的资源矿点，并有50%概率720±360天后、20%概率1440±360天后再来一次。

story.330 "Ancient Mineral Processing Hub": the last major mining and ore-processing hub in the galaxy. Destroying the drone base (on_fleet_destroyed_perp, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:1628, an ore_grinder fleet) shows story.332 "Mining Drone Base Destroyed": 4000 minerals and 500 alloys, plus the Surveyor relic (r_surveyor) with the Ancient Relics DLC. Activating the relic (relics.txt:373-394) shows story.335 "Surveyor Activated": a new resource deposit is found on a planet, with a 50% chance of another after 720+-360 days and 20% after 1440+-360 days.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

story.332 的两个选项互斥：无 Ancient Relics 时选 a（只给资源），有时选 b（资源加遗珍）。

The two options of story.332 are exclusive: without Ancient Relics a (resources only), with it b (resources and the relic).
