## 如何开始 How it starts

- 5级异常；在墓穴世界（未被标记禁用核爆世界异常的星球）上以权重3生成；格式塔帝国与拥有“纪念主义者”公民性的帝国生成权重为0。  
  Level-5 anomaly; spawn weight 3 on tomb worlds without the nuked_planet_anomalies_disabled flag; weight 0 for gestalt empires and empires with the Memorialist civic.
- 研究成功只有一个结果：4155“战场之下”。  
  Success has a single outcome: 4155 "Beneath the Battlefield".

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 4155：山下发现地下避难所，周围有野兽。可开启项目“地下避难所”（需3艘军舰、100天）；唯心主义帝国也可选择不打扰而获得影响力。  
  4155: a vault beneath the mountains, guarded by beasts. Start "Underground Vault" (3 military ships, 100 days); spiritualists may leave it be for influence.
- 项目完成：70% 4156（进入避难所，找到一个容器），30% anomaly_failure.4156（避难所坍塌，一无所获）。  
  Project result: 70% 4156 (a container is found), 30% anomaly_failure.4156 (the vault collapses).
- 4156：把容器送回母星（500工程学项目“撬开它”）或只研究避难所（影响力）。  
  4156: send the container home (500 engineering project "Cracking it Open") or only study the vault (influence).
- “撬开它”完成：40% 4157 冷冻舱中苏醒的外星将军马朱拉，30% anomaly_failure.4157 作物样本（下一项地形改造科技），30% anomaly_failure.4158 生物武器（帝国获“生育抑制生物武器”修正3600天）。  
  "Cracking it Open": 40% 4157 the cryopod warrior Majula awakens, 30% anomaly_failure.4157 crop samples (next terraforming tech), 30% anomaly_failure.4158 a bio-weapon (Fertility Inhibiting Bio-Weapon modifier for 3600 days).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 4157 的回答决定下一窗口：亲外/狂热唯心/和平（且非尚武、非狂热唯物）可说“你很安全”（设suspected_angels）；人人可说“你的母星已毁”；排外/尚武/狂热平等/狂热唯物可说“我们打捞了残骸”（设suspected_assholes）→ 4159。前两者 → 4158。  
  4157's reply decides the next window: xenophile/fanatic spiritualist/pacifist empires may reassure her (suspected_angels); anyone may say her world is ruined; xenophobe/militarist/fanatic egalitarian/fanatic materialist may say "we salvaged it" (suspected_assholes) → 4159; the first two → 4158.
- 4158/4159 是同一组同窗问答（关于她自己、为何被保存、她的人民），然后“你愿意加入吗？”按思潮与上述标志分为：4161 拒绝并回到母星、4160 加入（成为5级指挥官“持守者马朱拉”）、4162 挟持研究员逃跑被击毙（失去一名非研究主管的科学家）；或直接“离开吧”→ 4163 告别。  
  4158/4159 are the same in-window Q&A; then "Will you join us?" resolves by ethics and the flags into 4161 refusal, 4160 she joins (a skill-5 commander "Majula the Bearer"), or 4162 she takes a researcher hostage and is shot (a non-head scientist dies); or "Leave" → 4163 farewell.
