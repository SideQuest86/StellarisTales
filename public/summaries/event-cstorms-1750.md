## 如何开始 How it starts

电、粒子、引力、磁、太阳或天体风暴离开拥有寒冷殖民地的星系时（00_on_actions.txt:5437），隐藏事件 cstorms.1750 标记该星球，90天后 cstorms.1751 确认并弹出 cstorms.1755。

When an electric, particle, gravity, magnetic, solar or celestial storm leaves a system with a cold colony (00_on_actions.txt:5437) hidden cstorms.1750 marks the planet and, 90 days later, cstorms.1751 confirms and shows cstorms.1755.
