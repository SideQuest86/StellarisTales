- 分类 Category：起源 Origins · DLC：Federations

## 如何开始 How it starts

起始殖民地为破碎之环的可居住段（pc_shattered_ring_habitable）；电力、采矿、农业区不设上限，采矿区 +2。开局隐藏事件 origin.3150 清理废弃环世界并在2700 / 5400天后生成可保证的殖民地（origin.3155 为AI补建殖民地）。
The homeworld is a habitable ring segment; energy, mining and farming districts are uncapped with +2 mining. Hidden origin.3150 removes the ruined ring and spawns the guaranteed colonies after 2700 / 5400 days; hidden origin.3155 fills AI colonies.
