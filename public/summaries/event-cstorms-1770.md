# 不飞之羽 / Flightless Fauna

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

引力风暴进入拥有殖民地的星系时（on_storm_entered_system，00_on_actions.txt:5413），cstorms.1770 选中一个星球，90天后 cstorms.1771“不飞之羽”（每国一次，需星系仍在引力风暴中）。

When a gravity storm enters a system with a colony (on_storm_entered_system, 00_on_actions.txt:5413) cstorms.1770 picks a planet and, 90 days later, cstorms.1771 "Flightless Fauna" appears (once per country, the system must still be in a gravity storm).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

四个互斥选项取决于帝国：蜂群吞噬者（a）获得食物，演化捕食者起源再得进度；其他帝国（b）获得社会研究，或（c，需350矿物且非演化捕食者）救下这些鸟类并获得团结；演化捕食者起源（evopred）得到DNA进度。选c之后，下一次引力风暴离开该星系时 cstorms.1775 在360天后弹出 cstorms.1776“再次飞翔”，星球获得 d_avian_reserve 矿点。

Four exclusive options depending on the empire: Devouring Swarm hives (a) gain food (Evolutionary Predators also DNA progress); other empires (b) gain society research or (c, 350 minerals, not Evolutionary Predators) save the birds and gain unity; Evolutionary Predators (evopred) get DNA progress. After option c the next gravity storm leaving the system leads, 360 days later, to cstorms.1776 "Airborne Again", and the planet gets the d_avian_reserve deposit.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

只有选项c会引出后续 cstorms.1776。

Only option c leads on to cstorms.1776.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

Cosmic Storms DLC；蜂群吞噬者与演化捕食者有专属选项。

Cosmic Storms DLC; Devouring Swarm and Evolutionary Predators have their own options.
