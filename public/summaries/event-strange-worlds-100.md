# 突然消失 / Sudden Disappearance

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/strange_worlds_events.txt:1186-1520; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3732 on_colony_1_year_old (random 20/510) · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满1年时随机事件池以20/510抽中 strange_worlds.100（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3732），星球不能是火山类；30+1050天后 strange_worlds.105。触发前提：BioGenesis DLC，人类玩家的非首都、非母星、仍由原建立者持有的人造以外殖民星球，没有地面战斗，也未被占领；并且该殖民地尚未触发过其他“奇异世界”事件（colony_event 旗标），每个国家每条故事只触发一次。

A colony turning 1 year old can draw strange_worlds.100 (20/510, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3732) on a non-volcanic planet; strange_worlds.105 follows after 30+1050 days. Preconditions: BioGenesis DLC, a human player's non-capital, non-homeworld, original-owner, non-artificial colony without ground combat or occupation, which has not yet had another Strange Worlds event (colony_event flag); once per country per chain.

strange_worlds.105“突然消失”（失联的探险节点）→ 180±180天后 strange_worlds.110“腐蚀池塘” → 360±180天后 strange_worlds.115“消化泥沼”：a“培育这些生物”（沼泽植物成为资源）；b“改造这片土地”——360±180天后 strange_worlds.120“沼泽植物威胁解除”（肥沃土地；演化捕食者可再收获）。

strange_worlds.105 "Sudden Disappearance" (a lost exploration node) -> 180+-180 days later strange_worlds.110 "Caustic Pools" -> 360+-180 days later strange_worlds.115 "Digestive Marsh": a "Cultivate these organisms" (the bog plants become a resource); b "Repurpose the land" - 360+-180 days later strange_worlds.120 "Bogplants Neutralized" (fertile land; Evolutionary Predators can harvest the remains).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

115 的 a 与 b 互斥；只有 b 通向 120。

In 115, a and b are exclusive; only b leads on to 120.
