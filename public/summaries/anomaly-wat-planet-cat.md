## 如何开始 How it starts

- 3级异常；在海洋行星上以权重3生成，仅限非格式塔且未持禁止干涉政策的帝国。  
  Level-3 anomaly; spawn weight 3 on ocean planets for non-gestalt empires without the interference_not_allowed policy.
- 约30天后另有一份通用联络报告（story.1–8，共享枢纽故事），不属于本故事。  
  About 30 days later a generic contact report (story.1–8, a shared hub story) follows; it is not part of this story.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 4045“深处的回声”：海底深处的微弱“回声”，船员疲惫。选项取决于科学家特质：  
  4045 "Echoes from the Deep": a faint echo far below; the crew is exhausted. Options depend on the scientist:
- 细致或天才火花的科学家：校准扫描仪 → 4046 发现覆盖全球、具灵能的浮游意识（行星获“远古之物”资源点与敌对环境，负面舆论）。可尝试接触 → 4047（接受“邀请”，敌对环境移除）；下毒 → 4048（行星变为有毒世界，+4社会学）；或不予理会。  
  Meticulous or spark-of-genius scientist: recalibrate → 4046 a planet-wide psionic plankton mind (Ancient One deposit, hostile environment, negative press). Contact it → 4047 (an "invitation"; hostile environment removed), poison it → 4048 (planet turns toxic, +4 society), or leave it.
- 偏执的科学家：私自下潜 → 4049 科学家获得“狂躁”特质；让其休息 → 7天后 4050 获得灵能专长；或关押（科学家被移除）。  
  Paranoid scientist: an unauthorised dive → 4049 (Maniacal trait); let them rest → 4050 after 7 days (psionics expertise), or confine them (the scientist is removed).
- 任何科学家：船员健康优先 → 4051（正面舆论、休息良好修正，首次解锁“改善工作环境”法令）；或不眠不休 → 4052（发现新的海底节肢生物，医药创新修正，负面舆论）。  
  Any scientist: crew health first → 4051 (positive press, Well Rested Scientists; unlocks the Improved Working Environment edict the first time); or push on → 4052 (a new arthropod; Pharmaceutical Innovation, negative press).
