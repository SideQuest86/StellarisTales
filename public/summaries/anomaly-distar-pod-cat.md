# 不明吊舱 / Unidentified Drop Pods

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

《遥远繁星》专属星系 distar_pod_system（守护者星系，双星，每局至多一个）中，气态巨行星周围漂着成群吊舱，初始化在其上放置3级异常。

In the Distant Stars system distar_pod_system (the Caretaker system, binary, at most one) a halo of drop pods surrounds a gas giant, which gets this level-3 anomaly from the initializer.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.245“活动迹象”：吊舱里的乘客早已死去，只有一个吊舱仍有活动，可以呼叫它（或没时间）。
2. distar.246“守护者”：吊舱的守护机器人 AX7-b 负责照料移民，超空间故障让吊舱偏离目的地，乘客在空气耗尽后全部死亡。可提问（只显示回答）；欢迎它（作为领袖招募）；分析其部件 → distar.247；禁止人工智能的帝国摧毁它、机械智能拆解它 → distar.248。

1. distar.245 "Signs of Activity": the pods' passengers died long ago; one pod still shows activity and can be hailed (or ignored).
2. distar.246 "The Caretaker": its caretaker robot AX7-b looked after the settlers; a hyperdrive fault stranded the pods and everyone died when the air ran out. Questions only show replies; welcome it (recruited as a leader); analyse its parts → distar.247; AI-outlawing empires destroy it and machine intelligences dismantle it → distar.248.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.246 的结局互斥：招募、分析（247）、摧毁/拆解（248）。

distar.246's outcomes are exclusive: recruit, analyse (247), destroy/dismantle (248).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

“分析部件”不对机械智能和禁止人工智能的帝国开放；distar.248 正文按拆解或摧毁二选一。

"Analyse its parts" is closed to machine intelligences and AI-outlawing empires; distar.248 has a dismantle and a destroy text.
