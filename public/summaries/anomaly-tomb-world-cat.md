# 死寂星球 / Tomb World

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。有毒、核爆、荒芜或冷荒芜星球（未禁用核爆星球异常）可能生成（4级，权重3）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on toxic, nuked, barren or cold barren planets (unless nuked-planet anomalies are disabled) (level 4, weight 3).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

结果由星球类型决定（基础0）：
- 有毒/核爆 → distar.2005“武器测试”：星球被各种武器反复轰炸；分析样本 → WEAPONS_TESTING（60天）→ distar.2006“外星激光”：按尚缺的科技推进激光或装甲科技，全都有时只得到研究资源。
- 荒芜/冷荒芜（有站点矿藏时为0）→ distar.2015“原始之汤”：适合无生源论研究的有机“原汤”（社会学矿藏）；终结者机械智能则选择把它烧掉（凝聚力）。

The result depends on planet class (base 0):
- toxic/nuked → distar.2005 "Weapon Testing": the planet was bombarded by every kind of weapon; analyse samples → WEAPONS_TESTING (60 days) → distar.2006 "Alien Lasers": progress toward missing laser or armour techs, or research only if all are known.
- barren/cold barren (0 with station deposits) → distar.2015 "Primordial Soup": an organic soup that may lead to abiogenesis (society deposit); Terminator machines burn it instead (unity).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

两种结果按星球类型互斥。

The two results are exclusive by planet class.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.2015 有终结者专属正文与机械选项，演化猎手有专属选项；distar.2006 有“自愧不如/再次确认优越”两种文本。

distar.2015 has a Terminator text and machine option plus an Evolutionary Predators option; distar.2006 has an inferior/superior text pair.
