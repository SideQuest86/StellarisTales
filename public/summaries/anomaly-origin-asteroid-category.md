# 未知起源 / Unknown Origin

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

小行星可能生成（1级，权重3）。

Can spawn on an asteroid (level 1, weight 3).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

anomaly.1“粪化石小行星”（标本 coprolite，d_society_3 矿藏；星球有战略资源时权重为0）、anomaly.15“小行星化石”（每月社会研究，标本 asteroid_fossils；演化掠食者另有 DNA 选项）与 distar.225“垃圾球”（《遥远繁星》：基础权重0，拥有 DLC 时 +1；星球有战略资源时权重为0；合金矿藏，上古遗物时10个小型遗物，pop_lcluster_event）。

anomaly.1 "Coprolite Asteroid" (specimen coprolite, d_society_3 deposit; weight 0 if the planet has a strategic resource), anomaly.15 "Asteroid Fossils" (monthly society research, specimen asteroid_fossils; Evolutionary Predators get an extra DNA option) and distar.225 "Junk Ball" (Distant Stars: base weight 0, +1 with the DLC; weight 0 if the planet has a strategic resource; alloy deposit, 10 minor artifacts with Ancient Relics, pop_lcluster_event).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

三个结果互斥：无战略资源且有《遥远繁星》时各约1/3；无该 DLC 时 anomaly.1 与 15 各50%；有战略资源时只会得到 anomaly.15。

The three results are exclusive: about 1/3 each without a strategic resource and with Distant Stars; without the DLC anomaly.1 and 15 are 50% each; with a strategic resource only anomaly.15.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.225 需要《遥远繁星》。

distar.225 needs Distant Stars.
