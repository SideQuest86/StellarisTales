- 655「不可预见的机械故障」：a 每月团结（无风险）；b 获得 worse_parts，5% 概率 90+随机90天后 660「不可预见的社会故障」；c 获得能量与 worse_parts，30% 概率进入 660；d 采用 optimized_parts。无660时180+随机90天后 cyber.1000。
  655 "Unforeseeable Mechanical Failure": a monthly unity (no risk); b worse_parts with a 5% chance of 660 "Unforeseeable Societal Failure" after 90 days (+ random 90); c energy and worse_parts with a 30% chance of 660; d optimized_parts. Without 660, cyber.1000 follows after 180 days (+ random 90).
- 660：移除 worse_parts，或 discontent 修正（巨型企业付500团结，警察国家/反乌托邦有版本）；之后90+随机90天由 cyber.1000 衔接。
  660: removes worse_parts, or a discontent modifier (megacorps pay 500 unity; Police State / Dystopian have variants); cyber.1000 follows after 90 days (+ random 90).
