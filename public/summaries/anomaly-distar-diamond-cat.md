# 超重脉冲 / Heavy Pulse

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。调查无战略资源的F型恒星（白矮星外观）时可能生成（2级，权重1），且有 null_spawn_chance 0.5，即被选中后仍有50%不生成。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn when surveying an F-class star without strategic resources (level 2, weight 1); null_spawn_chance 0.5 means it still fails to appear half the time when picked.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.140“宇宙级钻石”：这颗恒星几乎完全由结晶碳构成，是一颗宇宙尺度的钻石。恒星获得物理学矿藏（清除原有采矿矿藏），并记录标本 cosmic_diamond_fragment。

distar.140 "Cosmic Diamond": the star is almost entirely crystallised carbon — a diamond of cosmic size. The star gains a physics deposit (mining deposits are cleared) and the specimen cosmic_diamond_fragment is recorded.
