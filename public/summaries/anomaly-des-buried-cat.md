## 如何开始 How it starts

- 4级异常，每个帝国最多一次；在沙漠行星上以权重3生成。  
  Level-4 anomaly, at most once per empire; spawn weight 3 on desert planets.
- 约30天后另有一份通用联络报告（story.1–8，共享枢纽故事），不属于本故事。  
  About 30 days later a generic contact report (story.1–8, a shared hub story) follows; it is not part of this story.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 两个结果各基础权重1：  
  Two outcomes, base weight 1 each:
- **沙之仆人**（4030）：坠毁的远古战机舰队与仍在运行的舰队AI。尝试接管（禁止AI的帝国不可选）→ 30天项目（科研船+科学家，限时1080天）；或摧毁AI（行星获“AI舰队”修正与工程学资源点）。已找到过该AI的帝国权重0。  
  **The Servants of Sand** (4030): a crashed ancient fleet with an active AI. Try to take control (not if AI is outlawed) → 30-day project (1,080-day limit); or destroy it (planet modifier and engineering deposit). Weight 0 if the AI was already found.
- **殖民者的教诲**（4135）：沙丘下殖民者遗址中的文献；研究（1200社会学）或出售（能源与矿物）。格式塔或已找到此著作时权重0。  
  **Teachings of Settlers** (4135): documents in a settlers' ruins; research (1,200 society) or sell. Weight 0 for gestalts or if found.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 接管AI：成功 4031 获得3级机器人指挥官（好斗、舰队后勤专家）；超时失败 anomaly_failure.4031 AI舰队起飞成为敌对势力。  
  AI project: success 4031 gives a skill-3 robot commander; on timeout anomaly_failure.4031 the AI fleet launches as a hostile faction.
- 翻译：70% 4136 解锁法令“大师的教诲：大义”；30% anomaly_failure.4136 部分译出。  
  Translation: 70% 4136 unlocks "Master's Teachings: The Greater Good"; 30% anomaly_failure.4136.
- “大师的著作”系列：大陆上的发现、钢之追寻、沙中迷藏、潜在发现四个异常各可找到“讲述者泰丽莎”的一部著作并解锁对应法令；四者互不依赖、没有顺序，失败结果 anomaly_failure.4136 共用。完成时若已找到其他著作，成功事件使用另一段“又一部”文本。  
  "Master's Writings": Continental Findings, A Feel For Steel, Buried in the Sand and Cold Hard Potential can each yield one work of "Telisa the Teller of Tales" and its edict; they are independent and unordered, sharing the failure outcome anomaly_failure.4136. If another writing was found before, the success event uses its "more teachings" text.
