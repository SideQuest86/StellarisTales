## 如何开始 How it starts

Can spawn on a regular uninhabitable planet without a strategic resource (level 2, weight 3).

Single result anomaly.2540 "Alien Mural": deposits cleared; the option enables project TRANSLATE_MURAL_PROJECT (science ship and scientist, 180 days). On completion it picks at random (50% each) anomaly.2541 (monthly engineering research, leader +200 experience, specimen index_of_knowledge) or anomaly.2542 (alien_mural modifier plus d_society_2, d_engineering_2 and d_physics_2 deposits, leader +200 experience, same specimen).
