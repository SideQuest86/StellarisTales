两个起始结果各占50%。
- anomaly.2520“巨大骨架”：清除矿藏，30天后 story.2 报告；选项（非机械终结者公民/终结者公民）启用项目 GIGANTIC_SKELETON_PROJECT“调查巨型骸骨”（180天，科学船+科学家），完成后随机（各50%）：anomaly.2521（organic_starship 修正、d_society_3 与 d_engineering_3 矿藏、领袖+200经验、标本 massive_metatarsal）或 anomaly.2522（领袖+200经验、每月物理研究）；死灵师（is_reanimator）另有选项启用 GIGANTIC_SKELETON_RESURRECTION_PROJECT“复活巨大骸骨”（360天）→ anomaly.2523（获得一支巨型亡灵军队运输舰队）。
- anomaly.6905“不要数行星”：a 获得 d_society_4 矿藏；b 启用项目 CRACK_THE_EGG“破壳”（720天），完成后90% anomaly.6910“星海蛋卷”（每月社会研究加成、d_society_8 矿藏、标本 enormous_scrambled_albumen）/ 10% anomaly.6915“感恩”（舰队被销毁，d_society_6 矿藏）；吞噬蜂群公民另有选项 c：d_food_10 矿藏。

Two starting results, 50% each.
- anomaly.2520 "Gigantic Skeleton": clears the deposits, a story.2 report after 30 days; the options (non-terminator / Machine Terminator civic) enable project GIGANTIC_SKELETON_PROJECT "Investigate Gigantic Skeleton" (180 days, science ship and scientist), whose completion picks at random (50% each) anomaly.2521 (organic_starship modifier, d_society_3 and d_engineering_3 deposits, leader +200 experience, specimen massive_metatarsal) or anomaly.2522 (leader +200 experience, monthly physics research); reanimators get an extra option that enables GIGANTIC_SKELETON_RESURRECTION_PROJECT "Resurrect the Gigantic Skeleton" (360 days) → anomaly.2523 (a giant undead army transport fleet).
- anomaly.6905 "Don't Count Your Planets": a takes a d_society_4 deposit; b enables project CRACK_THE_EGG (720 days), which on completion gives 90% anomaly.6910 "Galactic Omelet" (monthly society research, d_society_8 deposit, specimen enormous_scrambled_albumen) / 10% anomaly.6915 "Gratitude" (the fleet is destroyed, d_society_6 deposit); Devouring Swarm empires have an extra option c: a d_food_10 deposit.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

The two starting results are exclusive; 2520's project results 2521 / 2522 are exclusive (50/50); 6905's 6910 / 6915 are 90/10. 2520's options (standard / terminator / reanimator) appear by country type.
