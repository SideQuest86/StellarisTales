## 如何开始 How it starts

潜入虚境但没有找到其他事件（on_patron_delve_failed）时，从28个权重各为100的遭遇（另有权重1的 utopia.3002 兜底）中随机抽取一个满足条件的；每次潜入失败只出现一个遭遇，不会依次经历全部。本遭遇只给处于生命之织缕领域、未与其立约、也未背叛它的帝国，每个帝国一次；若该赞助者的协约已全部解锁，抽中权重×0.1。另需正在进行星球改造。

When a Shroud delve finds nothing else (on_patron_delve_failed), one eligible encounter is drawn at random from 28 candidates of weight 100 each (plus utopia.3002 at weight 1 as fallback); each failed delve shows one encounter, never all of them in sequence. Only for empires in the domain of the Composer of Strands, without a covenant with it and without having betrayed it; once per empire; weight ×0.1 once all of that patron's accords are unlocked. Also requires an ongoing terraforming.
