## 如何开始 How it starts

间谍网在“星海帝国”中的行动 operation_weaken_galactic_empire（削弱帝国权威）：需要 Nemesis DLC 或控制碎片、银河帝国存在且目标是皇帝、执行者是共同体成员而非皇帝；成本影响力60，维持能量7，间谍力35，三个难度 T3 的阶段，同时只能进行一个行动。

The Imperium operation operation_weaken_galactic_empire ("Weaken Imperial Authority"): needs Nemesis (or a control fragment), a galactic emperor to exist as the target and a performer who is a Community member but not the emperor; cost 60 influence, upkeep 7 energy, spy power 35, three tier-3 stages, one operation at a time.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

阶段1 operation.6000“渗透开始” → 阶段2 6005“建立掩护” → 阶段3 6010“确定目标”（选“开始行动”）→ 一天后 6015“行动成功”：随机从暗杀显要、暗杀情报官、炸毁纪念碑等一类行动中择一，帝国权威 -20，并杀死被锁定的帝国领袖或炸毁建筑；皇帝随即收到 6016“恐怖分子破坏帝国权威！”。

Stage 1 operation.6000 "Infiltration Begins" -> stage 2 6005 "Establishing Cover" -> stage 3 6010 "Target Determined" (choose "Commence Operation!") -> one day later 6015 "Operation Succeeds": one of several kinds of strike (assassinate a dignitary / agent / officer, blow up a monument or building, hijack a transmitter), Imperial authority -20 and the marked official killed or the building destroyed; the emperor then receives 6016 "Terrorists Undermine Imperial Authority!".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

只有一条成功线；检定失败会按通用失败事件处理并可能暴露/取消行动。

There is one success line; a failed check is handled by the generic failure events and may expose or cancel the operation.
