1. Ashen Environment (2500) → Bleached Landscape (2505): retreat to the Sphere (→ 2510 Rock Creatures) or prepare for contact (→ 2515 Rock Creatures).
2. 2510: fly toward the horizon (→ 2520 Darkest Dawn) or wait with the creatures (→ 2530); 2515: examine a creature (→ 2525 Bio-Geology → 2530) or wait (→ 2530).
3. Both options of 2520 (continue observing / turn away) lead to 2535 Black Light Blinded. 2530: observe the dark star (→ 2535) or retreat (→ 2540 Further Observation).
