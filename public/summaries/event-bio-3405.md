## 如何开始 How it starts

我方否决一项决议、而提案者是带“控制”碎片标记的失落帝国（不是银河皇帝、未交战）时，on_resolution_vetoed 触发 bio.3405。控制碎片只有在觉醒后才会加入星海共同体（fallen_empires_awakening.10，L463-469）。

When we veto a resolution proposed by the Fallen Empire carrying the Control-fragment flag (not Galactic Emperor, at peace), on_resolution_vetoed fires bio.3405. The Control fragment only joins the Galactic Community after awakening (fallen_empires_awakening.10, L463-469).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

控制碎片斥责我方是“星河合唱中的不和谐音”。唯一的回答之后，它以“驳回否决”为名、以统治为战争目标向我方宣战。

The Control fragment calls us a dissonant note in the galactic chorus. After the only reply it declares war on us ("Appeal the Veto", domination war goal).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

单一结局：开战。

Single outcome: war.
