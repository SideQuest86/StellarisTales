# 来自堕落帝国的任务：阻止人工智能研究 / Task: End AI Research

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

灵性堕落帝国要求制止别国研究人工智能：五年随机脉冲（on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614）把本任务作为候选；各候选权重相对980，抽中后仍须通过其触发条件。（权重10，灵性主义者×2；对手若有机器人工人科技则×2、有合成人科技则×0.5。）条件：非AI默认国家，非机器/终结者/吞噬/净化，不在战争中，我方未赋予AI权利或奴役，没有进行中的同类任务；有一个灵性堕落帝国要求制止与我方为对手、赋予AI权利/奴役的国家。

A spiritualist fallen empire asks us to stop another empire's AI research: The five-year random pulse (on_five_year_random_pulse_country_fe_list, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:595-614) lists this task among its candidates; weights are out of 980 and the event's own trigger must still pass. (weight 10; spiritualists x2; x2 if the rival has droid-worker tech, x0.5 if it has synthetic-worker tech.) Conditions: a human default country that is not machine, terminator, devouring or purifier, not at war, not itself granting AI rights or servitude, with no such task running; a spiritualist fallen empire names a rival that grants AI rights or servitude.
