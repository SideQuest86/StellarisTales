## 如何开始 How it starts

行动 operation_arm_privateers（武装私掠）：成本影响力180，维持能量12，间谍力60，三个难度 T6 的阶段；目标是默认国家且尚未被这个行动盯上（target_of_operation_arm_privateers）；同时只能进行一个行动。

The operation operation_arm_privateers ("Arm Privateers"): cost 180 influence, upkeep 12 energy, spy power 60, three tier-6 stages; the target is a default country not yet targeted by this operation (target_of_operation_arm_privateers); one operation at a time.
