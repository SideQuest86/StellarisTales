# 生死弥留：科技进步 / Limbo: Technological Advances

- 分类 Category：探索 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

on_tech_increased：国家研究了 tech_synthetic_workers，带有 limbo_downloaded 标志（当初在“生死弥留”异常的 anomaly.186 选了“只下载脑扫描”，见 anomaly-barr-base-cat），且未禁止人工智能（ai_outlawed）。隐藏事件 anomaly.1193 在20-40天后触发 anomaly.1194。

on_tech_increased: the country researched tech_synthetic_workers, has the limbo_downloaded flag (it chose "only download the brain scans" in anomaly.186 of the Limbo anomaly, see anomaly-barr-base-cat) and does not outlaw AI (ai_outlawed). The hidden event anomaly.1193 fires anomaly.1194 20-40 days later.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. anomaly.1194“生死弥留：科技进步”：选项a在首都启用项目 LIMBO_2_PROJECT“重获生机”（费用1000工程，无科学船要求）；选项b放弃，获得每月工程研究并清除 limbo 标志，事件链结束。
2. 项目完成（设置 limbo_uploaded_robots），按殖民条件的 if/else 链选出一个结果：有边境内外可殖民星球 → anomaly.189“生死弥留：新的开始”；仅边境内 → anomaly.1198；游牧国家 → anomaly.1200；仅境外且有 tech_droid_workers → anomaly.1199“生死弥留：觉醒”；都没有 → anomaly.1190“生死弥留：线缆中的灵魂”。
3. 189/1198/1200/1199 给出“荣幸接纳 / 分道扬镳 / 拆解”的选择：分道扬镳时 10 天后 anomaly.1196“生死弥留：觉醒者”（他们建立殖民地）或 40 天后 anomaly.1195“生死弥留：离去”。

1. anomaly.1194 "Limbo: Technological Advances": option a enables project LIMBO_2_PROJECT "A Second Chance" at the capital (cost 1000 engineering, no science ship needed); option b gives it up for monthly engineering research and clears the limbo flags, ending the chain.
2. When the project completes (sets limbo_uploaded_robots), an if/else chain on colonisable planets picks one result: colonisable planets inside and outside borders → anomaly.189 "Limbo: a New Beginning"; inside only → anomaly.1198; nomads → anomaly.1200; outside only and tech_droid_workers → anomaly.1199 "Limbo: Awakening"; none → anomaly.1190 "Limbo: Spirits in the Wires".
3. 189 / 1198 / 1200 / 1199 offer "be honoured / part ways / dismantle": parting ways leads 10 days later to anomaly.1196 "Limbo: the Awoken" (they found a colony) or 40 days later to anomaly.1195 "Limbo: Gone".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

项目完成后的五个结果互斥（if/else 链，按可殖民星球的位置）；189 的“分道扬镳”再分为 1196 / 1195（取决于是否有边境外的可殖民星球）。

The five results after the project are exclusive (an if/else chain on where colonisable planets are); parting ways in 189 then splits into 1196 / 1195 (depending on colonisable planets outside the borders).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

anomaly.1194 的选项a在 ai_outlawed 下不可用；游牧国家走单独分支（1200）。

anomaly.1194's option a is unavailable under ai_outlawed; nomad empires take their own branch (1200).
