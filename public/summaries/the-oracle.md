# 神谕 / The Oracle

- 分类 Category：先驱者与考古 Precursors · 来源 Sources：events/ancient_relics_arcsite_events_1.txt:4826–5370；common/archaeological_site_types/02_ancient_relics_arc_sites.txt:2185；common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2502–2506 · DLC：《远古遗物》Ancient Relics · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12

## 如何开始 How it starts

非AI帝国在星球上清除丛林、毒藻、深坑、流沙、沼泽或冰川类地块障碍时（on_blocker_cleared），隐藏事件 ancrel.4030 以 5/5/95 的权重在“神谕”“哨兵”和无事件之间抽取。神谕全银河只出现一次。1天后弹出 4031“隐秘世界”（按清除的障碍类型显示六种开头之一），可选择建立遗址或置之不理。

When a non-AI empire clears a jungle, kelp, sinkhole, quicksand, swamp or glacier blocker (on_blocker_cleared), hidden ancrel.4030 draws the Oracle, the Sentinels or nothing at 5/5/95. The Oracle occurs once per galaxy. A day later 4031 "Hidden Worlds" appears (one of six openings by blocker type); the site can be created or ignored.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

遗址四个阶段依次：4032 空间站居民笃信神谕、一生被预言支配；4033 墙内藏着监视系统，居民曾反抗；4034 神殿中的“神谕”其实是一台人工智能中枢；4035 修复后发现它既维持也终结了居民的生命，它想与我们交谈。

The site's four stages: 4032 the station's people lived by an oracle's prophecies; 4033 a hidden surveillance system and signs of revolt; 4034 the "Oracle" is an AI nexus; 4035 restored, it turns out to have both sustained and killed the inhabitants, and it wishes to speak with us.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 4035：关闭它 → 1000合金、3500工程学研究、500影响力，故事结束；或开启通讯 → 1天后 4036。
- 4036（领袖招募窗口）：五个提问只显示回答（居民因“产生自由意志”被神经毒气消灭；“共和国”已不存在等）；招募 → 神谕成为5级“知名”官员（狂热排外与噬杀蜂群不可选）；拆除 → 1天后 4038 再次确认：拆除得资源或仍然招募。

- 4035: disable it → 1000 alloys, 3500 engineering research, 500 influence, end; or communicate → 4036 a day later.
- 4036 (leader-recruit window): five questions only show replies (the inhabitants were gassed for developing free will; "the Republic is no more", etc.); recruit → the Oracle joins as a level-5 renowned official (not for fanatic xenophobes or devouring swarms); scrap → 4038 a day later to confirm: scrap for resources or recruit after all.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 4031 的六段开头由隐藏事件设置的载体标志决定，与被清除的障碍类型对应。
- 招募选项的措辞对机械帝国不同。

- 4031's six openings follow a carrier flag set by the dispatcher (the blocker type).
- The recruit option is worded differently for machine empires.
