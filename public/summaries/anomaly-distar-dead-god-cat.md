# 沉默巨兽 / Silent Behemoth

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。前超光速文明星球的不宜居卫星被调查时，这一异常（2级，权重 add 1000，几乎必然优先）生成，并给母星加隐藏修正 dead_god_planet_hidden。

Requires Distant Stars. When an uninhabitable moon of a primitive civilisation's planet is surveyed, this level-2 anomaly (add 1000, practically always chosen) spawns and gives the primitive planet the hidden modifier dead_god_planet_hidden.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.80“沉默巨兽”：卫星上的巨大结构其实是一头未知巨型生物的尸体，从原住民星球肉眼可见，并散发精神性化学物质。结果：社会学矿藏、社会学研究奖励，原住民星球改为 dead_god_planet 修正。
2. 若之后以地面入侵击败该星球的前超光速守军，隐藏事件 distar.81 在2天后触发 distar.82“死亡之神”：原住民把尸体奉为死亡之神，并把入侵者视为神的使者。

1. distar.80 "Silent Behemoth": the huge formation is the corpse of an unknown gargantuan creature, visible from the natives' world and shedding psychotropic chemicals. Results: a society deposit, a society research reward, and the native planet gets dead_god_planet.
2. If the primitive planet is later invaded and its defenders beaten, the hidden distar.81 fires distar.82 "God of Death" two days later: the natives worship the corpse as a god of death and see the invaders as its heralds.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.82：a 利用信仰（崇拜者物种的人口获得240个月“信念”修正，并记录标本）；b 拒绝配合；c 需吞噬虫群或允许净化；d 需仅允许驱逐。

distar.82: a exploit the belief (worshipper pops get Conviction for 240 months; a specimen is recorded); b refuse; c requires Devouring Swarm or purge allowed; d requires displacement-only purge policy.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.80 另有“演化掠食者”起源专属选项（获取局势进度与DNA）。入侵后事件不要求先调查异常，因为生成异常时已放置隐藏修正。

distar.80 has an extra Evolutionary Predators origin option (situation progress and DNA). The invasion event does not require the anomaly to be researched, because the hidden modifier is placed on spawn.
