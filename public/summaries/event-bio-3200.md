# 生态系统升级 / Ecosystem Upgrade

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

“生长”碎片存在、我方和平且拥有两个以上人口超过1000的非城市宜居星球时，五年随机脉冲可能抽中 bio.3200，立即弹出 bio.3205。触发后设置2560天的蜂巢失落帝国事件冷却（hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown），期间其他碎片要求不再出现。

With the Growth fragment present, our empire at peace and more than two non-urban habitable worlds with over 1000 pops, the five-year pulse can pick bio.3200, which shows bio.3205 at once. Firing sets a 2560-day cooldown (hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown) shared by the fragment demands.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

生长碎片把我方误认作送生物质的竞争者，随即宣布要在我方一颗殖民地上释放封存千年的基因样式。接受（或改指另一颗星球）后，360–720天后该星球触发“生物圈已改造”bio.3210。

The Growth fragment first mistakes us for a rival late with a biomass delivery, then announces it will release a long-stored genome on one of our colonies. If we accept (or offer another planet), Biosphere Modified bio.3210 fires on that planet 360-720 days later.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

bio.3210 随机得到三种结果之一：昆虫用无线电频率交流（接受或清除）、动物获得智慧（新增500人口）、整个世界发光嗡鸣（接受或砍伐覆盖）。拒绝 bio.3205 则只降低该碎片好感，没有后续。

bio.3210 gives one of three random results: insects talking on radio frequencies (accept or purge), animals gaining sapience (500 new pops), or a glowing, humming world (accept or cover it up). Refusing in bio.3205 only lowers the fragment's opinion, with no sequel.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

环保主义帝国不能接受改造，只能愤怒拒绝；地貌改造者帝国有专属的接受选项；空中更新机械帝国清除昆虫的选项不同；杀戮型帝国面对新智慧生物的回答不同。

Environmentalist empires cannot accept and only refuse angrily; Planetscaper empires get their own accept options; Over-the-Air-update machines purge the bugs differently; homicidal empires answer the new sapients differently.
