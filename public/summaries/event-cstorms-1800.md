# 风暴之眼 / Eye of the Storm

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/cosmic_storms_events_1.txt:3800-4005; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5404-5417 · DLC: Cosmic Storms · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

风暴进入某个已被占有的星系时（on_storm_entered_system），有10%概率触发 cstorms.1800（非“暴风消退”标记的风暴，每个国家仅一次，国家类型为默认）。它把风暴锁定在原地并给玩家 cstorms.1801。

When a storm enters an owned system (on_storm_entered_system) there is a 10% chance of cstorms.1800 (not for a stormfall storm, once per country, default country type). It locks the storm in place and shows cstorms.1801.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

cstorms.1801“风暴之眼”：选项“好奇”启用对应风暴类型的“风暴之眼”特殊项目并给少量物理研究。项目完成后 cstorms.1805“进入漩涡”：科学船收集资源——磁暴得合金，天体风暴得矿物，虚境风暴得 Zro，联结风暴得多种资源，其余得能量；10%概率让领袖受创。

cstorms.1801 "Eye of the Storm": the option "Curious" enables the "Eye of the Storm" special project for the storm type and grants a little physics research. When the project finishes cstorms.1805 "Into the Vortex": the science ship harvests resources - alloys from magnetic storms, minerals from celestial, Zro from shroud, several from nexus, energy otherwise; 10% chance the leader is traumatised.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

八种风暴类型各对应一个项目（互斥）；领袖创伤为10%随机。

One project per storm type (exclusive); leader trauma is a 10% roll.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

需要 Cosmic Storms DLC。

Needs the Cosmic Storms DLC.
