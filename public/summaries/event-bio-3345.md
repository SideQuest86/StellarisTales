# 星河赘瘤 / Galactic Excrescence

## 如何开始 How it starts

开局20年后，控制碎片存在、我方和平且有一个“碍眼”的边境星系（无己方殖民地、外凸于边界），并且近期未清理过边界时，五年随机脉冲可能抽中 bio.3345，立即弹出 bio.3350。触发后设置2560天的蜂巢失落帝国事件冷却（hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown），期间其他碎片要求不再出现。

After year 20, with the Control fragment present, our empire at peace, an "eyesore" border system (no colony of ours, jutting out of our borders) and no recent border clean-up, the five-year pulse can pick bio.3345, which shows bio.3350 at once. Firing sets a 2560-day cooldown (hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown) shared by the fragment demands.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

控制碎片斥责我方边界难看，要求拆除该星系的恒星基地。两个回答效果相同，都开启任务。

The Control fragment scolds our ugly borders and demands we disband the starbase in that system. Both replies have the same effect and start the task.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

放弃该星系时得到“明智”bio.3360（碎片满意，10年内不再提此要求）；若360–720天后仍持有，得到“头疼”bio.3355（碎片愤怒）。完成后五年内若重新取得该星系，也会得到 bio.3355。

Giving the system up gives Clarity bio.3360 (the fragment is pleased; no repeat for 10 years); still holding it after 360-720 days gives Headache bio.3355 (the fragment is angry). Regaining the system within five years of completing also gives bio.3355.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

热衷探索、硕果累累或星际弹弓起源的帝国有专属回答“我们就喜欢这样！”。

Eager Explorer, Fruitful Partnership or Star Slingshot empires get the reply "But we like it like this!".
