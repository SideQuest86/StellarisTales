# 补给船残骸 / Supply Ship Wreckage

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar） · 审阅 Reviewed：2026-10-12（已审阅 reviewed）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。任意天体都可能生成（2级，权重1），条件是本帝国不是杀戮型，且有一个可通讯、非敌对、非杀戮型的AI普通邻国。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on any body (level 2, weight 1) if the empire is not homicidal and has a non-homicidal AI default neighbour it has communications with and is not at war with.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.1“残骸身份确认”：补给船遭遇地磁太阳风暴而失事，货舱里有矿物，且很可能属于某个邻国。
- 拿走矿物（+300矿物、获得情报）：若有失主，180天后 distar.2 对方要求赔偿——装傻（关系变差）、归还300矿物，或付不起。
- 不冒险冲突：60天后 distar.3 对方致谢（影响力、人情、好感）。

distar.1 "Wreckage Identified": a supply ship lost to a geomagnetic storm; its hold has minerals that probably belong to a neighbour.
- Take them (+300 minerals, intel): if there is an owner, 180 days later distar.2 demands repayment — play dumb (opinion penalty), return 300 minerals, or cannot afford it.
- Avoid an altercation: distar.3 60 days later, they thank us (influence, a favour, opinion).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.1 两选项通向互斥的 distar.2 / distar.3；distar.2 的选项由矿物储量决定。

distar.1's options lead to the exclusive distar.2 / distar.3; distar.2's options depend on the mineral stockpile.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.2 有机械智能正文。原版 distar.1 的“无接触”正文与“有接触”使用相同触发条件（都要求失主存在），实际只会显示第一段。

distar.2 has a machine text. In the original, distar.1's "nocontact" text uses the same trigger as the "contact" text (both require an owner), so effectively only the first shows.
