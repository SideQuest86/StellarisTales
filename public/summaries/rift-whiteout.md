## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 空白天地（700）：球外只有一片纯白，所有仪器失灵。全速前进（→ 705 高墙，难度 2：一堵会缓慢扩张的黑色圆墙，获得星界丝缕）或释放无人机（→ 710 鸟眼视觉，难度 5：墙高总等于无人机高度，获得物理研究）。
2. 新进展（715）：一系列新墙出现在面前。取样（→ 720 响应，难度 6：墙体像无限高的墨迹，写下的图形会被复制，还多出像译文的符号；获得稀有晶体）或绘图（→ 725 问候，难度 3：从上方看墙体是文字，其中一个符号意为“你好”；获得社会学研究）。
3. 对话（730）：数周来与被称为“写作者”的实体艰难交流，最后出现“礼物”一词与一颗金色球体。

1. Whiteout (700): nothing but white outside the Sphere; every instrument fails. Maximize propulsion (→ 705 The Wall, difficulty 2: a slowly growing circular black wall; astral threads) or release a drone (→ 710 Bird's-Eye View, difficulty 5: the wall is always exactly as tall as the drone flies; physics research).
2. A New Development (715): new walls appear ahead. Take a sample (→ 720 A Response, difficulty 6: the walls behave like infinitely tall ink; drawn shapes are copied with an added symbol, perhaps a translation; rare crystals) or trace a map (→ 725 A Greeting, difficulty 3: from above the walls are writing, one symbol meaning “hello”; society research).
3. A Conversation (730): weeks of halting exchange with an entity named “the Writer”, ending with the word “Gift” and a golden sphere.
