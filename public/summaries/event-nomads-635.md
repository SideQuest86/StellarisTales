# 可汗之死 / Death of the Khan

- 分类 Category: C1 起源 / C1 Origins · 来源 Sources: events/nomads_events_1.txt:5561-5760; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2369 · DLC: Nomads · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12

## 如何开始 How it starts

可汗后裔起源称汗之后，大可汗（统治者特质“大可汗”）死亡时，on_leader_death_notify 触发 nomads.635（L5561-5580）。

After a Heirs of the Khan empire has become Khan, the death of its Great Khan ruler fires nomads.635 via on_leader_death_notify (L5561-5580).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

按传统，可汗死后汗国应放弃统治权，盟友氏族已停止派遣援军。

By tradition the Khanate must give up its power when the Khan dies; the allied clans have already stopped sending reinforcements.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

a：遵守传统，放弃附庸，回到掠夺者盟主身份，720–740天后转入氏族对话 nomads.750；b：宣称汗国永存。

a: follow tradition, give up the subjects and become the marauders' overlord again (clan dialogue nomads.750 after 720-740 days); b: declare the Khanate eternal.
