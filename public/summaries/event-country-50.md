# 宜居星球调查 / Habitable Worlds Survey

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/country_events_1.txt:1330-2238; common/event_chains/00_event_chains.txt:135-155; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:1138; common/special_projects/00_projects_1.txt (*_PROCUREMENT) · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

在第一次接触外星生命后（first_alien_life旗标，由故事事件设置），country.50 约每40个月随机弹出：科学团队提议调查所有宜居星球。接受即开始事件链，也可拒绝并换取少量团结。

After first contact with alien life (the first_alien_life flag set by story events) country.50 fires at random about every 40 months: the science team proposes surveying all habitable worlds. Accepting starts the chain; declining gives a small unity bonus.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

玩家勘测宜居类型星球（沙漠、热带、干旱、大陆、海洋、冻原、极地、高山、稀树草原、盖亚）；隐藏事件 country.52 在每次勘测时计数，满8颗后 country.51“调查完成”给出社会研究与能量奖励，并设置 museum_of_exobiology 旗标。该旗标是第二段故事的条件：约十年后（平均120个月）country.53 请求为外星生物博物馆采集样本，并在已勘测的海洋、沙漠、热带、干旱、大陆、极地、冻原星球上各启用一个特殊项目（塔布拉克、塔萨古德、欧林提、萨尼兰、古兰萨、乌杜兰、祖卡），项目成功后各有一个“已捕获”事件（country.54–60），七个全部完成后 country.61 结束事件链。

The player surveys habitable-class planets (desert, tropical, arid, continental, ocean, tundra, arctic, alpine, savannah, gaia); hidden country.52 counts each survey and at eight country.51 "Survey Completed" rewards society research and energy and sets the museum_of_exobiology flag. That flag gates the second part: about ten years later (mean 120 months) country.53 asks for specimens for the museum and enables one special project each on surveyed ocean, desert, tropical, arid, continental, arctic and tundra planets (Tarblac, Tassargoid, Orinthi, Sniran, Goolantha, Uddloran, Zulcor); each project success has a "captured" event (country.54-60) and when all seven are done country.61 ends the chain.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

无互斥分支；七个样本项目为并行，顺序任意。

No exclusive branches; the seven specimen projects are parallel in any order.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

人类玩家，非集群意识，非游牧；第二段还需要勘测过相应星球类型，且星系归属不能是堕落帝国或觉醒堕落帝国。

Human player, non-gestalt, non-nomadic; the second part also needs surveyed planets of those classes whose system is not owned by a fallen or awakened empire.
