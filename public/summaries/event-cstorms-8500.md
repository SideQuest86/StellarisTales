# 灵能切换 / Psionic Switch

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

虚境风暴在开始或移动时有20%概率对天体执行“灵能切换”（隐藏事件 cstorms.8500，一个行星只会被改变一次）。若天体所在系统有所有者，则通知所有者。

A shroud storm has a 20% chance when it starts or moves to run "Psionic exchange" on a celestial body (hidden cstorms.8500; a planet is changed only once). If the system has an owner it is notified.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

未殖民行星改变类别（温湿→干/冷，干→湿/冷……；少数变盖亚或核爆；荒芜类行星有机会变虚境、破碎、粉碎、灰雾并附带资源矿点）——cstorms.8510；殖民地中随机一个人口组换成新物种——cstorms.8515；单星恒星改变光谱类型（小概率变黑洞、中子星、脉冲星）——cstorms.8520。

An uncolonised planet changes class (wet to dry or cold, dry to wet or cold, ...; rarely gaia or nuked; barren-type worlds may become shrouded, broken, shattered or gray goo with a resource deposit) - cstorms.8510; in a colony one pop group is converted to a new species - cstorms.8515; a single star changes spectral class (small chance of black hole, neutron star or pulsar) - cstorms.8520.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

三种通知互斥，由目标类型决定；权重在隐藏事件中（如恒星各20，黑洞/中子星/脉冲星各5）。

The three notifications are exclusive and set by target type; weights are in the hidden event (stars 20 each, black hole / neutron star / pulsar 5 each).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

Cosmic Storms DLC；不影响栖息地、二联/三联星、黑洞等。

Cosmic Storms DLC; habitats, binary/trinary stars and black holes are not affected.
