# 坠毁的飞船 / Crashed Ship

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

小行星可能生成（3级，权重3）。

Can spawn on an asteroid (level 3, weight 3).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

anomaly.35“坠毁的侦查器”（每月工程研究加成）与 anomaly.65“坠毁的矿石货船”（采矿矿藏 d_minerals_3 + ore_freighter 修正；若星球有战略资源则权重为0）。

anomaly.35 "Crashed Scout Craft" (monthly engineering research) and anomaly.65 "Crashed Ore Freighter" (d_minerals_3 deposit plus the ore_freighter modifier; weight 0 if the planet has a strategic resource).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

两个结果互斥，各50%；有战略资源时只会得到 35。

The results are exclusive, 50% each; with a strategic resource only 35.
