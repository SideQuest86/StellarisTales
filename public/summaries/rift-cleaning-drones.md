# 化学废土 / Chemical Wasteland

- 分类 Category：星界裂隙 Astral rifts (C4) · DLC：星界位面 Astral Planes

## 如何开始 How it starts

从探索帝国的裂隙池按权重抽取，权重 1，无条件（`00_astral_rifts.txt:344–353`）。每阶段结束掷骰，≥14 进入下一章；第一章 2600 难度为初始难度。

Drawn by weight from the explorer's rift pool, weight 1, no conditions (`00_astral_rifts.txt:344–353`). Each phase ends with a roll; 14+ opens the next chapter.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 化学清洁（2600）：行星被抗菌剂彻底消毒；选择调查机器人（→ 2605）或探索洞穴（→ 2610）。
2. 机器人路线：遭遇无人机（2605）→ 让其靠近（2615 清洁射击，降低难度）或瘫痪它们（2620）→ 追踪信号 / 跟随无人机到 2635 “造主的迹象” → 实验室 2645；瘫痪后也可直接逃离（2660）。
3. 洞穴路线：一丝生机（2610）→ 鸟类解剖（2625）与无人机修复（2630）两项都要做，顺序不限（各自降低难度）→ 超洁净假设（2640）：利用技术（2665）/ 注入病毒（2670）/ 留作研究助理（2675）。
4. 实验室（2645）：记录研究（2650）或关闭（2655）。

1. Chemically Cleansed (2600): the planet is sterilised; investigate the robots (→ 2605) or explore the cave (→ 2610).
2. Robot route: Drone Encounter (2605) → allow them near (2615 Clean Shot, lowers difficulty) or disable them (2620) → follow to 2635 Signs of the Makers → the lab 2645; after disabling you may also flee (2660).
3. Cave route: Signs of Life (2610) → Avian Autopsy (2625) and Drone Repaired (2630), both required in either order (each lowers difficulty) → Hygienic Hypothesis (2640): repurpose the tech (2665) / engineer a virus (2670) / keep the drone as an assistant (2675).
4. The lab (2645): document it (2650) or shut it down (2655).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

六个结局都结束探索并给予大量星界丝缕：2650 记录基础设施（国家标记 edict_automated_disinfection）、2655 关闭设施（能量）、2660 逃离（合金）、2665 改造无人机（殖民无人机修正）、2670 病毒（加密科技或计算研究）、2675 研究助理（科学家特质）。没有失败分支。

All six endings finish the exploration with a large astral thread reward: 2650 documented infrastructure (country flag edict_automated_disinfection), 2655 shut down (energy), 2660 escape (alloys), 2665 repurposed drone (colonization modifier), 2670 virus (encryption tech or computing research), 2675 research assistant (scientist trait). No failure branch.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 2645：“记录并研究”仅限有机物种且非荒野帝国；机械物种只见“很迷人”；有机物种可选“关闭设施”。2645 与 2650/2655 的正文随标记 clean_shot 与帝国类型变化。
- 2625.b 对“演化猎手”起源换成专属选项并额外给予起源进度与 DNA。
- 2655 对不友善帝国给更高档能量。

- 2645: “Document and study” needs an organic species in a non-Wilderness empire; non-organic species only see “Fascinating”; organic species may also shut the facility down. Texts of 2645/2650/2655 vary with the clean_shot flag and empire type.
- 2625.b is replaced for Evolutionary Predators by an exclusive option with origin progress and DNA.
- 2655 gives a higher energy tier to unfriendly empires.
