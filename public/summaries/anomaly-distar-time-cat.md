## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。调查中子星或脉冲星时可能生成（1级，权重1，全局一次）；若玩家物种使用 mol4 头像（科塔罗人外形）则不会生成。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn when surveying a neutron star or pulsar (level 1, weight 1, once per game); not if the empire's species uses portrait mol4 (the Kohtalo look).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.60“炫目的命运”：残骸属于一艘名为“科塔罗”的外形独特的飞船，它脱离超光速时离脉冲星伽马射线流太近而毁灭，无人生还。
2. 360天后 distar.61“似曾相识”：一艘与残骸一模一样的船出现并呼叫我们 → distar.62–64：语言不通，改用数据交流；他们是来自另一星系的探险者，而对照数据后发现残骸就是这艘船——只是更“老”。
3. distar.65：警告他们 → distar.66 他们匆忙离去，360天后 distar.67“衔尾之蛇”：他们在星系外缘撞上彗星而毁灭——我们的警告恰恰导致了灾难；或不再干扰时间线 → 360天后 distar.68：同样撞上彗星，其负质量引擎无法准确预测出跃点。两种结局中两具残骸都留在脉冲星旁。

1. distar.60 "Blinding Fate": the wreck is a uniquely designed ship, the "Kohtalo", destroyed when it dropped out of FTL too close to the pulsar's gamma-ray jets; no survivors.
2. 360 days later distar.61 "Déjà Vu": an identical ship appears and hails us → distar.62–64: talking through raw data, they are explorers from another galaxy, and the data shows the wreck is this very ship — only older.
3. distar.65: warn them → distar.66, they leave in a hurry, and 360 days later distar.67 "Ouroboros": they hit a comet at the system's edge — our warning caused the disaster; or avoid further interference → distar.68 360 days later: the same collision, blamed on their negative-mass drive. Either way two wrecks now circle the pulsar.

distar.65's two options lead to the exclusive endings 67 / 68.
