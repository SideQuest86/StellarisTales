# [From.GetName]被轰炸 / [From.GetName] Bombarded

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/on_action_events_1.txt:7280-7400; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2185,2198 on_colony_zero_pops · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

星球遭轨道轰炸时（on_colony_zero_pops，00_on_actions.txt:2198）action.81 检查：轰炸姿态为“末日”或“天花”、轰炸者不是觉醒掠夺者、星球宜居且总人口不超过50时，星球变为核战废土（tomb world）。

On orbital bombardment (on_colony_zero_pops, 00_on_actions.txt:2198) action.81 checks: Armageddon or pox stance, the bombarder is not an awakened marauder, the planet is habitable and holds at most 50 pops; then it becomes a tomb world.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

前主人收到 action.82（原始版）；若起源为“祖蜂巢”且为首都，则改为 origin.3265；轰炸者收到 action.83（若有“成为危机”飞升则完成毁灭世界目标）。星球变为 pc_nuked、拆除建筑、重掷矿点。

The former owner receives action.82 (or origin.3265 for a Progenitor Hive capital); the bombarder gets action.83 (completing the destroy-worlds objective with Become the Crisis). The planet becomes pc_nuked, loses its buildings and rerolls its deposits.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

82 与 83 是同一事件的两方视角，同时发生。

action.82 and action.83 are the two perspectives of the same event and appear together.
