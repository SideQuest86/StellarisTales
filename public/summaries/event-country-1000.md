# 浪迹星涯 / Wanderlust

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/country_events_2.txt:341-1868; events/game_start.txt:15; common/event_chains/00_event_chains_2.txt:12-41 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

开局事件闸门以很低的权重（1）抽中。研究总督（科学家）弹出 country.1000 并被放逐，30天后 country.1001 开始第一条事件链，在首都1到5跳内标记三个搜索星系。

Drawn from the starting-event gate with a very low weight (1). The research councilor (a scientist) appears in country.1000 and is exiled; 30 days later country.1001 starts the first chain, marking three search systems 1-5 jumps from the capital.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

一、搜寻：舰队进入三个星系（任意顺序），前两次 country.1003“死胡同”，第三次 country.1005 找到科学官并进入对话（country.1010–1012）；他不耐烦后 country.1015 召来“碎片探测器”攻击。二、追击：击毁探测器（country.1020，他国击毁则 country.1022），启用“蛛丝马迹”项目，完成后 country.1025 开启第二条事件链，在3到8跳外标记一个星系；进入时 country.1030“遇上麻烦了”，阳炎海盗出现，科学官（country.1035）与海盗（country.1040）对话。三、对峙：付钱（country.1041）或开战（country.1042，击毁后 country.1045）；两条路都以与科学官的对话 country.1044/1045 收束，并经 country.1046 获得物理研究，country.1047 开启第三条事件链并标记最后星系。四、真相之窗：进入后 country.1050–1053 与科学官最后对话，country.1055 结束故事。

1) Search: fleets enter the three systems (any order); the first two give country.1003 "dead end", the third country.1005 finds the scientist and opens dialogue (country.1010-1012); when he gets fed up country.1015 sends "scrap probes". 2) Pursuit: destroy the probes (country.1020, or country.1022 if another empire does), enabling the "Trail of breadcrumbs" project; its completion country.1025 starts the second chain and marks a system 3-8 jumps away; entering gives country.1030 "In Trouble", the Sunblaster Pirates appear and the scientist (country.1035) and pirates (country.1040) talk. 3) Standoff: pay them off (country.1041) or fight (country.1042, then country.1045 once they die); both paths end in the scientist dialogue country.1044/1045, then country.1046 (physics research) and country.1047 which starts the third chain and marks the final system. 4) The window: entering gives country.1050-1053, the last conversation, and country.1055 ends the story.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

主要分支：探测器自己击毁或他国击毁；海盗付钱或开战（含他国消灭）。结局唯一，汇入 country.1055。

Main branches: probes killed by us or someone else; pirates paid off or fought (including killed by others). One ending, country.1055.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

付钱选项需要150能量及和平主义/物质主义伦理；排外者可有专属回应；开局条件见上。

Paying needs 150 energy and pacifist/materialist ethics; xenophobes get their own replies; start conditions above.
