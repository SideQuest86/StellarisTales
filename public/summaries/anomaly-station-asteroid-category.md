# 废弃空间站 / Abandoned Station

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

有矿物的小行星可能生成（3级，权重3，全局一次 max_once）。

Can spawn on an asteroid with minerals (level 3, weight 3, once per game via max_once).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

两个结果各占50%。anomaly.40“自动采矿基地”：若该小行星在帝国边境外，按它的矿物/能量资源给每月矿物或能量加成；若在边境内，则直接建立采矿站。anomaly.6810“感染”：a 随机（各50%）杀死一名科学家或获得超空间引擎科技进度；b 获得50影响力；c（需要利维坦或遥远繁星 DLC、机械物种（ART）、非杀戮帝国、系统无特殊标志）创建巨型外星策展人并进入 anomaly.6815“巨大外星节肢动物”→ anomaly.6820（建立与策展人飞地的联络、超空间引擎科技、标本 curator_survey）；d（虚空居民起源或有 tech_habitat_1）直接获得超空间引擎科技；演化掠食者另有 DNA 选项。

Two results, 50% each. anomaly.40 "Automated Mining Base": outside the empire's borders it grants monthly minerals or energy depending on the asteroid's resources; inside borders it builds a mining station. anomaly.6810 "Infestation": a random 50/50 between killing a scientist and progress toward hyperdrive tech; b 50 influence; c (needs Leviathans or Distant Stars, a robotic species, non-homicidal, and no special system flag) creates giant alien curators and continues to anomaly.6815 "The Giant Alien Arthropoids" then anomaly.6820 (contact with the curator enclave, hyperdrive tech, specimen curator_survey); d (Void Dweller origin or tech_habitat_1) hyperdrive tech directly; Evolutionary Predators get an extra DNA option.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

两个起始结果互斥；6810 的选项是并列选择，只有选项c延伸到 6815→6820 的策展人线。

The starting results are exclusive; 6810's options are alternatives and only option c continues into the 6815 -> 6820 curator line.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

anomaly.40 的描述与选项按小行星是否在帝国边境内变化；6810 的选项c/d受 DLC、物种类别、起源与科技限制。

anomaly.40's description and options depend on whether the asteroid is inside the empire's borders; 6810's options c/d are gated by DLC, species class, origin and technology.
