# 战胜敌方 / Victory over the Enemy
- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

赢得战争时（on_war_won，00_on_actions.txt:3122）弹出，仅限人类玩家；战争目标为征服、反击、独立或代理战争，且败者仍有未被宣称的殖民地。

Fires when a war is won (on_war_won, 00_on_actions.txt:3122); human players only; the war goal is conquest, counterattack, independence or proxy war and the loser still holds colonies not claimed.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

一个外交窗口，正文随败者的AI性格（荣誉战士、贸易者、堕落帝国……）、是否公司、是否 ketlings 而变。

A diplomatic window; the text varies with the loser's AI personality (honorbound warriors, traders, fallen empires ...), megacorp status and ketlings.
