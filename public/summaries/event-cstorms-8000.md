# 宇宙调整 / Cosmic Alteration

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

风暴消散时（storm_types 的 *_remove 效果），对风暴改动过矿点的有主星球有20%（磁暴25%）概率弹出 cstorms.8000“宇宙调整”（common/scripted_effects/cosmic_storms_scripted_effects.txt:411,520,629,738,847,956,1107）。

When a storm dissipates (the *_remove effects of storm_types) an owned planet whose deposits were altered has a 20% chance (25% for magnetic storms) of showing cstorms.8000 "Cosmic Alteration" (common/scripted_effects/cosmic_storms_scripted_effects.txt:411,520,629,738,847,956,1107).
