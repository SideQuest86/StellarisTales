# 干扰 / Interference

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。任意恒星可能生成（1级，权重1，每个帝国一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on any star (level 1, weight 1, once per empire).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.265“声化科学”：干扰中藏着一首歌——一个高等数学方程的声化表达，信号的测地线指向银河系之外。奖励社会学与物理学研究，并设置 lcluster_clue_sonic 标记。结果执行 pop_lcluster_event：若已遇到首座L星门且线索未集齐，有30%几率在10天后触发通用的 distar.9000“L星门见解”（另一故事）。若 distar.9000 因此出现，会使用与这首“歌”相关的专门文本。30天后的通用接触报告 story.5 是共享事件，不属于本故事。

distar.265 "Sonified Science": the interference hides a song — the sonification of an advanced equation whose geodesics point to an origin outside the galaxy. Rewards society and physics research and sets lcluster_clue_sonic. The result runs pop_lcluster_event: if the empire has met its first L-Gate and the clues are incomplete, there is a 30% chance of the generic distar.9000 "L-Gate Insights" 10 days later (a separate story). If distar.9000 then fires it uses a dedicated text about this song. The generic contact report story.5 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

Single outcome; no random branch.
