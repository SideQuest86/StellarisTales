## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。星系中有废弃星门时调查恒星可能生成（2级，权重100——为与其他恒星异常竞争而故意设高；每个帝国一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn when surveying the star of a system with a ruined gateway (level 2, weight 100 — deliberately high to compete with other star anomalies; once per empire).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.45“重力读数异常”：整个星系的重力读数异常，可能与星门有关 → 在星门处开启 DARK_FLOW_PROJECT（30天，科研船+科学家）→ distar.46“黑暗激流”：科学家追踪到星门中心，归来后描述星系之外巨大的物质结构正把整个星系拉向它们；读数随即消失。科学家获得偏执特质与2级技能，奖励物理学研究。结果执行 pop_lcluster_event：若已遇到首座L星门且线索未集齐，有30%几率在10天后触发通用的 distar.9000“L星门见解”（另一故事）。

distar.45 "Gravitometric Abnormalities": the whole system's gravity readings are off, perhaps because of the gateway → DARK_FLOW_PROJECT at the gateway (30 days, science ship + scientist) → distar.46 "Dark Flow": the scientist follows the source to the gateway's centre and returns speaking of vast structures beyond the stars pulling our galaxy toward them; the readings then stop. The scientist becomes Paranoid and gains 2 skill; physics research. The result runs pop_lcluster_event: if the empire has met its first L-Gate and the clues are incomplete, there is a 30% chance of the generic distar.9000 "L-Gate Insights" 10 days later (a separate story).
