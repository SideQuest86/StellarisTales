# 突变：政府改革？ / Mutation: Government Reformation?

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: common/traditions/01_mutation.txt:49-62; events/biogenesis_events.txt:3466 · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12

## 如何开始 How it starts

蜂巢意识完成“变异”传统树时，tr_mutation_finish 的 on_enabled 触发 bio.195（common/traditions/01_mutation.txt:49-62）。非蜂巢帝国在同一时刻触发的是 bio.170/175/180（另一故事）。

When a hive mind finishes the Mutation tradition tree, the on_enabled of tr_mutation_finish fires bio.195 (common/traditions/01_mutation.txt:49-62). Non-hive empires get bio.170/175/180 instead (another story).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单页事件：完成变异传统后，询问是否让政体随之改变。

One window: after finishing the Mutation tree, the hive is asked whether to reform its government accordingly.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

选择 a 设置 bio_mutation，改为变异权力机构并升级“环境整合”传统；进化掠食者起源另调用隐藏事件 bio.1105；b 维持原状。

Option a sets bio_mutation, changes to the Mutation authority and upgrades the Environmental Integration tradition; Evolutionary Predators also run hidden bio.1105; b keeps the government.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

这些传统树通常经由“基因重塑”情势的完成事件 bio.40 采纳；本事件没有后续窗口。

These trees are normally adopted through the Biomorphosis completion event bio.40; this event has no follow-up window.
