# 轨道残骸 / Orbital Debris

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/strange_worlds_terraforming_events.txt:573-723; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2105,2125 on_terraforming_progress_25 / on_terraforming_complete · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

星球环境改造进行到25%时（on_terraforming_progress_25，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2105，随机30/180），若星系里有被毁巨型建筑、该星球首次改造且国家尚未触发过，隐藏事件 strange_worlds.1045 记下该星球，30+150天后 strange_worlds.1050。

When a planet's terraforming reaches 25% (on_terraforming_progress_25, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2105, random 30/180), if the system has a ruined megastructure, it is the planet's first terraformation and the country has not had the chain, hidden strange_worlds.1045 records the planet and strange_worlds.1050 follows after 30+150 days.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

strange_worlds.1050“轨道残骸”：a“给我们造一个卫星”——改造总时间+25%，并在改造完成时（on_terraforming_complete，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2125）由隐藏事件 strange_worlds.1055 触发 strange_worlds.1060“垃圾之月”（生成一颗含矿物与合金矿点的垃圾卫星）；b“它自己会烧掉的”——星球获得 d_space_junk_hazards 矿点。两者都给工程研究。

strange_worlds.1050 "Orbital Debris": a "Make us a moon" - terraforming takes 25% longer and, when it completes (on_terraforming_complete, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:2125), hidden strange_worlds.1055 fires strange_worlds.1060 "Junk Moon" (a junk moon with mineral and alloy deposits is spawned); b "It will burn up on its own" - the planet gets the d_space_junk_hazards deposit. Both give engineering research.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

a（垃圾之月）与 b（碎片危害）互斥。

a (junk moon) and b (debris hazards) are exclusive.
