# 激进信徒 / Radical Cult

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/country_events_1.txt:2240-3772; events/country_events_2.txt:17-66; common/event_chains/00_event_chains.txt:157-186; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:981-986,1573,1615-1616,2042-2045; common/special_projects/00_projects_1.txt:2728-2775 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

开局事件闸门抽中后弹出 country.200：一支舰队被激进信徒劫持，信徒以首都星系内一颗行星为基地。对方被设为敌对，并在被劫持旗舰所在位置设一个兴趣点。

Drawn by the starting-event gate, country.200 appears: a fleet was hijacked by radical believers based on a planet in the capital system. They are made hostile and a point of interest marks the hijacked flagship.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

选项a：与劫持者开战。击瘫其旗舰（隐藏事件 country.201）触发 country.202，启用“登上邪教徒的舰船”项目，成功后 country.203 登船战斗，并在三个无主星系标记“搜索”点。舰队进入这些星系会遇到掠夺舰队（country.204/205/206）；前两支被击毁给 country.219，第三支击毁后出现战斗残骸 country.207 与“回收残骸”项目，完成后 country.208 揭示邪教船坞。摧毁船坞（country.209）后约150±50天 country.210 暴露旗舰位置，进入该星系得 country.211，击瘫旗舰（country.213）并登船（项目BOARD_CULT_SHIP_2）后 country.214 夺得旗舰，事件链结束。

Option a: fight. Disabling their flagship (hidden country.201) shows country.202 and enables the "Board the cultist ship" project; on success country.203 is the boarding fight and three unowned systems are marked as search points. Fleets entering them meet marauder fleets (country.204/205/206); the first two kills give country.219, the third leaves battle debris, country.207, and a salvage project; when done country.208 reveals a cultist shipyard. Destroying it (country.209) leads about 150+-50 days later to country.210, which reveals the flagship's system; entering gives country.211, disabling it (country.213) and boarding (project BOARD_CULT_SHIP_2) ends with country.214 capturing the flagship and the chain ends.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

选项b（125团结）买下舰队并直接结束；每个关键战斗都有“他国代为击毁”的替代窗口（country.218、221、223），结果等同。

Option b (125 unity) buys the fleet and ends immediately; every key fight has an "another empire did it" window (country.218, 221, 223) with the same result.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

非集群意识、人类玩家、非游牧；灵性主义者权重翻倍，物质主义者减半；生物舰船帝国使用生物舰船文本变体。

Non-gestalt, human player, not nomadic; spiritualists double the weight, materialists halve it; bio-ship empires get bio-ship text variants.
