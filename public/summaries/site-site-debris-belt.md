## 剧情脉络 Story outline

小行星带是奥德里斯坎人巨型栖息地的残骸；带中一艘小型“太空小屋”里是一名探宝者的遗骸和他的任务日志，日志由乐观走向疯狂，最终得到平静。

1. 小屋：残破栖息地中的“太空小屋”和一具千年遗骸（小型文物）
2. 长期旅居：任务日志：为治女儿的病而来，却家破人亡
3. 陷入疯狂：日志越来越疯狂；领袖获得“偏执”和“狂躁”特质
4. 终得安息：最后一刻的清醒：他找到了想找的东西，获得遗珍“奥德里斯坎水晶”

The asteroid belt is the wreckage of Odryskan habitats; a tiny "space cabin" holds a treasure-seeker's remains and mission logs that slide from hope to madness and finally peace.

1. The Cabin: a "space cabin" with a lone alien's remains and data crystals
2. A Long Sojourn: mission logs: he came to pay for his daughter's treatment and lost everything
3. Descent Into Madness: the logs turn mad; the leader gains the traits Paranoid and Maniacal
4. Peace At The Last: a last lucid moment; the relic "Odryskan Crystal" is gained
