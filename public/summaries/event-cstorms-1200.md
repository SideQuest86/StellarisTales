# 双星往事 / A Tale of Two Planets

## 如何开始 How it starts

拥有“相撞行星”修正的星球被殖民满一年后（on_colony_1_year_old，00_on_actions.txt:3723），cstorms.1200 触发（仅一次，需有所有者），开启事件链并给社会研究。

A planet carrying the "collided planet" modifier fires cstorms.1200 one year after being colonised (on_colony_1_year_old, 00_on_actions.txt:3723; once only, needs an owner); it starts the chain and grants society research.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

150天后 cstorms.1205“脆弱的平衡”，再150天后 cstorms.1210“蜕变新生”：此时星球进入崩坏倒计时（50年）。玩家有三个选择：a 启动“稳定撞击”项目；b 启动“改造相撞行星”项目；c 什么都不做。项目成功后随机得到结果，倒计时结束仍未处理则 cstorms.1240 毁灭星球。

150 days later cstorms.1205 "Precarious Balance", 150 days after that cstorms.1210 "Metamorphosis Unchained": the planet now has a 50-year collapse countdown. Three choices: a start the "Stabilize the Collision" project; b start the "Harmonize the Collision" project; c do nothing. A finished project gives a random result; if the countdown ends untreated cstorms.1240 destroys the planet.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

稳定撞击：95% cstorms.1220（保持大陆，或转为稀树草原/高山）、5% cstorms.1225（盖亚星球）。改造：33% 盖亚、63% cstorms.1230（八个坑洞/火山障碍）、4% cstorms.1235（星球分裂，社会研究奖励）。不处理或超时：cstorms.1240（星球被毁，社会研究奖励）。

Stabilise: 95% cstorms.1220 (stay continental, or turn savannah/alpine), 5% cstorms.1225 (gaia world). Harmonise: 33% gaia, 63% cstorms.1230 (eight crater/volcano blockers), 4% cstorms.1235 (the planet splits, society research reward). Neglect or timeout: cstorms.1240 (planet destroyed, society research reward).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

需要 Cosmic Storms DLC；仅限有所有者的殖民星球。

Needs the Cosmic Storms DLC; colonies with an owner only.
