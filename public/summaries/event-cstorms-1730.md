# 死火山 / Volcanic Inactivity

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Cosmic Storms

## 如何开始 How it starts

任意风暴离开拥有活火山殖民地的星系时（00_on_actions.txt:5435），隐藏事件 cstorms.1730 选中一个活火山殖民星球，360天后 cstorms.1731 再次确认，然后弹出 cstorms.1735。

When any storm leaves a system with a colony holding an active volcano (00_on_actions.txt:5435) hidden cstorms.1730 picks one volcano colony, cstorms.1731 re-checks it 360 days later and then cstorms.1735 appears.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

cstorms.1735“死火山”：移除活火山矿点，获得矿物收入并推进火山科技（25%）。

cstorms.1735 "Volcanic Inactivity": removes the active-volcano deposit, grants mineral income and 25% progress on the volcano tech.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

唯一选项“令人着迷”。

Single option "Fascinating".

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

Cosmic Storms DLC；每国一次。

Cosmic Storms DLC; once per country.
