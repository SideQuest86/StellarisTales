## 如何开始 How it starts

迷信者（Superstitious Beliefs）文明的周期事件触发：每个13年周期开始（首个周期除外）掷出吉或凶（shroud.210/220/230 → 410/415，events/shroud_events.txt:961-1221），再从 on_action 的16个等权事件中抽取一个（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5749-5789）；本事件每个帝国至多一次（国家旗标 shroud_700_triggered）。凶兆事件。无额外条件（正文分集群/非集群）。

Triggered by the cycles of a Superstitious Beliefs empire: each 13-year cycle (except the first) rolls good or bad luck (shroud.210/220/230 → 410/415, events/shroud_events.txt:961-1221) and one of the 16 equally weighted events of that on_action is drawn (common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5749-5789); this event happens at most once per empire (country flag shroud_700_triggered). A bad luck event. No further condition (text varies for gestalts).
