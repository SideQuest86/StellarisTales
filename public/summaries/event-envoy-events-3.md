# 星海段子手 / A Contribution to Galactic Comedy

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

Galactic Community 特使的随机事件：五年脉冲（action.2211）的负面列表 on_five_year_random_pulse_country_negative_list，权重见分支栏（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:576-592）；on_action 会先检查事件触发条件、不满足的从列表中剔除，所以只要有一个能触发就必有一个触发。通常共用5400天的 recent_envoy_event 冷却。 本事件：帝国是共同体成员，且其特使在理事会（或不在理事会）任职；无 recent_envoy_event；权重20/180。

A random Galactic Community envoy event: the negative list of the five-year pulse (action.2211), on_five_year_random_pulse_country_negative_list, weights in the branches (common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:576-592); on_actions check each event's trigger first and drop the failing ones, so as long as any can fire one always does. Most share a 5400-day recent_envoy_event cooldown. This event: The empire is a Community member and its envoy sits on (or off) the Council; no recent_envoy_event; weight 20 of 180.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

特使闹出外交笑话。选项：不予理睬（军国/排外/威权/集群，且非和平）或召回（其余；获得3600天的 galactic_community_diplo_corps_is_a_joke 修正）；选“召回重训”则特使被撤职并获得“忧郁”特质。

The envoy commits a diplomatic gaffe. Options: ignore it (militarist / xenophobe / authoritarian / gestalt, non-pacifist) or recall (others; the 3600-day modifier galactic_community_diplo_corps_is_a_joke); retraining unassigns the envoy and gives the Melancholic trait.
