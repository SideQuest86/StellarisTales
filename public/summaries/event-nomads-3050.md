## 如何开始 How it starts

游牧帝国承接合约 contract_prisoner_disposal（common/missions/missions/02_contracts.txt），方舟舰到达指定星系时触发。

A nomadic empire accepts the contract_prisoner_disposal contract (common/missions/missions/02_contracts.txt); events fire when its arkship reaches the flagged systems.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

游牧方承接“囚犯处置”合约：在接人星球收到3050，决定如何处置囚犯；在别处放下囚犯时（on_arkship_drop_off_prisoners → 3051）收到“囚犯离舰”3052，一天后“离舰报告”3053。

The nomad takes a prisoner disposal contract: at the pickup world it gets 3050 and decides how to handle the prisoners; dropping them off elsewhere (on_arkship_drop_off_prisoners -> 3051) gives Prisoners Disembarked 3052, and a day later the Disembarkment Report 3053.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

3050 的选项取决于是否奴隶制；3053 按是否被合约方或他国察觉分三种结果。

3050's options depend on slavery; 3053 has three results by whether the issuer or others detected it.
