## 如何开始 How it starts

不是随机抽取的裂隙（`randomized = no`，标记 `crystal_rift`）：它只由“研究水晶球”情境完成时的事件 astral_situation.5 在水晶球星系生成（`astral_planes_situation_events.txt:61–71`；情境 `common/situations/06_astral_planes_situations.txt:268`）。探索它的是被派去的科学家，探球一到就被另一侧的存在抓住，收回装置失效。

Not a randomly drawn rift (`randomized = no`, flag `crystal_rift`): it is spawned in the crystal sphere system only by astral_situation.5 when the “Study the Crystal Sphere” situation completes (`astral_planes_situation_events.txt:61–71`; situation `common/situations/06_astral_planes_situations.txt:268`). On arrival the Sphere is seized by something on the far side and its retraction device is disabled.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 理解（1000）：用人工智能检测隐藏规律（→ 1005 家园），或花 1000 能量币对锚链释放电磁脉冲（→ 1010 重获控制）。
2. 1010：提供人工智能支援（→ 1006 家园，难度较高）或赶紧逃离（→ 1025 逃离，结束）。
3. 家园（1005 / 1006）：保存粒子或场操控数据（各给一项研究选项）→ 升腾（1015）：保存物理学或社会学数据 → 碎裂（1020）。
4. 碎裂（1020）：打开传送门，或封印它（需虫洞稳定科技与 500 星界丝缕 → 1030 封印，结束）。

1. Understanding (1000): use A.I. to check for hidden patterns (→ 1005 Home), or spend 1000 energy on an E.M.P. through the anchor cord (→ 1010 Taking Control).
2. 1010: provide A.I. assistance (→ 1006 Home, higher difficulty) or get out at once (→ 1025 Escape, end).
3. Home (1005 / 1006): preserve the particle or field-manipulation data (each gives a research option) → Ascendance (1015): preserve physics or society data → Shatter (1020).
4. Shatter (1020): open the doorway, or seal it (needs Wormhole Stabilization and 500 astral threads → 1030 The Seal, end).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 1025 逃离：大量凝聚力与星界丝缕。
- 1030 封印：该星系获得矿藏 d_crystal_rift。
- 打开传送门（1020.a）：立即结束探索；2 天后隐藏事件 1095 在银河核心生成形态之系统，用天然虫洞取代裂隙并销毁它，探索帝国获得标记 opened_wormhole_to_formless_system，再过 1 天出现“古怪虫洞”（astral_planes.45）。此后与形态之物的故事由其他故事线承接。

- 1025 Escape: a large unity income and astral threads.
- 1030 The Seal: the system gains the deposit d_crystal_rift.
- Opening the doorway (1020.a): the exploration ends at once; 2 days later hidden event 1095 spawns the Formless system at the galactic core, replaces the rift with a natural wormhole and destroys it, the explorer gets flag opened_wormhole_to_formless_system, and a day later “Strange Wormhole” (astral_planes.45) appears. The later Formless story continues elsewhere.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 1000 的正文对格式塔有变体；电磁脉冲选项需要 1000 能量币。
- 1020.b 需要虫洞稳定科技与 500 星界丝缕（花费）；AI 对灵性帝国不选封印，对排外且已具备条件的帝国不选打开传送门。

- The text of 1000 has a gestalt variant; the E.M.P. option needs 1000 energy.
- 1020.b needs Wormhole Stabilization and 500 astral threads (spent); spiritualist AI empires do not seal it, xenophobic AI empires that can afford it do not open the doorway.
