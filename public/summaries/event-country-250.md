# 追寻风信子号 / The Hunt for the Hyacinth

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

与人类相关的剧情（human_2旗标，来自人类故事线）之后，country.250 随机弹出：星图提示三个附近星系可能藏着“风信子号”。接受后三个星系被设为兴趣点；也可拒绝并结束。

After the human story flag (human_2) is set, country.250 fires at random: a star chart hints that three nearby systems may hide the "Hyacinth". Accepting marks the three systems as points of interest; declining ends the chain.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

勘测三个标记星系中的每一个（country.251/252/253，或无船版本 country.259→260）。前两个只清除标记；第三个会在20到50距离处生成独立的风信子号星系，并设新的兴趣点。舰队进入该星系触发 country.254，启用“登上风信子号”项目，成功后 country.255 登船并给出标本，随后 country.256 的通讯对话，选第五项进入 country.257“盖棺定论”，事件链结束并获得社会研究。

Survey each marked system (country.251/252/253, or the shipless version country.259 -> 260). The first two only clear their markers; the third spawns the Hyacinth's own system 20-50 away with a new point of interest. A fleet entering it triggers country.254 and the "Board the Hyacinth" project; on success country.255 gives the boarding and a specimen, then the transmission dialogue country.256; the fifth reply leads to country.257 which ends the chain with a society research bonus.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

无结局分支；country.256 前四项为纯对话。

No ending branches; the first four replies of country.256 are pure dialogue.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

人类玩家、human_2旗标、非“破碎枷锁”起源。

Human player, the human_2 flag, not the Broken Shackles origin.
