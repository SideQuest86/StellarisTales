# 纯净：政府改革？ / Purity: Government Reformation?

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

蜂巢意识完成“纯净”传统树时，tr_purity_finish 的 on_enabled 触发 bio.185（common/traditions/01_purity.txt:20-33）。非蜂巢帝国在同一时刻触发的是 bio.170/175/180（另一故事）。

When a hive mind finishes the Purity tradition tree, the on_enabled of tr_purity_finish fires bio.185 (common/traditions/01_purity.txt:20-33). Non-hive empires get bio.170/175/180 instead (another story).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单页事件：完成纯净传统后，询问是否让政体随之改变。

One window: after finishing the Purity tree, the hive is asked whether to reform its government accordingly.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

选择 a 设置 bio_purity 标记并把权力机构改为纯净类型（工具提示 GetPurityAuthority）；b 维持原状。

Option a sets bio_purity and changes the authority to the Purity type (tooltip GetPurityAuthority); b keeps the government.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

这些传统树通常经由“基因重塑”情势的完成事件 bio.40 采纳；本事件没有后续窗口。

These trees are normally adopted through the Biomorphosis completion event bio.40; this event has no follow-up window.
