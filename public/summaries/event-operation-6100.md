## 如何开始 How it starts

星海帝国中的行动 operation_spark_rebellion（煽动叛乱）：成本影响力250，维持能量14，间谍力70，五个难度 T7 的阶段；需帝国权威≤50，且当前没有帝国内战；执行者是共同体成员而非皇帝，目标是皇帝。一个时段结束时若帝国权威回升到80以上则行动取消。

The Imperium operation operation_spark_rebellion ("Spark Rebellion"): cost 250 influence, upkeep 14 energy, spy power 70, five tier-7 stages; needs Imperial authority <= 50 and no civil war running; the performer is a Community member who is not the emperor, the target is the emperor. The operation is cancelled if Imperial authority climbs back above 80 when a period ends.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

阶段 6100“联络”→ 6105“消除威胁”→ 6110“布置人手”→ 6115“最后准备”→ 6120“准备就绪”（选“开始行动”）→ emperor.101“反叛的呼声！”：触发帝国内战——叛军（emperor.108 / 103 / 106 / 107）与效忠者（emperor.104 “最后抵抗”）各按所处阵营收到窗口。

Stages 6100 "Linking Up" -> 6105 "Threat Neutralization" -> 6110 "Positioning Assets" -> 6115 "Final Preparations" -> 6120 "Ready to Commence" (choose "Commence Operation!") -> emperor.101 "Cry of Rebellion!": the Imperium civil war starts — rebels (emperor.108 / 103 / 106 / 107) and loyalists (emperor.104 "Last Stand") each get windows by their side.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

每个阶段窗口都有“静待时机”选项（推迟）；开始后的皇帝战争线分支（emperor.103 的两个选项、106/107）。

Each stage window has a "bide our time" option (postpone); after launch the emperor war branches (the two options of emperor.103, 106/107).
