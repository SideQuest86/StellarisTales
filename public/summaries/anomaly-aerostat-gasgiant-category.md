- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration

## 如何开始 How it starts

气态巨行星可能生成（1级，权重3）。

Can spawn on a gas giant (level 1, weight 3).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

两个结果各占50%（on_success 1/1）：anomaly.310“社会实验”（获得每月社会研究加成；演化掠食者起源另有 DNA/情境进度选项）；anomaly.315“浮空殖民地”（清除原有矿藏，改为 d_engineering_3 工程研究矿藏并加 aerostat_colonies 修正）。

Two results, 50% each (on_success 1/1): anomaly.310 "Social Experiment" (monthly society research; Evolutionary Predators get an extra DNA/situation-progress option) and anomaly.315 "Aerostat Colonies" (existing deposits cleared unless a research-station deposit exists, adds a d_engineering_3 deposit and the aerostat_colonies modifier).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

The results are exclusive and there is no further chapter; story.5 (shared report, 30 days later) is a helper.
