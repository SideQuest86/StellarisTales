# 走私人口 / Smuggle Population

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

拥有 BioGenesis DLC 时，可对拥有500以上可偷运人口的普通或原始国家发动谍报行动“走私人口”；双方都是狂热平等主义时不可发动（operations.txt:690-768）。

With BioGenesis, the espionage operation Smuggle Population can target a regular or primitive country with more than 500 smugglable pops; not allowed if both sides are fanatic egalitarian (operations.txt:690-768).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

三个阶段依次弹出：人才获取 bio.900 → 隐身行动 bio.905 → 穿梭机 bio.910；“开始行动”一天后，走私 bio.915 结算并结束行动。阶段检定失败时转入通用谍报随机事件（另一故事）。

Three stages in order: Talent Acquisition bio.900 -> Disappearing Acts bio.905 -> Shuttles bio.910; one day after "Commence Operation", Smuggled Out bio.915 resolves and ends the operation. Failed rolls go to the generic operation random events (another story).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

bio.915 四个选项互斥：只带走无人看管的人口；优先解救本物种（目标中本物种超过100时）；在已有政府资产时冒险多带人口并暴露；或两者兼具。

bio.915 has four exclusive options: take only unattended pops; prioritise our own species (if more than 100 live there); with a government asset, push for more at the cost of exposure; or both.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

bio.915 正文按偷运规模（超过4则“已被察觉”）以及我方是否拥有宇宙创生世界分为四版。

bio.915 text has four versions: by smuggled amount (over 4 means detected) and by whether we own a Cosmogenesis world.
