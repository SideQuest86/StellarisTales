# 过去的碎片 / Fragments of the Past

## 如何开始 How it starts

从冠军之铸雇佣英杰“塞拉伦涅”后720–840天，“记忆的遗踪”nomads.1585 开启事件链“过去的碎片”，并在某星系标出线索（L9311-9455）。

Seralune hired from the Champion's Forge: 720-840 days later Traces of Memory nomads.1585 starts the chain "Fragments of the Past" and marks a clue system (L9311-9455).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 每次带着塞拉伦涅的舰队进入线索星系，都会推进一步记忆：第一次随机得到“缅怀的边缘”1595（仍想不起）或“家园之景”1600；之后依次可能出现“童年的回声”1605与“回收的碎片”1610。
  Each time a fleet with Seralune enters the clue system her memory advances: the first visit randomly gives Edge of Remembrance 1595 (still forgetting) or View of Home 1600; later visits can give Echo of Childhood 1605 and Recovered Fragment 1610.
- 在1600选择 b，或在1610，都会开启“毁坏的舰船”项目；完成后“幸存的继承人”1620 开启“修复方舟舰”项目，完成即“重返过去”1625。
  Option b in 1600, or 1610, enables the Ruined Vessel project; its completion gives The Surviving Heir 1620, which enables Restore the Arkship; completing it gives Past Reclaimed 1625.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

两种结局互斥：修复完成（1625），或她在此前死亡（1630，事件链结束）。

Two exclusive endings: restoration (1625), or her death before it (1630; the chain ends).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

“家园之景”对人类物种另有正文。

View of Home has a separate text for human species.
