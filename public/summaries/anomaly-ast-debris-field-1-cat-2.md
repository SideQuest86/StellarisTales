## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。小行星可能生成（3级，权重3，每个帝国一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on asteroids (level 3, weight 3, once per empire).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

结果三选一（各基础权重1）：
- distar.1080“无声狂啸”（已有克隆科技时权重×3；格式塔或 mol8 头像为0）：冰块中封着一只巨型腹足类化石。有克隆科技可立即克隆 → ICE_ALIEN（1000社会学）→ distar.1081“阿兹詹虫”；没有则可储存DNA，日后研究出克隆时 distar.1085 再问是否复活。也可扔掉或送进博物馆。distar.1081：做劳工（4个前智慧人口组）→ 之后每月20%几率 distar.1084“阿兹詹虫的智慧”（它们变为完全智慧）；进动物园（宠物矿藏）→ 每月20%几率 distar.1083 逃出暴走（星球受损并出现危险野生动物）；编入军队；或处理掉。
- distar.1050“吃或被吃”（机械/合成帝国为0）：一艘美食探险船被吃掉，它的“美味排行榜”让我们与“最美味的外星人”建立联系。
- distar.2000“粉碎星球”（星球已有站点矿藏时为0）：被热能束击碎的星球留下高品质矿物（矿物矿藏）。

One of three results (base weight 1 each):
- distar.1080 "Silent Shout" (×3 with Cloning; 0 for gestalts or portrait mol8): a gigantic gastropod fossil frozen in ice. With Cloning it can be cloned at once → ICE_ALIEN (1000 society) → distar.1081 "Azizians"; without it the DNA can be stored, and distar.1085 asks again once Cloning is researched. It can also be spaced or sent to a museum. distar.1081: laborers (4 pre-sapient pop groups) → monthly 20% chance of distar.1084 "Azizian Wisdom" (they become fully sapient); zoo (pet deposits) → monthly 20% chance of distar.1083, a rampage (devastation and dangerous wildlife); army; or disposal.
- distar.1050 "Eat or be Eaten" (0 for machine/synthetic empires): a gourmet expedition ship was eaten; its taste rankings put us in contact with "the galaxy's most delicious aliens".
- distar.2000 "Shattered World" (0 if the body has station deposits): a planet shattered by thermal beams left rich minerals (minerals deposit).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

三个异常结果互斥；distar.1081 四个处置互斥，只有劳工与动物园有后续（1084 / 1083）。

The three anomaly results are exclusive; distar.1081's four choices are exclusive and only laborers/zoo have follow-ups (1084 / 1083).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

克隆科技决定 distar.1080 的正文和可用选项；演化猎手在 1080、1081、1050 有专属选项；distar.1050 对石质物种的排名措辞不同。

Cloning decides distar.1080's text and options; Evolutionary Predators have extra options in 1080, 1081 and 1050; distar.1050 words the ranking differently for lithoids.
