## 如何开始 How it starts

潜入虚境但没有找到其他事件（on_patron_delve_failed）时，从28个权重各为100的遭遇（另有权重1的 utopia.3002 兜底）中随机抽取一个满足条件的；每次潜入失败只出现一个遭遇，不会依次经历全部。要求曾潜入虚境、尚未与四位主要赞助者中任何一位建立接触，并持有至少2000能量与500泽珞；20年内不重复。

When a Shroud delve finds nothing else (on_patron_delve_failed), one eligible encounter is drawn at random from 28 candidates of weight 100 each (plus utopia.3002 at weight 1 as fallback); each failed delve shows one encounter, never all of them in sequence. Requires earlier delves, no contact yet with any of the four major patrons, and at least 2,000 energy and 500 zro; not again within 20 years.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

我们感到庞大力量的共鸣，可用泽珞直接接触其中一位。

We feel the resonance of vast powers; zro would let our telepaths reach one of them directly.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

四个选项分别对应织缕、喰煞、器具、月窠（尚未接触者才显示）：各花费2000能量与500泽珞，对该赞助者契合度+400，然后 3125“遥远回声”（共享结局窗口）。也可直接离开。

Four options for the Composer, Eater, Instrument and Cradle (shown only if not yet contacted): each costs 2,000 energy and 500 zro, gives +400 attunement to that patron, then 3125 (shared closing window). Or leave.
