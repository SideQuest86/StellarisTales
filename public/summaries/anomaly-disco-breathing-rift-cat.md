# 光学现象 / Light Phenomenon

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：无DLC检查（定义位于《遥远繁星》文件中，但类别与事件都不检查 has_distar）/ no DLC check (defined in the Distant Stars files, but neither category nor events test has_distar)

## 如何开始 How it starts

黑洞、中子星或脉冲星可能生成（3级，权重2）。

Can spawn on black holes, neutron stars or pulsars (level 3, weight 2).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.3060“呼吸的裂隙”：天体附近的空间裂隙像在呼吸光子，可能也吸进过别的东西。派探测器监视 → breathing_rift_project（1000，无时限）→ 50% distar.3061（吐出富含矿物的小行星碎块）/ 50% distar.3062（吐出金属残骸）。之后每次收集，100–200天后再次随机：25% 矿物、25% 残骸、或100–150天后50% distar.3064“关闭”（裂隙消失，留下物理学研究）。也可远距离研究或离开。

distar.3060 "Breathing Rift": a rift near the body seems to breathe photons in and out, and may have swallowed other things. Monitor it → breathing_rift_project (1000, no limit) → 50% distar.3061 (mineral-laden asteroid fragments) / 50% distar.3062 (metal debris). After each collection another roll follows: 25% minerals and 25% debris after 100–200 days, or 50% distar.3064 "Closed" after 100–150 days (the rift vanishes; physics research). It can also be studied from afar or avoided.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

循环结构：3061/3062 可重复出现，直到 3064 关闭裂隙；每次三选一互斥。

A loop: 3061/3062 can repeat until 3064 closes the rift; each roll is exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

3061、3062 各有“首次”与“再次”两段正文。

3061 and 3062 each have a first-time and a repeat text.
