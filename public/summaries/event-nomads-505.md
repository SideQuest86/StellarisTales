# 熟悉的猎兵 / Familiar Hunters

- 分类 Category：C1 起源 / C1 Origins · DLC: Nomads

## 如何开始 How it starts

仅限可汗后裔起源：只要尚未在决斗中称汗，nomads.500 每200–300天派出一批赏金猎兵（nomads.505），玩家收到“熟悉的猎兵”506（L3701-4160）。

Heirs of the Khan only: until the heir wins the Khan duel, nomads.500 sends a band of bounty hunters every 200-300 days (nomads.505), and the player gets Familiar Hunters 506 (L3701-4160).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

之后两种收场：被猎兵截住时，若此前已约定“遇敌谈判”，他们发来“讨价还价”570（付过路费、威胁、或买断合约）；若猎兵舰队被打瘫，则发来“聆听请求”555（放走、拒绝投降，或按此前约定的条件勒索货物、船员、海军上将等）。

Two ways it ends: if they catch us and a negotiation had been agreed, they send A Bargain 570 (pay the toll, defy them, or buy out their contract); if their fleet is disabled they send A Plea 555 (let them go, refuse surrender, or extort cargo, crew, an admiral and so on depending on earlier choices).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

每批猎兵只会走其中一条；猎兵会周期性再来，直到称汗。

Each band takes only one path; bands keep coming until the heir becomes Khan.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

两份对话的正文与可选条件取决于此前谈判留下的标记（heirs_bargain_* / heirs_option_*）。

The texts and options of both dialogues depend on flags left by earlier bargains (heirs_bargain_* / heirs_option_*).
