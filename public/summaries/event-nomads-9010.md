## 如何开始 How it starts

游戏开始60–260天后（game_start.1），拥有 human_3 标记的玩家帝国收到“往日之声”nomads.9000：风信子号截获尤利西斯计划第五艘船“秋菊号”的旧求救信号（L17902-17935）。

At 60-260 days into the game (game_start.1), a player empire with the human_3 flag receives A Voice from the Past nomads.9000: the Hyacinth picks up an old distress call from the Chrysanthemum, the fifth Ulysses Initiative ship (L17902-17935).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

选择追踪信号后开启事件链：风信子号舰队到达中继星系发现浮标（“发现信号”9005），再前往天津四发现殖民地遗迹（“合一”9010），可创建考古遗址“联合星殖民地残骸”，三个阶段依次为“白热化”9015、“投票”9020、“日志”9025。

Following the signal starts the chain: the Hyacinth fleet finds the relay buoy (Signal Found 9005), then the colony remains at Deneb (Unity 9010), which can open the dig site Unity Colony Ruins with three stages: The Escalation 9015, The Vote 9020, The Logs 9025.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

在9000选择 b 只记录信号，不开启后续（事件链仍挂在帝国上，但没有舰队标记推进）。

Option b in 9000 only logs the signal (the chain is begun, but nothing advances it).
