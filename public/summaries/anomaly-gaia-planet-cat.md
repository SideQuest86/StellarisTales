## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。盖亚星球可能生成（3级，权重3，全局一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on Gaia planets (level 3, weight 3, once per game).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.1060“新婚情侣”：一种无毛哺乳类外星人把这里当作蜜月海滩，被我方飞船惊走后留下垃圾和一本外星旅行指南《银河系漫游指南》。所有选项都给予社会学研究；亲外选择鼓舞人民（10年修正），排外选择宣传它们是乱扔垃圾的累赘（10年修正）。

distar.1060 "Honeymooners": hairless mammalian aliens use the planet as a honeymoon beach, flee our ship and leave litter and an alien travel guide, "A Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy". Every option gives society research; xenophiles can make it an inspiration (10-year modifier), xenophobes a warning about littering aliens (10-year modifier).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

Single result; options depend on ethics.

Xenophile: inspire or keep quiet; xenophobe: denounce or keep quiet; others: "Gross!" or "Fascinating".
