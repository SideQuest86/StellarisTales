# 缺失质量 / Missing Mass

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：Shadows of the Shroud（has_shroud_dlc）

## 如何开始 How it starts

无矿藏的荒芜或寒冷荒芜星球可能生成（4级，权重3；需要 has_shroud_dlc）。

Can spawn on a barren or cold barren planet with no deposit (level 4, weight 3; needs has_shroud_dlc).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

单一结果 extreme_frontiers.230“深地幔环节动物”：选项a（排除亲外星帝国）每月矿物加成与 d_minerals_5 矿藏；选项b（非杀戮帝国）每月社会研究加成与 d_society_5 矿藏。描述对“土食”岩石蜂群有专门变体。

Single result extreme_frontiers.230 "Deep Mantle Annelids": option a (not for xenophile empires) monthly minerals and a d_minerals_5 deposit; option b (non-homicidal) monthly society research and a d_society_5 deposit. The description has a variant for Terravore lithoid swarms.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

唯一结果；选项a/b二选一。

Single outcome; options a/b are alternatives.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

230 的选项a禁止亲外星（xenophile）帝国，选项b禁止杀戮帝国。

230's option a is disallowed for xenophile empires and option b for homicidal empires.
