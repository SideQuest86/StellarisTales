## 如何开始 How it starts

“控制”碎片存在、我方和平且有该碎片尚未拥有的有机物种人口时，五年随机脉冲可能抽中 bio.3300，立即弹出 bio.3305。触发后设置2560天的蜂巢失落帝国事件冷却（hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown），期间其他碎片要求不再出现。

With the Control fragment present, our empire at peace and organic pops of a species it does not yet own, the five-year pulse can pick bio.3300, which shows bio.3305 at once. Firing sets a 2560-day cooldown (hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown) shared by the fragment demands.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

控制碎片的预录信息要求交出一名该物种成员供其“同化”。服从后，该物种一个人口迁往碎片首都，碎片还以该物种生成新人口和一名官员。360–720天后可能收到来自那名被带走者的“信号传输接入”。

A pre-recorded message from the Control fragment demands one member of that species for "assimilation". Complying moves one pop to the fragment's capital, where it also grows new pops and an official of that species. 360-720 days later a transmission from the taken individual may arrive.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

bio.3310：被同化者宣告我方物种基因已被收录（“熟悉面孔”公民性另有回答）。bio.3315：被同化者冒险传出数据包，接受时50%为真的失落帝国建筑，50%为试探并惹怒碎片。拒绝 bio.3305 则碎片不满，无后续。

bio.3310: the assimilated one announces our genome has been secured (Familiar Face hives get their own reply). bio.3315: they risk sending a data packet; accepting gives a 50% chance of a genuine Fallen Empire building, otherwise it was a test and angers the fragment. Refusing bio.3305 only displeases the fragment.
