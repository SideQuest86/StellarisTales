# 困于风暴的舰船 / Stormbound

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/cosmic_storms_events_1.txt:1726-2036; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:5404-5439; common/special_projects/03_projects_cosmic_storms.txt (project_cosmic_storms_stormbound) · DLC: Cosmic Storms · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

风暴离开某个星系时，有5/105的概率抽中 cstorms.1310“被困舰船再现”（仅一次，仅人类玩家），开启“困于风暴的舰船”事件链并给物理研究。

When a storm leaves a system there is a 5/105 chance of cstorms.1310 "Stormbound Rises" (once only, human players), which starts the "Stormbound" chain and grants physics research.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

800天后设置标记。之后任一风暴离开我方有研究站的星系时出现 cstorms.1313“被困舰船回归”；选“继续聆听”后，风暴离开有我方科学船的星系时出现 cstorms.1316“风暴中的低语”；再有风暴进入我方星系时 cstorms.1317 生成“被困舰船”并启用“风暴之魂”项目；项目完成 cstorms.1320“归乡之路”，约150±60天后 cstorms.1321“来自高维的讯息”，给最终奖励（可附带“流形之信”修正）并结束事件链。

800 days later a flag is set. Whenever a storm then leaves a system with our research station, cstorms.1313 "Stormbound Returns" appears; if we choose to keep listening, a storm leaving a system with our science ship shows cstorms.1316 "Whispers in the Tempest"; a storm entering one of our systems then makes cstorms.1317 spawn the stormbound ship and enable the "Spirits of the Storm" project; its completion shows cstorms.1320 "Homebound", and 150+-60 days later cstorms.1321 "Letters From The Manifold" gives the final reward (optionally with the "Letters of the Manifold" modifier) and ends the chain.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

cstorms.1313 可以直接选择结束事件链（获得团结），否则继续；cstorms.1321 的两个选项区别仅在于是否获得“流形之信”修正。

cstorms.1313 can end the chain at once (unity reward); otherwise it continues. The two options of cstorms.1321 differ only by whether the "Letters of the Manifold" modifier is added.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

Cosmic Storms DLC；需要研究站、科学船和风暴的位置条件，皆由 on_action 触发。

Cosmic Storms DLC; research station, science ship and storm positions, all via on_actions.
