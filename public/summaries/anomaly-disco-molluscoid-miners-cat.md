## 如何开始 How it starts

无战略资源的沙漠、干旱或草原星球可能生成（3级，权重2）——但第二个修正写作 factor 0 对 NOR{格式塔、狂热排外、mol6头像} 生效，即只有格式塔、狂热排外或使用 mol6 头像的帝国才可能遇到。这看起来与预期相反，记录为原文现状。

Can spawn on desert, arid or savannah planets without strategic resources (level 3, weight 2) — but the second modifier applies factor 0 to NOR {gestalt, fanatic xenophobe, mol6 portrait}, so only gestalts, fanatic xenophobes or mol6-portrait empires can get it. This looks inverted but is recorded as written.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.3030“软体矿工”：半智慧的软体生物以展示挖到的矿物炫耀地位，总能找到大矿脉。研究 → molluscoid_miners_project_1（60天）→ distar.3031：它们用伸入土壤的粘液丝感知地下矿脉。整合它们（非杀戮型）→ molluscoid_miners_project_2（3000）→ distar.3032：成为顺从勤劳的人口；只用知识 → molluscoid_miners_project_3（2000）→ distar.3033“矿物测绘”；开采它们的星球；演化猎手可收获DNA。distar.3030 也可直接开采矿藏。

distar.3030 "Molluscoid Miners": semi-sapient molluscs display unearthed minerals for status and always find large veins. Study → molluscoid_miners_project_1 (60 days) → distar.3031: they sense veins through slime strands seeping into the soil. Integrate them (non-homicidal) → molluscoid_miners_project_2 (3000) → distar.3032: obedient, hardworking pops; use the knowledge → molluscoid_miners_project_3 (2000) → distar.3033 "Mineral Mapping"; mine their planet; Evolutionary Predators may harvest their DNA. distar.3030 can also mine their minerals at once.
