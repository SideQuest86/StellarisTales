# 被奴役 / Enslaved

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7) · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12
- 来源 Sources：`events/first_contact_dlc_events.txt:9238-9331`；`common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:468, 538-560`（on_five_year_random_pulse_pre_ftl）；`events/on_action_events_2.txt:3146-3179`（action.620/621）

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每五年的国家检查运行 action.620：拥有“首次接触”DLC、且与某个前超光速文明有生效协议的普通帝国，会在 0–360 天后抽取“前超光速外交事件池”（无事 200，本事件 10，条件不满足的事件被跳过）。
  The five-year country pulse runs action.620: a regular empire with the First Contact DLC and an active agreement with a pre-FTL civilization draws from the pre-FTL diplomacy pool 0-360 days later (nothing 200, this event 10; events whose conditions fail are skipped).
- 条件：与非“挣脱桎梏”母族的前超光速文明有贸易协议；银河中存在威权的 AI 太空帝国；每个帝国一次。
  Conditions: a trade agreement with a pre-FTL that is not our Broken Shackles parent; an authoritarian AI spacefaring empire exists; once per empire.
- 发生后设置“近期前超光速外交事件”冷却，其他此类事件暂不出现。
  Afterwards a "recent pre-FTL diplomatic event" cooldown blocks the other events of this pool for a while.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 8045「被奴役」：他国奴隶贩子冒充商人，利用我们的贸易协议掳走当地居民。
  8045 "Enslaved": slavers of another empire pose as merchants under our trade deal and abduct the locals.
- 选项：营救被掳者（需 100 影响力与 5000 能量）；“与我们无关”（一名当地人口被迁往奴隶贩子首都）；（允许奴隶制）向他们收税（同样迁走一人口）。单页事件。
  Options: rescue the abductees (needs 100 influence and 5000 energy); "none of our business" (one pop is moved to the slavers' capital); (slavery allowed) tax them (the same resettlement). A single window.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 这是可重复抽取的随机外交插曲之一（各事件本身每个帝国通常一次），与同池其他事件没有固定顺序。
  One of the random diplomacy interludes of this pool (each usually once per empire), with no fixed order relative to the others.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 第三选项要求允许奴隶制。另见 8050（同名“被奴役”，奴役者是前超光速文明一方）。
  Option three needs slavery allowed. See also 8050 (same title, where the pre-FTL are the slavers).
