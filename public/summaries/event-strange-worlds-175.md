# 地域仇恨 / Interdistrict Hostility

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/strange_worlds_events.txt:2165-2600; common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3735 on_colony_1_year_old (random 20/510) · DLC: BioGenesis · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满1年时随机事件池以20/510抽中 strange_worlds.175（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3735），非蜂巢、非机器帝国；strange_worlds.180 每360天检查，直到殖民地已建成至少2种不同区划（且不是首都/奴隶/度假指定），触发 strange_worlds.185。

A colony turning 1 year old can draw strange_worlds.175 (20/510, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3735), not for hive or machine empires; strange_worlds.180 re-checks every 360 days until the colony has built at least 2 different district types (and is not a capital, slave or resort designation), then fires strange_worlds.185.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

strange_worlds.185“地域仇恨”（城乡对立）：玩家选支持城市、支持乡村或增派执法；720天后 strange_worlds.190“水中疑云”（根源是地幔里巨型生物尸体的细菌）：a“过滤地下水”——540天后 strange_worlds.195“新兴能力”；b“尝试基因改造细菌”——360天后 strange_worlds.200“细菌重测序”（三选一：强化繁殖、力量或认知）。

strange_worlds.185 "Interdistrict Hostility" (rural against urban): support the urban districts, the rural districts or deploy enforcement; 720 days later strange_worlds.190 "Something in the Water" (bacteria from a giant corpse in the mantle): a "Filter the groundwater" - 540 days later strange_worlds.195 "Emergent Abilities"; b "Attempt to genetically alter the bacteria" - 360 days later strange_worlds.200 "Bacterial Resequencing" (choose reproduction, strength or cognition).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

190 的 a（195）与 b（200）互斥。

In 190, a (195) and b (200) are exclusive.
