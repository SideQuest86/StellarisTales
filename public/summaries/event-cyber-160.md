## 如何开始 How it starts

- 网络化局势（Cyberization）每月运行隐藏事件 cyber.5，从 on_cyberization_situation_random_events_list 中等权重（各1）抽取一个满足条件的随机事件（共7个：120、160、210、220、260、320、360）；事件期间设置 situation_cyberization_pause 暂停局势，结束后移除。
  The Cyberization situation runs hidden cyber.5 monthly, which draws one valid event with equal weight (1 each) from on_cyberization_situation_random_events_list (seven events: 120, 160, 210, 220, 260, 320, 360); the event sets situation_cyberization_pause while open and removes it afterwards.
- 触发：局势进度>450；每局至多一次（cyber_160_happened）。
  Trigger: situation progress > 450; at most once (cyber_160_happened).

  "Cybernetic Dissociation" (challenge): a hit drama stirs public fear of implants. Options: PR campaign (researcher_media_relations 180 days); tighten regulation (situation progress speed -50% for 6 months); let it blow over (bad_publicity 360 days); censor the drama (authoritarian, cyber_state_censorship); block the news (media conglomerate, 1000 energy, media_blockade).
