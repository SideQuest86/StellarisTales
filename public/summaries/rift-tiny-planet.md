## 如何开始 How it starts

从探索帝国的裂隙池按权重抽取，权重 1，无条件，无失败分支（`00_astral_rifts.txt:366–375`）。每阶段结束掷骰，≥14 进入下一章。

Drawn by weight from the explorer's rift pool, weight 1, no conditions, no failure branch (`00_astral_rifts.txt:366–375`). Each phase ends with a roll; 14+ opens the next chapter.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 小小星球（2800）→ 月面冲击（2805，探球的引力撞上了卫星）→ 袖珍问题（2810）：星球上的前 FTL 物种发现了探球。
2. 2810：建立联络 / 索要赎金 / 告诉他们末日要来（→ 2815 通讯建立）；被动观测 / 静观灾难（→ 2830 远距离观测，结束）。
3. 2815：修复卫星轨道（→ 2820 回到轨道 → 2835 突破潮汐锁定，结束）；摧毁卫星（→ 2825 永别卫星）；切断通讯（→ 2830）。
4. 2825：浅冲击（→ 2840，结束）或部署遥测设施（→ 2845 模型行星，结束；3600 天后 2890 袖珍行星大灭绝）。

1. It's a Small World (2800) → Lunar Impact (2805, the Sphere's gravity hits the moon) → A Small Problem (2810): the pre-FTL species notices the Sphere.
2. 2810: establish contact / demand ransom / announce doom (→ 2815 Communications Established); observe passively / watch the destruction (→ 2830 Observed From Afar, end).
3. 2815: fix the moon's orbit (→ 2820 Back in Orbit → 2835 Tidal Locking Breakthrough, end); destroy the moon (→ 2825 No More Moon); cut communications (→ 2830).
4. 2825: shallow impact (→ 2840, end) or deploy remote devices (→ 2845 Model Planet, end; 3600 days later 2890 Tiny Planet Extinction).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

互斥的结局：2830（社会矿藏 d_society_10）、2835（国家标记 tidal_lock_knowledge）、2840（标记 can_build_microplanet_memorial）、2845（矿藏 d_society_15，十年后被 2890 移除）。都结束探索并给予大量星界丝缕。

Exclusive endings: 2830 (society deposit d_society_10), 2835 (country flag tidal_lock_knowledge), 2840 (flag can_build_microplanet_memorial), 2845 (deposit d_society_15, removed ten years later by 2890). All finish the exploration with a large astral thread reward.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 不友善帝国在 2810 只能选“静观灾难扩散”，在 2815 只能切断通讯（d）。
- “野蛮掠夺者”公民多出“索要赎金”；狂暴灭绝者多出“告诉他们末日要来”。
- 修复轨道需非不友善；摧毁卫星需非和平主义且非亲异族。

- Unfriendly empires only get “Watch the destruction unfold” in 2810 and “Cut communications” (d) in 2815.
- The Barbaric Despoilers civic adds “Demand ransom”; homicidal empires add “Inform them their doom has come”.
- Fixing the orbit needs a non-unfriendly empire; destroying the moon needs a non-pacifist, non-xenophile one.
