# 货舱传出的噪音 / Noises from the Cargo Deck

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/nomads_events_1.txt:16297-16734; events/nomads_events_1.txt:15716-15763 · DLC: Nomads · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12

## 如何开始 How it starts

游牧帝国（非格式塔）执行资源类合约时，从合约事件池抽中“货舱传出的噪音”nomads.6020（只发生一次）。

A non-gestalt nomadic empire on a resource contract can draw Noises from the Cargo Deck nomads.6020 from the contract event pool (once only).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

货舱里传出怪声。选择 a 置之不理；选择 b 调查，10–30天后揭晓是六种情况之一：发光生物、超凶的猫、故障机械体、易爆气体、未登记的存在或未经授权的生物货物，各有处置选项。

Strange noises come from the hold. Option a ignores them; option b investigates and 10-30 days later reveals one of six: glowing critters, a feral feline, a malfunctioning mechanoid, combustive gases, an unregistered presence or unauthorized biological cargo, each with its own choices.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

六个结果互斥，各有一到三个处置选项（部分需要合金、贸易值或消费品）。

The six results are exclusive, each with one to three ways to deal with it (some need alloys, trade or consumer goods).
