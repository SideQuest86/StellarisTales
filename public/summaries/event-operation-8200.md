## 如何开始 How it starts

行动 operation_diplomatic_incident（引爆外交事故）：需要 Nemesis DLC、执行者通讯>3；成本影响力30，维持能量5，间谍力25，三个难度 T1 的阶段；目标是默认国家且不嗜杀、非集群，通讯>3，近期没有同类事件（operation_diplomatic_incident_timer）。

The operation operation_diplomatic_incident ("Stage a Diplomatic Incident"): needs Nemesis and the performer with more than 3 communications; cost 30 influence, upkeep 5 energy, spy power 25, three tier-1 stages; the target is a non-homicidal, non-gestalt default country with more than 3 communications and no recent incident (operation_diplomatic_incident_timer).
