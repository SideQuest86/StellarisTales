# 风扫行星 / Windswept Planet

- 分类 Category：星界裂隙 Astral rifts (C4) · DLC：星界位面 Astral Planes

## 如何开始 How it starts

从探索帝国的裂隙池按权重抽取，权重 1，无条件，无失败分支（`00_astral_rifts.txt:355–364`）。每阶段结束掷骰，≥14 进入下一章。

Drawn by weight from the explorer's rift pool, weight 1, no conditions, no failure branch (`00_astral_rifts.txt:355–364`). Each phase ends with a roll; 14+ opens the next chapter.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. 风扫行星（2700）：猛烈的风暴；升上风暴之上（→ 2705）或躲进洞穴（→ 2710）。
2. 风之线：平流层之上（2705）→ 活着的风（2715，风中有微生物）→ 真菌威胁（2725）：设法清除真菌（→ 2755）或调查真菌（→ 2735 真菌通讯 → 2745 真菌之辩护）。
3. 洞穴线：真菌洞穴（2710）→ 真菌之中（2720）→ 食肉之风（2730）：花 50 矿物保护生态系统（→ 2760）或研究风（→ 2740 当前事件 → 2750 狂风之辩护）。
4. 汇合：2745 / 2750 让玩家决定风与真菌的命运，通向四种结局（见下）。

1. Windswept Planet (2700): a fierce storm; rise above it (→ 2705) or shelter in the cave (→ 2710).
2. Wind line: Beyond the Stratosphere (2705) → Living Wind (2715, microorganisms in the wind) → Fungal Threat (2725): remove the fungus (→ 2755) or investigate it (→ 2735 Fungal Transmission → 2745 The Case for the Fungus).
3. Cave line: Fungal Cavern (2710) → Among the Fungus (2720) → Carnivorous Winds (2730): spend 50 minerals to protect the ecosystem (→ 2760) or study the wind (→ 2740 Current Events → 2750 The Case for the Wind).
4. Convergence: 2745 / 2750 let the player decide the fate of wind and fungus, leading to four outcomes (below).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

互斥的四种结局，都结束探索：
- 2755 镰鼬之风（加固风，25 合金）：解锁培育风生物。
- 2760 真菌绽放（滋养真菌，50 矿物）：标本“超维真菌”与修正。
- 2765 困难的协商（非狂暴灭绝者）：社会矿藏、奇异气体、能量、星界丝缕；非格式塔帝国两天后触发 2790 “特殊请求”，科学家可转任使节。
- 2770 生物灭绝（狂暴灭绝者）：食物、凝聚力、星界丝缕。
演化猎手起源在 2755/2760/2765/2770 另有专属选项，给予对应 DNA。

Four exclusive endings, all finishing the exploration:
- 2755 Cutting Winds (fortify the wind, 25 alloys): unlocks incubating wind creatures.
- 2760 Fungal Bloom (nourish the fungus, 50 minerals): the extradimensional fungus specimen and a modifier.
- 2765 A Difficult Compromise (non-homicidal): society deposit, exotic gases, energy, astral threads; non-gestalt empires get 2790 “A Special Request” two days later, where the scientist may become an envoy.
- 2770 Biological Eradication (homicidal): food, unity, astral threads.
Evolutionary Predators have an exclusive option in each outcome giving the matching DNA.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 2745 / 2750：折衷仅限非狂暴灭绝者，灭绝仅限狂暴灭绝者（AI 对后者有 +100 权重）；2745 正文对不友善帝国有变体。
- 2790：军国、排外、不友善帝国的 AI 倾向不转任。

- 2745 / 2750: compromise only for non-homicidal, eradication only for homicidal empires (AI weight +100 for the latter); 2745 text varies for unfriendly empires.
- 2790: militarist, xenophobe and unfriendly AI empires tend not to reassign.
