# 银河之星 / The Galatron

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

迦拉特隆是行商联盟圣物箱里极稀有的奖品（1/200，全银河仅一件）。持有者激活遗物 r_galatron 时弹出 cara.970；通过战争目标夺取或被夺取时分别弹出 cara.950 / cara.951。

The Galatron is the very rare Reliquary prize of the Coalition (1/200, a single one in the galaxy). Its holder activating the relic r_galatron gets cara.970; seizing or losing it through the war goals fires cara.950 / cara.951.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

三个互不相连的窗口：激活奖励（cara.970）、夺取（cara.950）、失去（cara.951）。另有背景脉冲 cara.960：持有 galatron_modifier 的国家有40%概率得到一份随机资源奖励或永久的海军容量+10。

Three unconnected windows: activation reward (cara.970), seizing (cara.950), losing (cara.951). A background pulse cara.960 also gives the holder (galatron_modifier) a 40% chance of a random resource gift or a permanent +10 naval capacity.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

无选择分支；970 的奖励为随机。

No choice branches; the reward of 970 is random.
