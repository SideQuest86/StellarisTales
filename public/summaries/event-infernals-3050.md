# 燃起的神话 / Kindled Myths

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany
- DLC：Infernals Species Pack

## 如何开始 How it starts

有地狱族的玩家在一个以火山星球为首都的前FTL文明上设有观察前哨，且该文明和地狱帝国都没触发过（triggered_kindled_myths）、地狱帝国不嗜杀时，情势事件 infernals.3050 向地狱帝国发出 infernals.3055。

When an Infernals empire holds an observation outpost on a pre-FTL civilisation whose capital is a volcanic world, with neither side having triggered it (triggered_kindled_myths) and the Infernals empire not homicidal, the situation event infernals.3050 sends infernals.3055 to it.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

3055 两个选项：忽略（3060 “愚蠢的假设”）或深入调查（50/50：3065 “原始动荡”——接触失误引发骚乱；或 3070 “惊人发现”）。

3055 offers two options: ignore it (3060 "Foolish Assumption") or investigate (50/50: 3065 "Primitive Turmoils" — careless contact causes unrest; or 3070 "Astounding Discovery").

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

忽略 / 调查（调查有随机互斥结果）。

Ignore / investigate (investigation has random exclusive outcomes).
