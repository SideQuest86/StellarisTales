# 第一次接触 / First Contact

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 三个入口：1）对前超光速文明的行星轰炸或入侵：awareness.125 把觉察度设为100，建立联系并发出 action.2（机器人起义不算）；2）觉察度升至“完全”：on_awareness_level_increase → 隐藏 awareness.900 → 910「暴露！」；3）前超光速科技进步局势（preftl.41）在其已达完全觉察后发出 920「谁在监视监视者？」（也由观测站建造的 fircon.30 附带通信）。
  Three entries: 1) bombarding or invading a pre-FTL civilization: awareness.125 sets awareness to 100, makes contact and fires action.2 (the robot uprising does not count); 2) awareness reaches "full": on_awareness_level_increase -> hidden awareness.900 -> 910 "Exposed!"; 3) the pre-FTL tech-progress situation (preftl.41) fires 920 "Who Watches the Watchers?" once full awareness was reached (fircon.30 also adds communications when an observation station is built).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 910 / 920 的选项都把玩家带到 action.2「首次接触」：窗口文字按被接触文明的觉察度而不同；窗口后按条件显示 progress.4/5/6 的通知（第一次外星遭遇 / 发展中文明）。
  The options of 910 and 920 both lead to action.2 "First Contact", whose text depends on the contacted civilization's awareness level; afterwards progress.4/5/6 notifications appear by condition (first alien encounter / developing civilization).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 三个入口并行（不同情形各自触发）；progress.4/5/6 按条件互斥。
  The three entries are parallel (different situations); progress.4/5/6 are exclusive by condition.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 选项按伦理（亲外/排外/灵性/唯物/军国/和平/平等）显示不同措辞；风暴追逐者起源有专属版本。
  Option wording varies with ethics (xenophile/xenophobe/spiritualist/materialist/militarist/pacifist/egalitarian); Storm Chasers have their own variant.
