# 盟友间的争端 / Disputes Among Allies

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / Galactic miscellany

## 如何开始 How it starts

联邦法律的集权度（低/中/高/极高）改变后，fire_random_centralisation_flavour_event_effect 以1/5的概率触发隐藏事件 envoy_events.16，再按1/2选一个 envoy_events.17。

After a federation law of centralisation (low / medium / high / very high) changes, fire_random_centralisation_flavour_event_effect fires the hidden envoy_events.16 with probability 1/5, which then fires envoy_events.17 with probability 1/2 (modified).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

17：联邦法律变动引发与另一成员（按军国/和平、灵性/唯物、亲外/排外、平等/威权配对）的意识形态分歧。选项：坚持立场（隐藏效果引出 18）、为团结保持沉默（19，获得5400天的联邦意识形态修正如 federation_militarist_ideology）、召回特使重训。

17: a federation law change causes an ideological dispute with another member (paired by militarist/pacifist, spiritualist/materialist, xenophile/xenophobe, egalitarian/authoritarian). Options: hold the line (a hidden effect leads to 18), keep silent in the interest of unity (19, with a 5400-day federation ideology modifier such as federation_militarist_ideology), or recall the envoy for retraining.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

三选一，18/19 各为一条回复窗口。

One of three; 18 and 19 are reply windows.
