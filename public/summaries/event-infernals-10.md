## 剧情脉络 Story outline

情势共五个阶段（进度300/600/900/1200/1500）：第2阶段 infernals.65（保护母星或利用热能的专项二选一）、第3阶段 70、第4阶段 75、第5阶段 80（无窗口）；进度走满为新星（50 → 24个月后 51）；若被逆转则 60（恢复常态）。第2阶段起，每月有概率触发 infernals.95“恒星隧道”，开启专项“三角定位亚空间干扰源”：在三个地点依次出现 105、110、115，最后一个发现卢瑟斯采矿站所在的陌生星系，那里有守卫无人机——击毁它们引出 2151“神秘的采矿站”：翻译语言（专项 → 2125 谈判）或强攻（专项 → 2115 登船）或直接摧毁（2121/2122）。谈判、登船的各选项导向 2130/2131/2135 等“亚空间采矿作业”，24个月后 2140“稳定前景”，再到 2145“稳定达成”，最后 2160“危机火花”。

The situation has five stages (progress 300/600/900/1200/1500): stage 2 infernals.65 (a choice of two projects: protect the homeworld or exploit the heat), stage 3 infernals.70, stage 4 75, stage 5 80 (no window); full progress is the nova (50 -> 51 after 24 months); if reversed, 60 (back to normal). From stage 2 there is a monthly chance of infernals.95 "Stellar Tunnels", opening the project "Triangulate the subspace interference": 105, 110 and 115 appear at three locations in turn, the last revealing the unknown system of the Lusus mining station, guarded by drones — destroying them brings 2151 "A Mysterious Station": translate (project -> 2125 negotiations), board by force (project -> 2115) or destroy it (2121/2122). The options of negotiation and boarding lead to the "Subspace Mining Operation" events 2130/2131/2135, 24 months later 2140 "Prospect of Stability", then 2145 "Stability Achieved" and finally 2160 "A Spark of Crisis".

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

新星 / 恢复；保护母星 / 利用热能（65）；谈判时的五个选项（庇护、给船让他们离开、奴役、远程排空、让他们自行判断）和登船后的七个选项（摧毁、审讯、奴役、清除、对话、净化、吃掉）；稳定星体时的三种命运（固定现状、加速膨胀、恢复常态，2140）。

Nova / restored; protect the homeworld / exploit the heat (65); five options in negotiation (sanctuary, give them a ship, enslave, vent remotely, let them judge) and seven after boarding (demolish, interrogate, enslave, eliminate, speak, purge, eat); three fates when stabilising the star (fix it, accelerate it, return to normal; 2140).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

选项依伦理、政体和起源而异（如奴役需允许奴隶制，吃掉仅限掠食类）；65 的正文有商团版本。

Options depend on ethics, government and origin (enslaving needs slavery to be allowed, eating is for predators only); the text of 65 has a megacorp version.
