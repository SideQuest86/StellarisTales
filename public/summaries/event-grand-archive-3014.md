# 令人不快的意外 / An Unpleasant Surprise

- 分类 Category：银河轶事 C8 stories · 来源 Sources：events/grand_archive_events.txt, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt · DLC：Grand Archive · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

岩居兽猎杀项目（见故事“救回[this.GetName]”）生成潜伏的岩居兽（舰船标记 ambushing_cutholoid）。该岩居兽被消灭时隐藏事件 3014（events/grand_archive_events.txt:3315-3351）、被俘时 3035（:3687-3729，on_fauna_captured）都会在 1 天后对项目所有者的暴露舰队触发 3015，再触发 3016。
Both the destruction (3014) and the capture (3035) of the ambushing Cutholoid fire hidden 3015 on the revealed fleet one day later, then 3016.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

3015：让舰队的指挥官被“岩居兽吞噬”（50% 几率获得特质 leader_trait_cutholoid_victim；若本国已俘获潜伏岩居兽 captured_hiding_cutholoid 则不会）；3016 “令人不快的意外”：显示事件（指挥官被吞噬时有专门正文），解除瘫痪、舰队失踪返航；若是俘获了潜伏岩居兽且尚未与岩居兽联络，5 天后触发 2900 “岩居兽”首次接触内容。
3016: the commander may become a Cutholoid victim; if a hidden Cutholoid was captured and contact is missing, 2900 follows 5 days later.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 3014（击毁）与 3035（俘获）是两个入口，汇入同一个 3016。 Two entries (destroyed / captured) lead to the same 3016.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 指挥官被吞噬的正文与特质只在随机命中时出现。 The victim text appears only when the 50% roll hits.
