# 奇异的植物毯子 / Bizarre Blanket

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。尺寸大于15的非城市宜居星球可能生成（1级，权重10）；生成时星球立即获得 exofungus（外来真菌）修正。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on a non-urban habitable planet larger than size 15 (level 1, weight 10); on spawn the planet immediately gets the exofungus modifier.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.105“侵入性外来真菌”：覆盖大陆的生长物是来源不明的侵入性真菌，吞噬生物质、压制本地植物；未来殖民者需要不断焚烧清理。奖励社会学研究，记录标本 invasive_exofungus。

distar.105 "Invasive Exofungus": the blanketing growth is an invasive fungus of unknown origin that feeds on biomatter and chokes native plants; future settlers will have to keep burning it back. Rewards society research; specimen invasive_exofungus.
