## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每五年的国家检查运行 action.620：拥有“首次接触”DLC、且与某个前超光速文明有生效协议的普通帝国，会在 0–360 天后抽取“前超光速外交事件池”（无事 200，本事件 10，条件不满足的事件被跳过）。
  The five-year country pulse runs action.620: a regular empire with the First Contact DLC and an active agreement with a pre-FTL civilization draws from the pre-FTL diplomacy pool 0-360 days later (nothing 200, this event 10; events whose conditions fail are skipped).
- 条件：我们是格式塔帝国，拥有观测哨站；境内有完全知晓我们、且正接受我们公开科技启蒙的非蜂巢前超光速文明；15 年内不重复。
  Conditions: we are a gestalt with an observation post; a non-hive pre-FTL within our borders is fully aware of us and under our open technological enlightenment; not again for 15 years.
- 发生后设置“近期前超光速外交事件”冷却，其他此类事件暂不出现。
  Afterwards a "recent pre-FTL diplomatic event" cooldown blocks the other events of this pool for a while.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 7505「万象归一」：他们以我们为唯一参照，认为宇宙中所有生命都像我们一样相互连接。
  7505 "All is One": with only us as reference, they believe all life is connected, like us.
- “是的，我们即一切”（蜂巢措辞）：他们进入下一科技时代但暂时不信任我们；“说我们自己也不确定”：他们以后的时代进步快 25%，但暂时不信任我们；“纠正他们的误解”。单页事件。
  "Yes, we are all" (hive wording): they advance to their next age but distrust us for a time; "tell them we aren't sure ourselves": their future ages progress 25% faster, but they distrust us for a time; "correct their misunderstanding". A single window.
