# 大灭绝 / Mass Extinction

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/country_events_2.txt:1875-3158; events/game_start.txt:15; common/event_chains/00_event_chains_2.txt:45-52; common/special_projects/00_projects_2.txt (MASSEX_*) · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

开局事件闸门抽中（权重10）。country.1100 报告在银河系历史中发现了多次生物大灭绝的迹象，接受后开始“古往今来的大灭绝”事件链，隐藏事件在首都1到8跳内为五种星球（小行星、熔岩、荒芜、有毒、冰冻）各标记一个无主星球。

Drawn from the starting-event gate (weight 10). country.1100 reports signs of repeated mass extinctions in galactic history; accepting starts the "Mass Extinction Through the Ages" chain and a hidden event marks one unowned planet each of five classes (asteroid, molten, barren, toxic, frozen) 1-8 jumps from the capital.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

舰队进入任一标记星球时，对应的发现事件 country.1105/1110/1115/1120/1125 弹出（若舰队无法进入该星系，选项“已被占领”会另选一个星球重新标记）；随后启用对应特殊项目 MASSEX_1–5。项目完成的事件 country.1130/1135/1140/1145/1150 使计数加一；第五个完成时选项指向 country.1155“研究已完成”，结束事件链。

When a fleet enters a marked planet the matching discovery event country.1105/1110/1115/1120/1125 appears (if the fleet has no access to that system, the "owned" option marks another planet instead); then the special project MASSEX_1-5 is enabled. The completion events country.1130/1135/1140/1145/1150 add to the counter; the fifth one's option points to country.1155 "Research Complete", which ends the chain.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

五个星球并行，无互斥分支；country.1155 在两种奖励中二选一。

The five planets are parallel; no exclusive branches; country.1155 offers two rewards.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

非集群意识、非生命播种、非游牧；年轻、虚空居民与破碎环起源不会触发。

Non-gestalt, not Life Seeded, not nomadic; young, void dweller and shattered ring origins never get it.
