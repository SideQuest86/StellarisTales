## 如何开始 How it starts

飞船首次进入带有 lgate 标记、含L星门基座（lgate_base）的星系，且本国没有L星团事件链、也没遇过L星门时，触发 apoc.3（on_entering_system_first_time）。

A ship first entering a system flagged lgate that holds an L-gate base (lgate_base), when the empire has neither the L-Cluster chain nor met an L-gate before, triggers apoc.3 (on_entering_system_first_time).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

apoc.3 报告一座被人为封闭的远古星门，唯一选项“有意思”启动事件链“L星团”（start_lcluster_chain），事件链卡片只是总览；后续的L星团内容不在本故事。

apoc.3 reports a deliberately sealed ancient gateway; its only option starts the event chain "L-Cluster" (start_lcluster_chain); the chain card is only an overview, the L-Cluster content itself is not in this story.
