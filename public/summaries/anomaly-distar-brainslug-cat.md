## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。有危险野生动物（阻碍或修正）的宜居星球可能生成（4级，权重10，全局一次；荒野帝国为0）；生成时添加“废弃的原始家园”矿藏。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on habitable planets with dangerous wildlife (blocker or modifier) (level 4, weight 10, once per game; 0 for Wilderness empires); on spawn it adds the abandoned primitive homesteads deposit.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.170“断掉的枢纽”：星球上的爬行动物曾在一种附着于脑干的神经共生体（脑虫）帮助下建立农业社会，共生中断后退化为野兽。非机械帝国可开启 BRAINSLUG_PROJECT（需星球上有可作为宿主的人口），或没有时间；机械智能只有一个评论选项。
2. 项目完成 → distar.171：共生体只接受自愿的宿主，科学家请求亲自尝试。拒绝则结束；同意 → 30天后 distar.172：结合成功，共生体讲述爬行动物出于猜疑撕下并杀死它们，它们愿与自愿者结合。
3. distar.172：接受（隐藏 distar.174 使人才库中20%领袖获得脑虫特质，distar.175 让含宿主的星球产生初期动荡）、演化猎手复制分发（全体领袖与人口获得宿主特质），或到此为止。

1. distar.170 "Broken Union": the planet's reptiles once built a farming society thanks to a neural symbiont (brain slug) attached to their brain stems and devolved when the union ended. Non-machine empires can start BRAINSLUG_PROJECT (needs pops that can host) or decline; machine intelligences only get a remark.
2. Project → distar.171: the symbionts only accept willing hosts; the scientist asks to try. Refuse and it ends; agree → distar.172 after 30 days: the union works; the slugs explain that their suspicious hosts tore them off and killed them, and offer union to willing volunteers.
3. distar.172: accept (hidden distar.174 gives 20% of pool leaders the brain-slug trait, distar.175 makes planets with hosts start with unrest), Evolutionary Predators replicate them for everyone (all leaders and pops gain the host trait), or stop here.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.171 两选项互斥；distar.172 的接受有两个同名版本（排外帝国需已有宿主人口），与“到此为止”互斥。

distar.171's options are exclusive; distar.172's accept exists in two same-key versions (xenophobes need existing host pops), exclusive with "stop".

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.170 有机械智能正文；演化猎手起源有专属选项。

distar.170 has a machine text; Evolutionary Predators have a dedicated option.
