## 如何开始 How it starts

潜入虚境但没有找到其他事件（on_patron_delve_failed）时，从28个权重各为100的遭遇（另有权重1的 utopia.3002 兜底）中随机抽取一个满足条件的；每次潜入失败只出现一个遭遇，不会依次经历全部。要求尚未拥有或可研究“灵能闪电”，且处于某位尚未立约的主要赞助者领域；此前非组件类潜入越多、或最近一次组件潜入失败，抽中权重越高。

When a Shroud delve finds nothing else (on_patron_delve_failed), one eligible encounter is drawn at random from 28 candidates of weight 100 each (plus utopia.3002 at weight 1 as fallback); each failed delve shows one encounter, never all of them in sequence. Requires not having (or having as an option) Psionic Lightning, and being in the domain of a major patron without a covenant; its weight grows with previous non-component delves and after a recent failed component delve.
