# 金属晶体结构 / Metallic Crystal Formations

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar） · 审阅 Reviewed：2026-10-12（已审阅 reviewed）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》。冷荒芜或冰冻星球可能生成（2级，权重1，每个帝国一次）。

Requires Distant Stars. Can spawn on cold barren or frozen planets (level 2, weight 1, once per empire).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.275“纳米机器人晶体格子”：形似摩天楼的巨大晶体由无数失活的纳米机器人组成，来源不明。奖励工程学研究和200纳米机器人。若帝国正在进行“L星团”事件链（l_cluster_chain，需《遥远繁星》），结果增加一条线索（共需7条）；集齐时触发 distar.10904“L星门见解汇集”（另一故事）。30天后的通用接触报告 story.5 是共享事件，不属于本故事。

distar.275 "Nanite Crystal Lattices": the skyscraper-like crystals are trillions of deactivated nanites of unknown origin. Rewards engineering research and 200 nanites. If the empire is running the L-Cluster event chain (l_cluster_chain, Distant Stars), the result adds one clue (7 needed); completing the count fires distar.10904 "L-Gate Insights Gathered" (a separate story). The generic contact report story.5 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

唯一结果，无随机分支。

Single outcome; no random branch.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

正文按帝国是否已有通讯对象分两种（有：对比已知帝国的纳米技术；无：说明星球从未有过大气）。排外帝国只有“令人不安”选项：多得社会学研究，但没有纳米机器人。

Two texts depending on whether the empire has any communications (compares with known empires' nanotech / notes the planet never had an atmosphere). Xenophobes only get "Disconcerting": extra society research but no nanites.
