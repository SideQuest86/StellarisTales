## 如何开始 How it starts

潜入虚境但没有找到其他事件（on_patron_delve_failed）时，从28个权重各为100的遭遇（另有权重1的 utopia.3002 兜底）中随机抽取一个满足条件的；每次潜入失败只出现一个遭遇，不会依次经历全部。要求与小赞助者“位面之外客”已完成接触、未在“自寻出路”、处于月窠或织缕领域之一，每个帝国一次。

When a Shroud delve finds nothing else (on_patron_delve_failed), one eligible encounter is drawn at random from 28 candidates of weight 100 each (plus utopia.3002 at weight 1 as fallback); each failed delve shows one encounter, never all of them in sequence. Requires completed contact with the minor patron the Outsider, not forging its own path, and being in the domain of the Cradle or the Composer; once per empire.

The Outsider warns us; it may be recognised as the Zroni "Prophet".
