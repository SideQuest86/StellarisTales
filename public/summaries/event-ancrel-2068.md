## 剧情脉络 Story outline

拆解C.A.R.E交互界面：该物种的人口被清除，行星改归占领者，旧国家被摧毁；选项“太棒了”：若尚无则获得遗珍“碎片整理者”（r_the_defragmentor）并得到200稀有文物。

Disassembling the interface: the species' pops are killed, the planet changes hands and the old country is destroyed; the option "Excellent" grants the relic r_the_defragmentor (if not owned) and 200 minor artifacts.
