# 罕见特质 / Rare Trait

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

“生长”碎片存在、我方和平且有带“有趣特质”的有机人口时，五年随机脉冲可能抽中 bio.3225，立即弹出 bio.3230。触发后设置2560天的蜂巢失落帝国事件冷却（hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown），期间其他碎片要求不再出现。

With the Growth fragment present, our empire at peace and organic pops with an "interesting" trait, the five-year pulse can pick bio.3225, which shows bio.3230 at once. Firing sets a 2560-day cooldown (hive_fallen_empire_event_cooldown) shared by the fragment demands.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

生长碎片要求一份带有该罕见特质的活体样本。

The Growth fragment demands a living sample carrying the rare trait.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

同意则碎片物种获得该特质并对我方满意；拒绝则不满。无后续窗口。

Agreeing gives the fragment's species that trait and pleases it; refusing displeases it. No follow-up window.
