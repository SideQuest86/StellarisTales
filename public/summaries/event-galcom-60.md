## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 若“外维实验”决议生效且该国尚无零点矿科技选项 → 5天后 federations.10020「外维实验」。
  If the "Extradimensional Experimentation" resolution is active and the empire lacks the Zro mine tech option -> federations.10020 "Extradimensional Experimentation" 5 days later.
- 若该国没有市场准入标志而别的成员已有 → action.115「银河市场」（另有每年的 action.105 补发，随机40天内）。
  If the empire lacks market access while another member has it -> action.115 "Galactic Market" (the yearly action.105 re-sends it within a random 40 days).
