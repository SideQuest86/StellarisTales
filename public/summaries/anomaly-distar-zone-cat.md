## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.220“区域A”：唯一的遗迹是一片约13×13公里、被称为“区域A”的地带，散落着性质诡异的造物，却让来访者感到深深的满足；科学官推测曾有航天文明在此短暂停留。星球获得“区域”矿藏，奖励工程、物理研究与凝聚力，记录标本 zone_a_sedative。结果执行 pop_lcluster_event：若已遇到首座L星门且线索未集齐，有30%几率在10天后触发通用的 distar.9000“L星门见解”（另一故事）。30天后的通用接触报告 story.5 是共享事件，不属于本故事。

distar.220 "Zone A": the only ruin is a roughly 13×13 km area, "Zone A", strewn with bizarre artifacts that nonetheless fill visitors with deep contentment; perhaps a spacefaring civilisation once stopped here. The planet gains the Zone deposit; rewards engineering and physics research and unity; specimen zone_a_sedative. The result runs pop_lcluster_event: if the empire has met its first L-Gate and the clues are incomplete, there is a 30% chance of the generic distar.9000 "L-Gate Insights" 10 days later (a separate story). The generic contact report story.5 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.
