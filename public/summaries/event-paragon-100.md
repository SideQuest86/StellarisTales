# 失去金字塔 / Monolith Lost

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/paragon_events.txt:3-100 · DLC: Paragon · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

“观察者”事件链（锚点异常 paragon_beholder_category 的后续）进行时：被选中的前智慧星球若被他国殖民（on_colonized，paragon.100）或其原住民被提升（on_uplift_completion，paragon.101），链被中止并弹出 paragon.102。

While the Beholder chain (the follow-up of the paragon_beholder_category anomaly) is running, if the chosen pre-sapient planet is colonised by someone else (on_colonized, paragon.100) or its natives are uplifted (on_uplift_completion, paragon.101) the chain is aborted and paragon.102 appears.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

paragon.100/101 是隐藏事件；若是链所有者自己殖民，则改为添加“观察者”建筑。paragon.102 移除“前智慧生物受保护”修正和“观察者”建筑，并清除全局目标；唯一选项为遗憾。

paragon.100/101 are hidden; if the chain owner itself colonises the planet the Beholder building is added instead. paragon.102 removes the "pre-sapients protected" modifier and the Beholder building and clears the global targets; its only option is UNFORTUNATE.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

需要 Paragon DLC 且全局目标 beholder_planet 存在。

Paragon DLC and an existing beholder_planet global target.
