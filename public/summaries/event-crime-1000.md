## 如何开始 How it starts

月度脉冲（on_monthly_pulse，common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:112）运行隐藏事件 crime.1000：若某个行星有犯罪辛迪加分部且行星犯罪值低于1、双方没有商业协定、该辛迪加不在冷却期，则以1/20的概率选中一处，360±40天后触发 crime.1001。

The monthly pulse (on_monthly_pulse, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:112) runs hidden crime.1000: if a planet hosts a criminal syndicate's branch office, its crime is below 1, there is no commercial pact and the syndicate is not on cooldown, it picks one with chance 1/20 and fires crime.1001 360+-40 days later.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

crime.1001“分部被关闭”给行星所有者：关闭分部（辛迪加10年内不再触发），并给分部所有者 crime.1002 的通知。

crime.1001 "Branch Office shut down" shows the planet owner a window to close the branch office (the syndicate gets a 10-year cooldown) and sends crime.1002 to the office owner.
