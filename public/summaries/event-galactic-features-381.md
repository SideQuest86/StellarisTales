## 剧情脉络 Story outline

若击杀国是银河共同体成员且《缇扬奇保护法》生效 → galcom.107 “违反保护法”（十年内违反银河法）；否则（事件未在显示中）1天后 galactic_features.385 “缇扬奇被猎杀”：收获资源并按各国伦理改变好感。

If the killer's nation is a Galactic Community member and the Tiyanki Conservation Act is in effect → galcom.107 "Tiyanki Conservation Act Violation" (violating galactic law for ten years); otherwise (the event is not shown) 1 day later galactic_features.385 (Tiyanki hunted): resources are harvested and opinions change according to each nation's ethics.
