# 仇外心理 / Xenophiliacs

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7) · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12
- 来源 Sources：`events/first_contact_dlc_events.txt:8604-8981`；`common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:468, 538-560`（on_five_year_random_pulse_pre_ftl）；`events/on_action_events_2.txt:3146-3179`（action.620/621）

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每五年的国家检查运行 action.620：拥有“首次接触”DLC、且与某个前超光速文明有生效协议的普通帝国，会在 0–360 天后抽取“前超光速外交事件池”（无事 200，本事件 10，条件不满足的事件被跳过）。
  The five-year country pulse runs action.620: a regular empire with the First Contact DLC and an active agreement with a pre-FTL civilization draws from the pre-FTL diplomacy pool 0-360 days later (nothing 200, this event 10; events whose conditions fail are skipped).
- 条件：我们不是格式塔，拥有观测哨站；境内有完全知晓我们的非蜂巢前超光速文明（非“挣脱桎梏”母族）；每个帝国一次。
  Conditions: we are not a gestalt and have an observation post; a non-hive pre-FTL within our borders is fully aware of us (not our Broken Shackles parent); once per empire.
- 发生后设置“近期前超光速外交事件”冷却，其他此类事件暂不出现。
  Afterwards a "recent pre-FTL diplomatic event" cooldown blocks the other events of this pool for a while.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 8000「仇外心理」（英文标题为 Xenophiliacs）：他们的年轻人迷上了我们的一切，形成新的亚文化。
  8000 "Xenophiliacs" (the Chinese title reads "xenophobia"): their youth become obsessed with all things ours.
- “有趣”→ 360–420 天后 8001「扮装文化」：约 30% 他们的人口与 10% 我方人口转向亲外；（非亲外非排外，需 100 影响力）“我们的文化属于我们自己”→ 360–420 天后 50% 8002「重返归根之地」（各约 10% 转向排外）/ 50% 8003「再无粉丝」（他们约 30%、我们约 10% 转向排外）。
  "Interesting" → 360-420 days later 8001 "Costume Culture": about 30% of their pops and 10% of ours turn xenophile; (neither xenophile nor xenophobe, 100 influence) "our culture is our own" → 360-420 days later 50% 8002 "Roots Reclaimed" (about 10% each turn xenophobe) / 50% 8003 "Fans No More" (about 30% of theirs and 10% of ours turn xenophobe).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 8002 与 8003 为互斥随机结果（50/50）。
  8002 and 8003 are exclusive random results (50/50).
- 这是可重复抽取的随机外交插曲之一（各事件本身每个帝国通常一次），与同池其他事件没有固定顺序。
  One of the random diplomacy interludes of this pool (each usually once per empire), with no fixed order relative to the others.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 8001 的确认选项按我们是否亲外分两份（措辞相同）。
  8001's option has two copies by whether we are xenophile (same wording).
- （评注）本事件中英文标题含义相反：英文 Xenophiliacs，中文“仇外心理”。
  (Commentary) The English title "Xenophiliacs" and the Chinese title "仇外心理" (xenophobia) disagree.
