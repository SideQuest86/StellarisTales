## 如何开始 How it starts

- 3级异常；在冰冻或寒冷贫瘠行星上以权重3生成。  
  Level-3 anomaly; spawn weight 3 on frozen or cold barren planets.
- 约30天后另有一份通用联络报告（story.1–8，共享枢纽故事），不属于本故事。  
  About 30 days later a generic contact report (story.1–8, a shared hub story) follows; it is not part of this story.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 四个结果各基础权重1：  
  Four outcomes, base weight 1 each:
- **璞玉浑金**（4055）：行星原资源点被清除并改为6矿物；可把功臣提拔为新科学家（热切、天才火花），或留在船上（船员修正）。格式塔帝国或行星已有战略资源时权重0。  
  **Diamond in the Rough** (4055): the planet's deposits are replaced by +6 minerals; promote the protégé to a new scientist or keep them aboard. Weight 0 for gestalts or if the planet has a strategic resource.
- **小体量，大力量**（4075）：寒带太阳能细菌。30天后：研究能源用途 → 4076（永久太阳能细菌修正）；或研究军事用途（未拥有先进护盾时）→ 4077（按现有护盾科技给下一级护盾进度）。持禁止干涉政策时权重0。  
  **Strength from Small Places** (4075): solar-cell bacteria. After 30 days: energy use → 4076 (permanent modifier), or military use (without Advanced Shields) → 4077 (progress toward the next shield tech). Weight 0 with the interference_not_allowed policy.
- **天际巨响**（4120）：大气爆炸奇观，帝国获科学热情修正3600天。格式塔权重0。  
  **Explosions in the Sky** (4120): science inspiration for 3600 days. Weight 0 for gestalts.
- **探索者的教诲**（4140）：外星远征队留下的文献；研究（1200社会学）或出售（能源与消费品）。格式塔、已找到此著作或已触发过4140时权重0。  
  **Teachings of Explorers** (4140): an expedition's documents; research (1,200 society) or sell (energy, consumer goods). Weight 0 for gestalts, if this writing was found, or if 4140 already fired.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 翻译：70% 4141 解锁法令“大师的教诲：哲学思维”并给予标本；30% anomaly_failure.4136 部分译出（影响力）。  
  Translation: 70% 4141 unlocks "Master's Teachings: Philosophical Mindset" and a specimen; 30% anomaly_failure.4136 partial (influence).
- “大师的著作”系列：大陆上的发现、钢之追寻、沙中迷藏、潜在发现四个异常各可找到“讲述者泰丽莎”的一部著作并解锁对应法令；四者互不依赖、没有顺序，失败结果 anomaly_failure.4136 共用。完成时若已找到其他著作，成功事件使用另一段“又一部”文本。  
  "Master's Writings": Continental Findings, A Feel For Steel, Buried in the Sand and Cold Hard Potential can each yield one work of "Telisa the Teller of Tales" and its edict; they are independent and unordered, sharing the failure outcome anomaly_failure.4136. If another writing was found before, the success event uses its "more teachings" text.
