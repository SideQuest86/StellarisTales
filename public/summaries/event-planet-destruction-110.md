# 超空间捷径 / Hyperspace Bypass

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/planet_destruction_events.txt:442-520; common/on_actions/01_planet_destruction.txt:18,196 · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-13

## 如何开始 How it starts

用世界粉碎者或冶炼者毁灭太阳系的地球（行星旗标 planet_earth / nuked_earth，或原始人类文明的星球）而施放者不是人类时，隐藏事件 planet_destruction.110 以50%概率触发。

When a World Cracker or Smelter destroys Earth in the Sol system (planet flag planet_earth / nuked_earth, or a primitive human world) and the firing empire is not human, hidden planet_destruction.110 triggers with 50% chance.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

planet_destruction.111“超空间捷径”：在50–300距离外的另一个系统生成一对相连的虫洞，选项仅提示。

planet_destruction.111 "Hyperspace Bypass": a linked pair of natural wormholes is spawned between Sol and a system 50-300 away; the option is only a tooltip.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

只对非人类的施放者，Sol 系统。

Only for non-human firing empires, in the Sol system.
