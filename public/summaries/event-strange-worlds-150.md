# 恶心虫虫 / Revolting Pests

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满1年时随机事件池以20/510抽中 strange_worlds.150（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3734），机器帝国除外；360天后 strange_worlds.155。触发前提：BioGenesis DLC，人类玩家的非首都、非母星、仍由原建立者持有的人造以外殖民星球，没有地面战斗，也未被占领；并且该殖民地尚未触发过其他“奇异世界”事件（colony_event 旗标），每个国家每条故事只触发一次。

A colony turning 1 year old can draw strange_worlds.150 (20/510, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3734), machine empires excluded; strange_worlds.155 follows after 360 days. Preconditions: BioGenesis DLC, a human player's non-capital, non-homeworld, original-owner, non-artificial colony without ground combat or occupation, which has not yet had another Strange Worlds event (colony_event flag); once per country per chain.
