## 如何开始 How it starts

星海帝国中皇帝执行的行动 operation_target_seditionists（针对颠覆势力）：成本影响力60，维持能量7，间谍力35，三个难度 T3 的阶段；执行者是皇帝，目标是共同体成员且是对手或其特使正在损害帝国权威；若皇帝不再是皇帝或目标退出共同体则中止。

The Imperium operation run by the emperor, operation_target_seditionists: cost 60 influence, upkeep 7 energy, spy power 35, three tier-3 stages; the performer is the emperor and the target a Community member who is a rival or whose envoys undermine Imperial authority; it aborts if the emperor stops being emperor or the target leaves the Community.
