## 如何开始 How it starts

- 2级异常，全局仅一次；在非恒星、非气态巨行星上权重1，机器人与机械智能帝国为0（“它们已经不朽”）；null_spawn_chance 0.5。  
  Level-2 anomaly, once per galaxy; weight 1 on non-star, non-gas-giant bodies, 0 for robot and machine empires ("already immortal"); null_spawn_chance 0.5.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 7105“高密度能量”：一颗蓝色球体与刻有时间符号的地面。研究（噬杀蜂群改为“吃掉它”）→ 30–40天后 7115；或认为太危险而离开 → 30–40天后 7110（球体与刻痕像时间倒流般消失，物理研究）。  
  7105 "Concentrated Energy": a blue orb amid time-related carvings. Study it (Devouring Swarms "eat" it) → 7115 after 30–40 days; or leave → 7110 after 30–40 days (orb and carvings vanish as if running backwards; physics research).
- 7115“时光如流水”：科学家触碰球体后脱离时间。若科学家本已不朽或是继承人，则改由一名新生成的初级科学家承受。  
  7115 "Time Flows Like a River": the scientist is unmoored from time; if they are already immortal or an heir, a newly created junior scientist is affected instead.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 让其独自远航（获得不朽，被放逐）→ 约36000天（一百年）后 7120“返航”：带回大量物理研究。  
  Let them travel alone (made immortal, exiled) → about 36,000 days (a century) later 7120 "The Return" brings a large physics reward.
- 留下效力：科学家获得“超越时间”特质；若为不朽/继承人情形，则复制出一名带此特质的新科学家。  
  Keep them: the scientist gains the Intemporal trait; in the immortal/heir case a cloned new scientist with that trait joins.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 7115、7120 的正文按蜂巢、噬杀蜂群、不朽或继承人科学家区分。  
  7115 and 7120 texts vary for hive minds, Devouring Swarms and immortal or heir scientists.
