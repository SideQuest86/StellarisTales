# 玛琳·卡琳：花之魅力 / Mharin Kharin: Floral Fascination

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 殖民地2岁（3/958）或3岁（2/739）时的随机事件；全局一次；要求非意识集合体、非个体机械，且星球不是干旱、沙漠、稀树草原、火山、栖息地等。
  Random colony event at age 2 (3/958) or 3 (2/739); once per game; needs a non-Gestalt-Consciousness, non-individual-machine empire and a planet that is not arid, desert, savannah, volcanic, habitat etc.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 1520：启用“花卉研究”项目（成功 → 1534 获得社会研究），并在360天后触发 1521。
  1520: enables the "Floral Study" project (success -> 1534, society research) and fires 1521 360 days later.
- 1521「嗅觉刺激」：可付400能量终止；否则星球获得第一级花粉修正，400（或买不起时500）天后 1522。
  1521: pay 400 energy to stop the chain; otherwise the planet gains the first pollen modifier and 1522 follows after 400 days (500 if unaffordable).
- 1522：第二级修正并启用“劳动研究”项目（成功 → 1523 获得研究并缓解），300天后 1524，再100天后 1525。
  1522: second modifier and the "Labor Study" project (success -> 1523, research and relief); 1524 after 300 days, 1525 after 100 more.
- 1525 分支：b 接管 → 1526（200天）→ 1527（300天）→ 1528（360天）军队清除花粉，链结束；a 放任 → 1529 花粉崇拜（200天）→ 1530（60天）→ 灾难项目“末日预感”。
  1525 branches: b take over -> 1526 (200 days) -> 1527 (300) -> 1528 (360) loyal forces purge the plant, chain ends; a hands-off -> 1529 pollen worship (200) -> 1530 (60) -> the "Impending Doom" project.
- 项目成功 → 1531 灾难避免；失败 → 1532 殖民地被毁、星球变毒性并带来10年负面修正，720天后 1533 星球恢复为沙漠可再殖民。
  Project success -> 1531 disaster averted; failure -> 1532 the colony is destroyed, the planet turns toxic with a 10-year penalty, and 1533 restores it as a desert 720 days later.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 接管与放任互斥；项目成功与失败互斥。
  Take-over and hands-off are exclusive; project success and failure are exclusive.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 1521 的付费选项需要能量储备≥400，否则出现替代选项。
  The paying option at 1521 needs 400 energy; otherwise the alternative option appears.
