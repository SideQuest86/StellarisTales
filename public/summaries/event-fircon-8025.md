# 建立自由港 / Freeport Established

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7) · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12
- 来源 Sources：`events/first_contact_dlc_events.txt:8969-9031`；`common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:468, 538-560`（on_five_year_random_pulse_pre_ftl）；`events/on_action_events_2.txt:3146-3179`（action.620/621）

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每五年的国家检查运行 action.620：拥有“首次接触”DLC、且与某个前超光速文明有生效协议的普通帝国，会在 0–360 天后抽取“前超光速外交事件池”（无事 200，本事件 10，条件不满足的事件被跳过）。
  The five-year country pulse runs action.620: a regular empire with the First Contact DLC and an active agreement with a pre-FTL civilization draws from the pre-FTL diplomacy pool 0-360 days later (nothing 200, this event 10; events whose conditions fail are skipped).
- 条件：与某前超光速文明有贸易协议；每个帝国一次。
  Conditions: a trade agreement with a pre-FTL civilization; once per empire.
- 发生后设置“近期前超光速外交事件”冷却，其他此类事件暂不出现。
  Afterwards a "recent pre-FTL diplomatic event" cooldown blocks the other events of this pool for a while.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 8025「建立自由港」：按贸易协议设立的自由港带来暴利，但走私者和帮派随之而来。
  8025 "Freeport Established": the freeport set up under the trade deal brings huge profits, and smugglers and gangs.
- 选项：要求开设更多自由港（需 50 影响力）；“我们没做错什么”；打击走私（需 1000 凝聚力）。单页事件。
  Options: ask for more freeports (needs 50 influence); "we're not doing anything wrong"; crack down on smugglers (needs 1000 unity). A single window.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 这是可重复抽取的随机外交插曲之一（各事件本身每个帝国通常一次），与同池其他事件没有固定顺序。
  One of the random diplomacy interludes of this pool (each usually once per empire), with no fixed order relative to the others.
