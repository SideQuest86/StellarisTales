# 定居完成 / Settlement Complete

- 分类 Category: C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · 来源 Sources: events/nomads_events_1.txt:13520-13810; common/on_actions/02_component_on_actions.txt:385 · DLC: Nomads · 审阅 Reviewed 2026-10-12

## 如何开始 How it starts

游牧帝国让方舟舰在星球上定居完成时（on_arkship_settle → nomads.4700），收到 nomads.4705（L13520-13560）。

When a nomadic empire finishes settling an arkship on a planet (on_arkship_settle -> nomads.4700), it receives nomads.4705 (L13520-13560).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

方舟舰的航程结束。四个选择：以此星球为新帝国的基础（两种），继续漫游，或在此统治群星（创建附属国并转到游牧经济/星海日志，nomads.20）。

The arkship's voyage ends. Four choices: make this planet the foundation of a new empire (two variants), keep roaming, or rule the stars from here (creating a subject and moving on to the nomad economy and Star Journal via nomads.20).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

选项互斥；选项 c/d 不适用于杀戮型帝国，c 不适用于毒素骑士。

Options are exclusive; c/d are unavailable to homicidal empires, c to Toxic Knights.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

正文按普通/蜂巢/机械等分为六段。

Six text variants for regular, hive, machine and so on.
