# 太空创生家艾莎琳 / Astrocreator Azaryn

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: Paragon

## 如何开始 How it starts

每五年脉冲时隐藏事件 paragon.200 以1%概率启动（99%什么都不发生）：需要 Paragon DLC、非AI、非军国主义、非集群意识、已发生过首次接触协议事件、且没有现成的艾莎琳；成功后 1 到 900 天内触发 paragon.202。

On the five-year pulse hidden paragon.200 starts the chain with 1% chance (99% nothing happens): Paragon DLC, human player, not militarist, not gestalt, the first-contact protocol event already happened and no Azaryn exists yet; paragon.202 follows within 1-900 days.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

一、死信号：paragon.202（“死通讯1”）与120±90天后 paragon.204，可选“追踪”启用“追踪源头”项目，完成后 paragon.206 找到通讯源头。二、问答：paragon.208–212（对话，选项记分 variable_azaryn_score），完成后启用“艾莎琳的礼物”项目，成功后 paragon.214 给2000社会研究。三、再次问答 paragon.216–226（取生命？牺牲？犹豫？），启用“艾莎琳的大王花”项目，成功后 paragon.220 给5000社会研究。四、paragon.228 招募窗口，60天后 paragon.230：按得分（≥4最佳、1–3中等、<1最差）获得传奇领袖艾莎琳，并获得对应的“信息素鸡尾酒”修正。五、艾莎琳乘圆顶船为星球环境改造：每次改造耗用一个“改造核”并换成较少花蕾的肖像——第一次后 paragon.232（凋零），第二次后 paragon.235（创生与凋亡），第三次（最后一个）paragon.240“最后的花蕾”：艾莎琳死去、圆顶船枯萎；paragon.241 结束对话。

1) Dead signal: paragon.202 and, 120+-90 days later, paragon.204; choosing to trace it enables the "Trace the Source" project, whose completion shows paragon.206. 2) Quiz: paragon.208-212 (dialogue; answers adjust variable_azaryn_score), then the "Azaryn's Gift" project, whose success paragon.214 pays 2000 society research. 3) Second quiz paragon.216-226 (take a life? sacrifice? hesitate?) and the "Azaryn's Rafflesia" project, success paragon.220 pays 5000 society research. 4) paragon.228 recruitment window, 60 days later paragon.230: by score (>=4 best, 1-3 middle, <1 worst) Azaryn is hired as a legendary leader with a matching "pheromone cocktail" modifier. 5) Azaryn terraforms planets from her dome ship: each terraformation uses one terraformation nucleus and swaps her portrait for one with fewer buds - after the first paragon.232 (Withering), the second paragon.235 (Growth & Decay), the third and last paragon.240 "The Last Bud": Azaryn dies and the dome ship withers; paragon.241 closes the conversation.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

每次开始环境改造（paragon.252）若还剩改造核，有25%概率在360±360天后触发 paragon.260（核心样本被盗，仅一次）：a 启用“环境改造之核研究”项目 → paragon.265（研究完成，给科技或社会研究；25%概率90±90天后 paragon.270 设施摧垮）；b 约30±30天后 paragon.261（样本归还，得“信息素鸡尾酒·最佳”修正）。paragon.270 选a花费资源、10±10天后 paragon.275“绿色恐怖”（杀死5名人口、拆除区划）→ paragon.280；选b → paragon.272/273（向艾莎琳报告，花2500能量）。

Each time terraforming starts (paragon.252) with nuclei left there is a 25% chance of paragon.260 after 360+-360 days (nucleus sample stolen, once only): a enables the "Terraformation Nucleus Research" project -> paragon.265 (research done: tech or society research; 25% chance of paragon.270 Facility Overrun after 90+-90 days); b gives paragon.261 about 30+-30 days later (sample returned, best pheromone cocktail modifier). paragon.270 option a pays resources and 10+-10 days later paragon.275 "Green Terror" (kills 5 pops, removes districts) -> paragon.280; option b -> paragon.272/273 (report to Azaryn, 2500 energy).

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

三次提问的得分决定最终得到哪种修正；可以拒绝或终止通讯（每个对话窗口都有告别选项），那样链条会停在对应位置。

The three quiz answers set which modifier is earned; the player may refuse or hang up at any dialogue (goodbye options), which stops the chain there.
