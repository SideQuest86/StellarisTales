## 如何开始 How it starts

《遥远繁星》专属星系 living_planet_system 中的海洋星球由初始化带有5级异常“彷徨海”。

In the Distant Stars system living_planet_system an ocean planet receives the level-5 anomaly "Haunting Sea" from the initializer.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

1. distar.190“未知接触”：覆盖全球的海洋本身是活的，会以影响脑活动的频率与接触者交流。普通帝国开启“未知接触”事件链；选择研究时，若已拥有该星系则开启 STUDY_LIVING_SEA（30天，科研船+科学家），否则留下兴趣点，待在该星系建成前哨后由 distar.1999/distar.199 开启项目（该路径在另一故事 event-distar-1999 中）。
2. 项目完成 → distar.191：在轨道上设立研究站；随后 180–210天 distar.192“奇特的召唤”，以及10年后 distar.193“启示”。
3. distar.192：最初发现海洋的科学家声称被它日夜呼唤——允许其加入轨道研究队 → 360–420天后 distar.194“启示”（科学家留在站上）；拒绝 → 90–100天后 distar.195 他发来诀别信，10天后 distar.196 他乘登陆舱坠入海洋身亡；解职 → 科学家离开，没有后续事件。

1. distar.190 "Unknown Contact": the world-ocean is alive and communicates with frequencies that affect the brain. Regular empires begin the "Unknown Contact" chain; choosing to study it starts STUDY_LIVING_SEA (30 days, science ship + scientist) if the system is already ours, otherwise a point of interest remains until an outpost there lets distar.1999/distar.199 enable the project (that path is the separate story event-distar-1999).
2. Project → distar.191: an orbital research station is set up; then distar.192 "Strange Calling" after 180–210 days and distar.193 "Revelations" after 10 years.
3. distar.192: the scientist who found the sea says it calls to them day and night — let them join the orbital team → distar.194 "Revelations" after 360–420 days (the scientist stays on the station); refuse → distar.195 a farewell message after 90–100 days and distar.196 ten days later, when they crash a landing pod into the ocean and die; dismiss → they leave service with no further event.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

distar.190：研究 / 没时间（普通帝国）或“令人好奇”（格式塔）。distar.192 三个选项互斥；distar.193 与 192 的结果并行，都会发生。

distar.190: study / no time (regular empires) or "Curious" (gestalts). distar.192's three options are exclusive; distar.193 runs in parallel with distar.192's results.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

distar.190 有普通、机械与蜂巢三段正文（格式塔不受其影响，也不开启事件链）；distar.191、192 有不同代词版本。

distar.190 has normal, machine and hive texts (gestalts are unaffected and do not start the chain); distar.191/192 have pronoun variants.
