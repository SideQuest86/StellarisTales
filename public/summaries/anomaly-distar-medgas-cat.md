# 坚固的核心 / Solid Core

- 分类 Category：深空异闻 Exploration · DLC：《遥远繁星》Distant Stars（has_distar）

## 如何开始 How it starts

需要《遥远繁星》的“L星团”事件链。无战略资源的气态巨行星在该帝国正进行 l_cluster_chain 时权重为5，已集齐线索时为1，两者都不满足时不会生成（3级，每个帝国一次）。

Tied to the Distant Stars L-Cluster chain. On gas giants without strategic resources the weight is 5 while the empire runs l_cluster_chain and 1 once its clues are complete; otherwise it does not spawn (level 3, once per empire).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

distar.285“远古医学建筑”：巨行星异常大的固体核心上覆盖着被压缩的数百万年前的医疗建筑群，技术与L星门如出一辙；也许这是星门建造者的行星医疗设施，被遗弃后自动气体复制器失控，把星球变成了气态巨行星。清除原矿藏并给予奇异气体矿藏，奖励社会学研究。若帝国正在进行“L星团”事件链（l_cluster_chain，需《遥远繁星》），结果增加一条线索（共需7条）；集齐时触发 distar.10904“L星门见解汇集”（另一故事）。30天后的通用接触报告 story.5 是共享事件，不属于本故事。

distar.285 "Ancient Medical Complex": the oversized core is covered in the compressed remains of a medical complex millions of years old, matching L-Gate technology; perhaps a Gatebuilder planetary hospital whose runaway gas replicators turned the planet into a gas giant. Existing deposits are replaced by exotic gases; rewards society research. If the empire is running the L-Cluster event chain (l_cluster_chain, Distant Stars), the result adds one clue (7 needed); completing the count fires distar.10904 "L-Gate Insights Gathered" (a separate story). The generic contact report story.5 30 days later is a shared helper, not part of this story.
