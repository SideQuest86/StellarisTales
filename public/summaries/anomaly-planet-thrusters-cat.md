单一结果 extreme_frontiers.380“行星推进器”：选项a直接获得每月合金、聚变能科技进度（或物理研究）和遗物/工程研究；选项b获得聚变能科技进度并启用项目 REPURPOSE_THRUSTERS_PROJECT“挪用的行星推进器”（360天）。完成后 extreme_frontiers.385“抽丝剥茧”：每月工程与物理研究，该巨行星变成大小18的熔岩星球并添加 chthonian_planet 修正与矿物矿藏（anomaly.6662）。

Single result extreme_frontiers.380 "Planet Thrusters": option a directly gives monthly alloys, fusion-power tech progress (or physics research) and artifacts/engineering research; option b gives fusion-power progress and enables project REPURPOSE_THRUSTERS_PROJECT (360 days). Completion fires extreme_frontiers.385 "Gases Stripped": monthly engineering and physics research, and the giant becomes a size-18 molten planet with the chthonian_planet modifier and a minerals deposit (anomaly.6662).
