## 如何开始 How it starts

Galactic Community 特使的随机事件：五年脉冲（action.2211）的负面列表 on_five_year_random_pulse_country_negative_list，权重见分支栏（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:576-592）；on_action 会先检查事件触发条件、不满足的从列表中剔除，所以只要有一个能触发就必有一个触发。通常共用5400天的 recent_envoy_event 冷却。 本事件：非集群，特使任职于共同体，无 recent_envoy_event；权重20/180。

A random Galactic Community envoy event: the negative list of the five-year pulse (action.2211), on_five_year_random_pulse_country_negative_list, weights in the branches (common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:576-592); on_actions check each event's trigger first and drop the failing ones, so as long as any can fire one always does. Most share a 5400-day recent_envoy_event cooldown. This event: Non-gestalt, an envoy assigned to the Community, no recent_envoy_event; weight 20 of 180.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

特使被游说者收买。选项一：每月获得能量加成（tier1），但10年内受 beholden_to_special_interests 修正拖累；选项二：召回重训，特使被撤职并获得“腐败”特质。

The envoy is courted by lobbyists. Option one: a monthly energy bonus (tier 1) but ten years of the beholden_to_special_interests modifier; option two: recall for retraining, the envoy is unassigned and gains the Corrupt trait.
