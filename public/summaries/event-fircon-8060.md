## 如何开始 How it starts

- 每五年的国家检查运行 action.620：拥有“首次接触”DLC、且与某个前超光速文明有生效协议的普通帝国，会在 0–360 天后抽取“前超光速外交事件池”（无事 200，本事件 10，条件不满足的事件被跳过）。
  The five-year country pulse runs action.620: a regular empire with the First Contact DLC and an active agreement with a pre-FTL civilization draws from the pre-FTL diplomacy pool 0-360 days later (nothing 200, this event 10; events whose conditions fail are skipped).
- 条件：我们不是格式塔，拥有观测哨站；境内有非格式塔、非远古时代的被观测前超光速文明；本事件 5400 天内不重复（冷却 1800 天）。
  Conditions: we are not a gestalt and have an observation post; an observed non-gestalt pre-FTL past the ancient eras within our borders; not again within 5400 days (pool cooldown 1800 days).
- 发生后设置“近期前超光速外交事件”冷却，其他此类事件暂不出现。
  Afterwards a "recent pre-FTL diplomatic event" cooldown blocks the other events of this pool for a while.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 8060「市场波动」：得知宇宙中并非孤独后，他们的金融体系崩跌。
  8060 "Market Fluctuations": learning they are not alone sends their financial system into free fall.
- “救助他们”→ 720–820 天后 8065「市场已稳定」；“相信市场”→ 720–820 天后 25% 8070「市场知道什么最好」/ 75% 8075「世纪大崩溃」。
  "Bail them out" → 720-820 days later 8065 "Markets Stabilized"; "trust the market" → 720-820 days later 25% 8070 "Market Knows Best" / 75% 8075 "Once in a Generation Collapse".
