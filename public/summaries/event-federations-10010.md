# 自我升华 / Greater Than Ourselves

- 分类 Category：文明纪事 Society (C7)

## 如何开始 How it starts

- 决议“超越自我”（resolution_greatergood_greater_than_ourselves）通过：effect 立即向违规者发出 10210，并在15天后向所有成员发出 10010。
  When the resolution "Greater Than Ourselves" passes its effect fires 10210 at once at members in breach and 10010 at all members 15 days later.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

- 10010：告知现在可鼓励失业工人自愿迁居其他宜居世界；选项仅确认。
  10010: announces that the unemployed can now be encouraged to resettle voluntarily; the option only acknowledges.
- 10210：禁止分层经济生活水准与牲畜/奴隶/电网融合奴役；选项：令奴隶充军 / 为仆役 / 遵守 / 保留 / 拒不服从（排外威权者的版本带团结奖励）。
  10210: bans Stratified Economy and chattel / livestock / grid-amalgamation slavery; options: make the slaves fight / serve, comply, retain, refuse (xenophobic authoritarian variants add unity rewards).

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 两个窗口面向不同对象，并行。
  The two windows address different empires and are parallel.

## 条件与文明差异 Conditions and variants

- 选项取决于是否军国主义、奴役类型与生活水准。
  Options depend on militarism, slavery type and living standard.
