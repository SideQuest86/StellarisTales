## 如何开始 How it starts

Hidden origin.100 marks a neighbouring planet and its parent empire as the lost colony's parent (flags such as lost_colony_planet, lost_colony_parent_of).

The greeting variants for meeting the parent empire live in generic first-contact events action.1 / action.2 (about twenty variants), first_contact.102, bio.215 and namarian.10; they are not part of this story.
