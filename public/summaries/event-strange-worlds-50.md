# 自然触电 / Nature Shock

- 分类 Category：C8 银河轶事 / C8 Galactic miscellany · DLC: BioGenesis

## 如何开始 How it starts

殖民地满1年时随机事件池以20/510抽中隐藏门卫 strange_worlds.50（common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3730）；非AI与默认国家类型限制见前提，门卫在其后每180天（随机至3420天）检查一次殖民地是否有能源区划或能源网络/枢纽建筑。

A colony turning 1 year old can draw the hidden gatekeeper strange_worlds.50 (20/510, common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:3730); hidden strange_worlds.51 then re-checks every 180 days (random up to 3420) until the colony has an energy district or energy grid / nexus building.

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

strange_worlds.55“自然触电”（巨型草食动物撞上能源设施自杀；非有机/岩石或机器人物种看到另一版正文）→ 270天后 strange_worlds.60“催化生理”（社会研究、合金）→ 180天后 strange_worlds.65“动物磁吸”：a“收集它们的粪便”给星球 pm_catalytic_excrement 修正；b“盛宴！”（吞噬蜂群专属，或普通版）给 pm_frying_fauna_2，演化捕食者另得DNA进度。

strange_worlds.55 "Nature Shock" (megafauna suicide on the power stations; lithoid or robotic species see another text) -> 270 days later strange_worlds.60 "Catalytic Physiology" (society research, alloys) -> 180 days later strange_worlds.65 "Animal Magnetism": a "Harvest their droppings" gives the planet pm_catalytic_excrement; b "Time to feast!" (Devouring Swarm version or default) gives pm_frying_fauna_2, and Evolutionary Predators also DNA progress.

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

65 的 a 与 b 互斥（吞噬蜂群无法选 a）。

In 65, a and b are exclusive (Devouring Swarm cannot pick a).
