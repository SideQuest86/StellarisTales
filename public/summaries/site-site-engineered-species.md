## 如何开始 How it starts

随机遗址：勘察时按权重抽取，权重+25，出现在可居住星球上；全银河最多一处（common/archaeological_site_types/00_base_game_arc_sites.txt:429）。

A random site: drawn by weight on survey, +25 on habitable planets, at most one per galaxy (common/archaeological_site_types/00_base_game_arc_sites.txt:429).

## 剧情脉络 Story outline

大量保存完好的化石暗示这些物种是被基因改造的；前三个阶段每次都要在“研究”与“展览/日常”二选一（30天后各自的后续事件）。选“研究”的次数决定结局：至少两次为“真相”，否则为“无知”。

1. 承骨之重：大量化石；研究化石（+1）或办化石展览
2. 穴居者：洞穴居民：保存历史（研究，+1）或保存日常生活
3. 骨结构：骨质结构：研究建筑（+1）或研究内部
4. 地下设施：外星访客的实验室

Large fossil deposits suggest engineered species. The first three stages each ask you to study or use/display the finds (the follow-up fires 30 days later). The number of "study" choices decides the ending: two or more gives Revelation, fewer gives Ignorance.

1. Loads of Bones: loads of fossils: study them (+1) or stage an exhibition
2. Cave Dwellers: cave dwellers: preserve their history (+1) or their daily life
3. Bone Structure: a bone structure: study the building (+1) or its interior
4. Underground Facility: an underground facility: the alien visitors' lab

## 分支与结局 Branches and outcomes

- 突破 / Breakthrough：远古的太空文明对原住民做基因改造。学习≥2次：真相（社会学研究、“工程物种：真相”修正）；<2次：无知（研究与团结、“无知”修正）；排外者与亲外者有额外回应 / a spacefaring civilisation engineered the natives. Studied >=2: Revelation (society research, modifier engineered_species_revelation); <2: Ignorance (research and unity, modifier engineered_species_ignorance); xenophobes and xenophiles get extra answers
